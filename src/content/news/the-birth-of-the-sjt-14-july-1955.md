<p>The Stephen Joseph Theatre was founded on 14 July 1955 when the Library Theatre made its debut in Scarborough.</p>

<p>To mark the occasion, I’ve launched the latest of my videos – <a href="https://youtu.be/Zk4_VTcyWU4">The Birth of the SJT: 14 July 1955</a> – which asks what it would have been like to attend that opening night of the Library Theatre.</p>

<p>From descriptions of the experience and theatre to reactions to the play, the video offers a fascinating insight into the experience of seeing the debut of the UK’s first professional theatre-in-the-round company.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/Zk4_VTcyWU4">The Birth of the SJT: 14 July 1955</a> can be found on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Official Ayckbourn YouTube</a> channel by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/Zk4_VTcyWU4">here</a> – and please don’t forget to subscribe if you’ve enjoyed the video.</p>
