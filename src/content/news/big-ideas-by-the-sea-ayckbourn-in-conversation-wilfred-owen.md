<p>Scarborough’s newest festival, <a href="https://bigideasbythesea.com/festival/"><em>Big Ideas by the Sea</em></a>, launches today and an Ayckbourn premiere is part of it.</p>

<p><em>Big Ideas by the Sea</em> aims to be the North’s leading environmental and creative festival by 2025. This not-for-profit annual festival will bring leading speakers, thinkers, and creative people from science, the arts, business, technology and the environment to debate the Big Ideas that concern all of us.</p>

</div>

<p>As part of its opening weekend, there will be the premiere of a new filmed interview on Saturday 17 July with Alan Ayckbourn, who has made Scarborough his home since the late 1960s and who is closely associated with the town’s Stephen Joseph Theatre. </p>

<p><em><a href="https://bigideasbythesea.com/festival/index.php/b11/">Sir Alan Ayckbourn In Conversation</a></em> is an hour-long interview conducted by Dr Paul Elsam at the playwright’s home and sees the playwright cover a variety of issues from his most recent play, <em>The Girl Next Door</em>, back through his long career as writer and director as well as touching on what post-pandemic theatre might look like.</p>

<p>The film will be followed by <em>Theatre Futures: The Creative Producer’s Perspective</em> in which the Stephen Joseph Theatre’s Creative Producer, Amy Fisher, will have a discussion and Q&amp;A on the likely shape of theatre in a changed world.</p>

<p><em>Sir Alan Ayckbourn In Conversation</em> will be premiered at Scarborough’s Old Parcels Office at Scarborough railway station (at the far end of the car park) on Saturday 17 July 2021 at 7.30pm. Tickets are priced at £15 (plus booking fee).</p>
