<p>Last week, the blog took a more in-depth look at the central display of the forthcoming <em>The Library Theatre at 70</em> exhibition at Scarborough from 10 – 12 July (click <strong><a href="https://archivingayckbourn.home.blog/2025/06/12/the-library-theatre-at-70-in-depth-pt-1/">here</a></strong> to read).</p>

<p>Here we delve into what else can be found in the exhibition which celebrates the 70th anniversary of the founding of the UK’s first professional theatre-in-the-round and the impact of the Library Theatre between 1955 and 1976.</p>

<p>The exhibition has been organised and curated by Simon Murgatroyd, Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist and theatre historian, in conjunction with North Yorkshire Council.</p>

<p><strong>The Lecture Room</strong></p>

<p>The lecture room on the first floor of the library – next to the foyer where the photographic exhibition is being displayed – was, historically, the dressing room, wardrobe and administration area of the Library Theatre. For the exhibition, it will be home to several displays.</p>

<p><strong>The Stephen Joseph Exhibition:</strong> The exhibition is fortunate enough to be displaying a number of framed examples of the original display boards used by Stephen Joseph for his theatre-in-the-round exhibition, which ran at the Library Theatre from 1955 to 1965.</p>

<p>The original exhibition – located in the then Large Lecture Room – told the story of theatre-in-the-round globally, later incorporating material relating to previous Scarborough seasons. For this exhibition, there are examples of  the mounted pieces relating to the inaugural season as well as pieces from the historical display – all original and dating back to the mid 1950s.</p>

<p>It even includes two of the boards from the 1956 season exhibition which highlighted productions from the company’s inaugural year in 1955.</p>

<p><strong>The Video Presentation:</strong> Simon Murgatroyd has created an hour-long video specifically for the exhibition divided into three parts (starting every 20 minutes and repeating on the hour).</p>

<p>The first part offers a very rare glimpse of archival footage of the Library Theatre from its final year in 1976 including rehearsal and performance footage as well as brief contemporary interviews with Alan Ayckbourn and Ken Boden. This is believed to be the only existing footage relating to the Library Theatre.</p>

<p>It is accompanied by a modern interview with Alan Ayckbourn in which he talks about his memories of working at the Library Theatre from 1957 to 1976; the full transcribed interview can  be found in the accompanying book <em><strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-187/page-144/page-176/">A Theatrical Revolution</a></strong></em>.</p>

<p>Finally, Simon hosts a look at what it would have been like to visit the Library Theatre on its opening night of 14 July 1955. The three video segments are interspaced with photos from every production from 1955 to 1976, where they have survived, and a pictorial guide to the Library Theatre.</p>

<p><strong>The Stephen Joseph Touring Exhibition:</strong> Earlier this year, Simon Murgatroyd took possession of the surviving pieces from Stephen Joseph’s portable mounted display which he would take to the many talks he gave on theatres and theatre-in-the-round. A number of original pieces from this will also be on display.</p>

<p><strong>Programmes and Posters:</strong> Scarborough Library holds the most complete collection in archive of programmes relating to the Stephen Joseph Theatre from 1955 to 1995 and these will be on display and available to view during the exhibition alongside information about the Library Theatre during its first year.</p>

<p>A number of original mounted posters from Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre will also be on display including a fascinating poster from Germany where the Library Theatre company made its first international tour in 1976.</p>

<p><strong>The Scarborough Room</strong></p>

<p>Finally, the Scarborough Room on the ground floor will be holding a month-long, small display during July of items held in its archive relating to the Library Theatre and its founding. Many of these pieces – including several items donated by Simon Murgatroyd and the Ayckbourn Archive – have never been on public display before and will offer a fascinating insight the history of the UK’s first professional theatre-in-the-round.</p>

<p><em>The Library Theatre at 70 – Celebrating Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre (1955 – 1976)</em> can be seen on the first floor of Scarborough Library on Thursday 10 and Friday 11 July from 10am – 5pm and on Saturday 12 July from 10am to 2pm. Entry is free.</p>

<p>Simon will also be talking about the significance of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre in the Concert Room on Thursday 10 July at 6pm and Saturday 12 July at 11am. Admission is free and no booking required.</p>
