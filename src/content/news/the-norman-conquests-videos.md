<p><em>In 2024, Alan Ayckbourn’s classic trilogy The Norman Conquests is celebrating the 50th anniversary of its West End premiere.</em></p>

<p><em>The blog is celebrating the anniversary with several articles looking at the history of the play and its West End success.</em></p>

<p><strong>The Norman Conquests: Videos</strong></p>

<p>If you’re looking to find out a bit more about the history of <em><strong><a href="/plays/the-norman-conquests">The Norman Conquests</a></strong></em>, then look no further as Alan Ayckbourn’s website <strong><a href="/">www.alanayckbourn.net</a></strong> has produced several videos in which the playwright’s archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, looks at the history of this acclaimed trilogy.</p>
