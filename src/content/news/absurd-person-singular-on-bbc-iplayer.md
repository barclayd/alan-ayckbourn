<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s classic tragicomedy <em>Absurd Person Singular</em> is now available to view on BBC iPlayer for the first time.</p>

<p>The acclaimed adaptation of the play, first screened in 1985, is available in a remastered version alongside a string of classic BBC dramas celebrating post-war British and Irish theatre.</p>

</div>

<p><em>Absurd Person Singular</em> stars Cheryl Campbell, Michael Gambon, Nicky Henson, Maureen Lipman, Geoffrey Pamer and Prunella Scales and was directed by Michael Simpson. It is regarded, alongside the BBC adaptations of Season’s Greetings and Absent Friends, as one of the best television adaptations of the playwright’s work.</p>

<p>The play is available on BBC iPlayer for free for the next year and is available alongside <em>A Taste of Honey</em>, <em>An Inspector Calls</em>, <em>The Birthday Party</em>, <em>Black Watch</em>, <em>The Cheviot, The Stag and the Black</em>, <em>Black Oil</em>, <em>Copenhagen</em>, <em>Cyprus Avenue</em>, <em>The Deep Blue Sea</em>, <em>Elmina’s Kitchen</em>, <em>The Entertainer</em>, <em>My Night with Reg</em>, <em>Not I</em>, <em>Salt</em>, <em>Top Girls</em> and <em>Under Milk Wood</em>.</p>

<p>To view <em>Absurd Person Singular</em>, click <strong><a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m002ykss">here</a></strong> or it can be found via the BBC iPlayer app.</p>
