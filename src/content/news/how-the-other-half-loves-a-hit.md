<p>The first reviews are in for The Old Vic’s revival of Alan Ayckbourn’s <em>How The Other Half Loves</em>.</p>

<p>With a swathe of four star reviews, it’s the best reviewed Ayckbourn revival in the West End since – appropriately – The Old Vic’s revival of <em>The Norman Conquests</em> in 2008.</p>

<p>Judging by the reviews, this production is one not to miss. It is directed by Phillip Breen and stars Roger Allam, Dorothy Atkinson, Ayesha Antoine, Laura Elsworthy, Adam Gillen and Ron Polonski.</p>

<p>Here’s a quick round-up of reviews…</p>

<p>**** Financial Times: “A masterclass in comic timing.”</p>

<p>**** The Times: “Extraordinarily clever.”</p>

<p>**** The Guardian: “The cast of six, though, is more than half the joy, their verbal and physical humour stopping the show as surely as big numbers in musicals.”</p>

<p>**** Financial Times: “How The Other Half Loves still resonates.”</p>

<p>**** The Telegraph: “This genius writer delivers his comedy with a sting in the tail.”</p>

<p>**** The Independent: “There’s so much ingenious craftsmanship in Ayckbourn’s play.”</p>

<p>**** What’s On Stage: “A laugh-out-loud and multi-layered production.”</p>

<p>**** Broadway World: “Philip Breen’s direction ticks like clockwork.”</p>

<p>**** West End Theatre: “A masterfully crafted, brilliantly performed revival of a structurally ingenious farce.”</p>

<p>**** London Theatre: “If laughter is the best medicine, how wonderful that Old Vic audiences are now getting a welcome dose.”</p>

<p><em>How The Other Half Loves</em> can be seen at The Old Vic until 19 September and further details and bookings can be found <strong><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/">here</a></strong>.</p>
