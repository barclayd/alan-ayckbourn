<p>Alan Ayckbourn talks about his new play <em>The Girl Next Door</em> in the latest <a href="https://youtu.be/FFTrO0prJbc">NewsRound video</a> on the official <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube channel</a>.</p>

<p>The playwright talked to his Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, in April about the play and <a href="https://youtu.be/FFTrO0prJbc">NewsRound</a> has some of this exclusive interview to listen to.</p>

<p>Simon also presents details about the recently announced cast for the production as well as looking at two significant milestones the play will mark. Plus there’s details about when the play is running and how to book.</p>

<p>NewRound can be found on The <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Official Alan Ayckbourn Website YouTube</a> Channel by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/FFTrO0prJbc">here</a>.</p>

<p><em>The Girl Next Door</em> is running at the Stephen Joseph Theatre from 4 June to 3 July, 2021.</p>

<p>Further details about the play can be found at <a href="/">Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website</a> by clicking <a href="http://thegirlnextdoor.alanayckbourn.net">here</a>.</p>
