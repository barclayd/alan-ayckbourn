<p><em>The Library Theatre at 70</em> exhibition is coming to Scarborough Library from 10 – 12 July 2025 to celebrate the library’s connection with <strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-4/">Stephen Joseph</a></strong> and the founding of the UK’s first theatre-in-the-round company in 1955.</p>

<p>Although it’s still a couple of months from the exhibition opening, its curator – theatre historian Simon Murgatroyd – has been working on it for more than a year.</p>

<p>So to whet your appetite, here’s a brief behind-the-scenes look at what’s coming to the library in July, where Simon will be looking at the story of <strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-187/page-14/">Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre</a></strong> from 1955 to 1976 and why Scarborough Library plays such a significant role in British theatre history during this period.</p>

</div>

<p>The exhibition is centred on 70 images from 1955 to 1976, here several are being prepared for display in July. Many of the images have not been displayed in public before.</p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2025/7-c2a9-simon-murgatroyd-w819.jpg" alt="" width="819" height="1023" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>

<p>The exhibition will include a special hour-long video presentation which will include both archival footage of the Library Theatre as well as an exclusive interview with Sir Alan Ayckbourn about his memories of the Library Theatre.</p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2025/5-c2a9-simon-murgatroyd-w819.jpg" alt="" width="819" height="1023" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>

<p>Simon Murgatroyd has not only curated the exhibition but also written an accompanying book to celebrate the inaugural year of the exhibition called <strong><em><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-187/page-144/page-176/">A Theatrical Revolution</a></em></strong>. Limited to just 70 signed and numbered copies, the final copies arrived last week – an exciting day!</p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2025/4-c2a9-simon-murgatroyd-w817.jpg" alt="" width="817" height="1024" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>

<p>The exhibition will also include a section dedicated to original material used in exhibitions by Stephen Joseph – both from his large scale theatre-in-the-round exhibition displayed in Scarborough library during the 1950s and a smaller touring exhibition he used for talks.</p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2025/2-c2a9-simon-murgatroyd-w816.jpg" alt="" width="816" height="1023" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>

<p>Preparing signage and information sheets including a newly created floor-plan showing how the Library Theatre was laid out in 1955.</p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2025/3-c2a9-simon-murgatroyd-w819.jpg" alt="" width="819" height="1023" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>

<p>The exhibition will also include a number of original mounted posters from the Library Theatre including the poster for the world premiere of <em><strong><a href="/plays/relatively-speaking">Relatively Speaking</a></strong></em> by Alan Ayckbourn.</p>

<p><em>The Library Theatre at 70 – Celebrating Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre (1955 – 1976)</em> can be seen on the first floor of Scarborough Library on Thursday 10 and Friday 11 July from 10am – 5pm and on Saturday 12 July from 10am to 2pm. Entry is free.</p>

<p>Further details can be found on the A Round Town website <a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-187/page-144/"><strong>here</strong></a> and any enquiries should be directed to Simon at <strong><a href="mailto:admin@alanayckbourn.net">admin@alanayckbourn.net</a></strong>.</p>

<p>The blog will be carrying more details of the exhibition in the weeks ahead.</p>

<p><em>The Library Theatre at 70 </em>exhibition is organised by Simon Murgatroyd in association with North Yorkshire Council. It is supported by <strong><a href="/">Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website</a></strong> and the <strong><a href="http://www.a-round-town.com">A Round Town</a></strong> website.</p>
