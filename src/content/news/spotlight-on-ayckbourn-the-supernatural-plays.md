<p>It’s Halloween and the spirits are out and haunting the latest <a href="https://youtu.be/Bg5zO2uTr6U">SpotLight on Ayckbourn</a> video.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, latest <a href="https://youtu.be/Bg5zO2uTr6U">SpotLight</a> video takes a peek at the supernatural plays of Alan Ayckbourn and how ghosts and ghouls crop up surprisingly often in his plays.</p>

<p>From well-known works such as <em>Haunting Julia</em> and <em>Snake in the Grass</em> to obscure pieces such as <em>The Fearsome Threesome</em> and <em>The Ghost of ‘Enry Albert Murgatroyd</em>, Simon lifts the coffin lid on the spookiest Ayckbourn works.</p>

<p>It also features an exclusive extract from Alan Ayckbourn’s 2020 recording of <em>Haunting Julia</em>. The video can be found on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/Bg5zO2uTr6U">here</a>. Please subscribe to the channel if you enjoy the video.</p>
