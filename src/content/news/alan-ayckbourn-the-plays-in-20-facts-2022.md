<p>The essential guide to Alan Ayckbourn’s plays has been updated for 2022 with the latest video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg/featured">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p><a href="https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=5Vq_3BZNDJ0&amp;feature=youtu.be">Alan Ayckbourn: The Plays in 20 Facts</a> (2022 Edition) has been completely updated for 2022 and features 20 slides with fascinating facts and figures about Alan Ayckbourn’s plays.</p>

<p>It has been researched and narrated by the playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, and includes details from his writing from 1959 to the present day.</p>
