<p>A Scarborough theatre historian has been helping to fill gaps in the town’s theatre in the round archive collections.</p>

<p>The UK’s first professional theatre in the round company was founded in Scarborough in 1955 by <strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-4/">Stephen Joseph</a></strong> – whose protégé <strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-107/">Alan Ayckbourn</a></strong> is now most associated with the company. It began life on the first floor of Scarborough Library as <strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-187/page-14/">Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre</a></strong> and, 69 years later, is known as the Stephen Joseph Theatre now in its third home in a former converted Odeon cinema.</p>

<p>The town has two significant archives relating to the history of theatre in the round – the Stephen Joseph Collection at Scarborough Library and The Bob Watson Archive at the SJT. </p>

<p>Both have impressive collections of programmes relating to the history of, respectively , Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre from 1955 – 1976 and the overall history of the Stephen Joseph Theatre from 1955 to the present – but there are gaps in both collections.</p>

<p>Theatre historian and Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., has been working with both Scarborough Library and the SJT. Identifying relevant duplicates from each collection, programmes have been swapped to fill some of the archival gaps. For the library, this was material pertaining from 1973 to 1976 and for the SJT, programmes relating to the company’s early history during the 1950s and 1960s. </p>

<p>“I’m delighted to have been able to work with and help these archives,” said Simon. “Both archives are rich in material chronicling the history of theatre in the round in the Scarborough and its significance to 20th century British theatre. It’s so important to make sure that history is preserved and kept accessible for future generations through archives such as these.”</p>

<p>With regard to Scarborough Library’s collection, Simon was also able to donate some of his own duplicate Ayckbourn programmes to fill in further gaps. The Stephen Joseph Collection at Scarborough Library now holds, he believes, the most complete collection of programmes relating to the first 20 years of Stephen Joseph’s company – whilst it was based at the library – in the UK.</p>

<p>Amongst his archival work, Simon curates the website <strong><a href="http://www.a-round-town.com">www.a-round-town.com</a></strong>, which offers an insight into the history of theatre in the round in the town particular focusing on its first two homes at Scarborough Library and, later, the former Westwood County Modern School.</p>

<p>He is also working closely with Scarborough Library to mark the 70th anniversary of the founding of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre during 2025.</p>
