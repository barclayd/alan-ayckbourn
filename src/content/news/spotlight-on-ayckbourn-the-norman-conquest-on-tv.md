<p><em>The Norman Conquests</em> is one of Alan Ayckbourn’s most successful and famous works and the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/c/AlanAyckbournPlaywright/playlists">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> has now finished its own trilogy about the famed trilogy.</p>

<p><em><a href="https://youtu.be/rSZG6cPxo34">SpotLight on Ayckbourn: The Norman Conquests on TV</a></em>  goes behind the scenes of the adaptation of the trilogy for television in 1977 and just how the plays transferred from stage to screen.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., delves into the Ayckbourn Archive to tell the story of how the BBC and Thames Television fought for the rights to produce the plays, about how the playwright fought for them to remain true to the original scripts and even how producers wanted a spin-off television series!</p>

<p>Researched, written and presented by Simon Murgatroyd with exclusive material drawn from the Ayckbourn Archive, this SpotLight offers a look at a previously unexplored area of <em>The Norman Conquests</em>‘ phenomenal success.</p>

<p><em><a href="https://youtu.be/rSZG6cPxo34">SpotLight on Ayckbourn: The Norman Conquests on TV</a></em> can be found on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/c/AlanAyckbournPlaywright/playlists">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/rSZG6cPxo34">here</a>.</p>
