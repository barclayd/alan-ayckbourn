<p>Scarborough Art Gallery is now selling several Alan Ayckbourn related publications to tie in with its current display of The Ayckbourn Collection.</p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2024/2a-w819.jpg" alt="" width="819" height="1023" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption><sub>The Shop at Scarborough Art Gallery (© Simon Murgatroyd)</sub></figcaption></figure>
</div>

<p>The shop is currently selling hardback editions of Paul Allen’s biography of Alan Ayckbourn, <em>Grinning At The Edge</em>, signed by the author. The biography covers the first six decades of the playwright’s life and many of his most notable achievements in British theatre during the second half of the 20th century.</p>

<p>It is also one of only two shops selling <em><strong><a href="http://www.alanayckbourn.net/styled-13/page28.html">Unseen Ayckbourn</a></strong></em> by Alan Ayckbourn’s archivist, Simon Murgatroyd. <em>Unseen Ayckbourn</em> looks at the unpublished, withdrawn, unwritten and altered works of the playwright from 1955 to 2023 and offers an insight into the playwright’s writing and process. It includes quotes from the playwright himself as well as exclusive extracts and quotes from many of the texts.</p>

<p>The shop is also selling three collected volumes of Alan Ayckbourn’s plays. <em>Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 4</em> contains <em><strong><a href="/plays/the-revengers-comedies">The Revengers’ Comedies</a></strong></em>, <em><strong><a href="/plays/things-we-do-for-love">Things We Do For Love</a></strong></em> and <em><strong><a href="/plays/house-and">House &amp; Garden</a></strong></em>; <em>Things We Do For Love</em> is being revived by the playwright this May at The Old Laundry Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere, and <em>House &amp; Garden</em> celebrates its 25th anniversary this year.</p>

<div>

<p><em>Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 5</em> contains <em><strong><a href="/plays/snake-in-the-grass">Snake in the Grass</a></strong></em>, <em><strong><a href="/plays/if-i-were-you">If I Were You</a></strong></em>, <em><strong><a href="/plays/life-and-beth">Life &amp; Beth</a></strong></em>, <em><strong><a href="/plays/my-wonderful-day">My Wonderful Day</a></strong></em> and <em><strong><a href="/plays/life-of-riley">Life of Riley</a></strong></em>, whilst the <em>Damsels in Distress</em> volume contains <em><strong><a href="/plays/gameplan">GamePlan</a></strong></em>, <em><strong><a href="/plays/flatspin">FlatSpin</a></strong></em> and <em><strong><a href="http://roleplay.alanayckbourn.net">RolePlay</a></strong></em>.</p>

<p>Scarborough Art Gallery is currently exhibiting items from the Ayckbourn Collection, which were donated to Scarborough Museums and Galleries by the playwright and his archivist last year.</p>

<p>Items on display include a copy of the playwright’s first play – <em><strong><a href="/plays/the-square-cat">The Square Cat</a></strong></em>, an original manuscript from <em><strong><a href="/plays/the-norman-conquests">The Norman Conquests</a></strong></em>, manuscript, a portrait of the playwright by John Bratby, designs and drawings by <strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-4/">Stephen Joseph</a></strong> – Alan’s mentor and founder of theatre in the round in Scarborough as well as other items.</p>

<p>Scarborough Art Gallery and the Shop are open Tuesdays to Sundays, 10am to 5pm. Admission to the gallery is £3 for an annual pass with entry to both the gallery and the Rotunda Museum. Further details can be found <strong><a href="https://scarboroughmuseumsandgalleries.org.uk/whats-on/event/the-ayckbourn-collection/">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p>Simon Murgatroyd hopes to be giving several guided talks of The Ayckbourn Collection in the coming months, watch this space for details.</p>
