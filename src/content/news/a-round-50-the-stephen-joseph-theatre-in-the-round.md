<p>There are still tickets available for Simon Murgatroyd’s talk celebrating the 50th anniversary of the opening of the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round on Saturday 20 June as part of Scarborough Fringe.</p>

<p>2026 marks the 50th anniversary of the opening of the second home of the theatre in the round in Scarborough – between 1976 and 1996 – after it left The Library Theatre in September 1976.  </p>

<p>Simon, a theatre historian and Alan Ayckbourn’s archivist, will be looking at why and how the theatre moved to the former Westwood County Modern School, what the move meant for the company as well as celebrating its achievements and how Alan Ayckbourn particularly helped make it into one of the most significant regional theatres in the UK during the ’80s and ’90s renowned for its commitment to encouraging new writing.</p>

</div>

<p>Originally known as Theatre in the Round at Westwood and only intended to be a temporary home for the company, the theatre opened on 26 October 1976. Over the next 20 years, it had a huge cultural impact on the town and was recognised nationally and internationally for its work. It not only witnessed the world premiere of some of Ayckbourn’s most successful works but also the debuts of playwrights such as Tim Firth and shows such as <em>The Woman in Black</em>. </p>

<p>The talk, <em><strong><a href="https://scarboroughfair.uk/events/a-round-50-theatre-in-the-round-at-westwood/">A Round 50: Theatre in the Round at Westwood</a></strong></em>, will be taking place on Saturday 20 June in the Sitwell Library at Woodend Gallery and Studios, Scarborough. Tickets are priced at £5 and can be booked <strong><a href="https://scarboroughfair.uk/events/a-round-50-theatre-in-the-round-at-westwood/">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p>Simon is also celebrating the 70th anniversary of the founding of theatre in the round in Scarborough in 1955 with his exhibition, <em>The Library Theatre at 70</em>, which is on display at Scarborough Library until Saturday 27 June. The free display of 90 photographs is open during normal library opening hours with further information <strong><a href="https://www.theatre-in-the-round.com/page-41/page-118/">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p>An open edition of Simon’s book exploring the founding year of theatre in the round in Scarborough, <em>A Theatrical Revolution</em>, has also just been published – following a sell-out limited edition in 2025 with further details available <strong><a href="https://www.theatre-in-the-round.com/page-41/page-118/page-119/">here</a></strong>. They will be available to buy at the talk and Simon will also be signing copies.</p>

<p>To find out about what Alan Ayckbourn considers his favourite of the three homes of Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round and why it is so culturally significant, don’t miss there <em>A Round 50</em> <a href="https://scarboroughfair.uk/events/a-round-50-theatre-in-the-round-at-westwood/"><strong>talk</strong></a>.</p>

<p>Further details about the history of theatre in the round in Scarborough including the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round can be found at <strong><a href="https://www.a-round-town.com">www.a-round-town.com</a></strong>.</p>
