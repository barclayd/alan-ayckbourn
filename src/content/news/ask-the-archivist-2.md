<p>There’s a new <a href="https://youtu.be/Da0ljxClQiI">Ask The Archivist</a> on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> looking at Alan Ayckbourn’s famed trilogy <em>The Norman Conquests</em>.</p>

<p>The trilogy celebrates its 50th anniversary in 2023 and the question asked is, when did <em>The Norman Conquests</em> become <em>The Norman Conquests</em>?</p>

<p>Not as odd a question as its might appear as when the trilogy premiered in Scarborough in 1973, it was not known as <em>The Norman Conquests</em>, that only came later.</p>

<p>The playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, is on hand to answer this question as well as what the correct order to see the trilogy is in the video.</p>
