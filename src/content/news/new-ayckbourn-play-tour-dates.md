<p>Details of the short tour of Alan Ayckbourn’s new play <em>The Trial of Romeo Oscar</em> have been announced.</p>

<p>The playwright’s 92nd full-length play will be touring to <strong><a href="https://victoriawoodtheatre.com/whats-on/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar">The Victoria Wood Theatre</a></strong>, Bowness-on-Windermere, from 13 – 17 October and <strong><a href="https://www.newvictheatre.org.uk/productions/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar/">The New Vic</a></strong>, Newcastle-under-Lyme, from 21 – 31 October. It will be directed by the playwright.</p>

<p>In space, no-one can hear you dream… In the year 2194, humanity is scattered around the solar system and day-to-day life on earth looks very different. Preparations are underway for a landmark legal case.</p>

</div>

<p>Romeo Oscar, a military issue android, is on trial for murder, the first non-human to be tried in a court of law. The circumstances surrounding the incident are unclear, the evidence is polarising, and selecting an impartial jury is proving a challenge.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s latest play is also his first courtroom drama exploring a question that artists have been asking for centuries – what makes us human?</p>

<p><em>The Trail of Romeo Oscar</em> will premiere play at the <strong><a href="https://sjt.uk.com/events/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar">Stephen Joseph Theatre</a></strong>, Scarborough, and run from 4 September – 3 October. </p>
