<p>The journey of Alan Ayckbourn’s famed trilogy, <em>The Norman Conquests</em>, from Scarborough to the West End is the subject of the latest <a href="https://youtu.be/9t4rzw_GJ30">SpotLight on Ayckbourn</a> video.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/9t4rzw_GJ30"><em>The Norman Conquests</em> in the West End</a> follows the convoluted journey of the trilogy from Scarborough to Greenwich to the West End and the incidents which befell the trilogy on the way.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, tells how the plays were never guaranteed a West End transfer and how producers were initially reluctant to taken them on until a lucky encounter.</p>

<p>With photographs and material drawn from the Ayckbourn Archive, Simon tells the story of how what is now regarded as one of Alan Ayckbourn’s most successful and popular works achieved its phenomenal West End triumph.</p>
