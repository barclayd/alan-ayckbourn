<p>One of Alan Ayckbourn’s most acclaimed and popular works kicks off the first <a href="https://youtu.be/JarOT_EqmI8">SpotLight on Ayckbourn</a> videos of 2022.</p>

<p><em>The Norman Conquests</em> is the subject of the new video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/c/AlanAyckbournPlaywright">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> looking behind the scenes at the creation of and background to the acclaimed trilogy.</p>

<div><figure><figcaption><sub>Christopher Godwin &amp; Janet Dale in the world premiere of The Norman Conquests (© Scarborough Theatre Trust)</sub></figcaption></figure></div>

<p>For the first time in a SpotLight video, there’s contemporary interview recordings with Alan Ayckbourn from the 1970s in which the playwright talks about how he wrote the play and what inspired him.</p>

<p>The video looks at how the seeds of the trilogy were sewn at the Sheffield Crucible and how Alan went about writing such a complex and challenging work with three interconnected plays telling the story of one weekend from three different perspectives.</p>

<p>There’s also exclusive photos and material drawn from the Ayckbourn Archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives, some of which has never been publicly seen before including letters from Alan’s legendary agent, Margaret ‘Peggy’ Ramsay.</p>

<p><em><a href="https://youtu.be/JarOT_EqmI8">SpotLight on Ayckbourn: The Norman Conquests</a></em> can be found <a href="https://youtu.be/JarOT_EqmI8">here</a> on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/c/AlanAyckbournPlaywright">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> and please don’t forget to subscribe if you enjoy it.</p>

<p>And, finally, a word of thanks to all the supporters of this and the YouTube sites, this weekend saw the channel hit 100 subscribers and get its official URL: <a href="https://www.youtube.com/c/AlanAyckbournPlaywright">www.youtube.com/c/AlanAyckbournPlaywright</a></p>
