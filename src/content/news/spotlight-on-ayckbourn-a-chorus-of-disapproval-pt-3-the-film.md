<p>In 1989, Alan Ayckbourn’s popular play <em>A Chorus of Disapproval</em> became his first work to be adapted for the big screen.</p>

<p>It was, to not put too fine a point on it, a disaster. Directed by Michael Winner, the decision to allow the play to be made into a film because one hugely regretted by the playwright.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/5ms6NjQ6m0w">SpotLight on Ayckbourn</a> concludes its three part look at <em>A Chorus of Disapproval</em> with a <a href="https://youtu.be/5ms6NjQ6m0w">video</a> exploring the making of the movie, looking into why Alan agreed to it, the disagreements between Winner and the playwright as well as the response and aftermath of the film.</p>

<p>Drawing extensively on correspondence held in the Ayckbourn Archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives, this offers a never-before-seen glimpse into what really happened with the first Ayckbourn film adaptation.</p>

<p><em>SpotLight on Ayckbourn</em> is researched, written and presented by the playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A. with royalty-free music provided by BenSound.</p>
