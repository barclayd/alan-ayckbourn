<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s classic play <em>How The Other Half Loves</em> has been announced for this summer at The Old Vic.</p>

<p>The production has been announced as part of Artistic Director Matthew Warchus’s final season at The Old Vic and will see Phillip Breen (<em>Anna Karenina</em>, <em>Red or Dead</em>) directing the play starring Roger Allam (<em>Endeavour</em>, <em>The Thick of It</em>) and Dorothy Atkinson (<em>Ludwig</em>, <em>Mum</em>).</p>

<p>Notably the production will be staged in-the-round, marking the first time <em><strong><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/">How The Other Half Loves</a></strong></em> has been produced in the West End in the staging it was written and originally produced for.</p>

<p>When Fiona Foster and Bob Phillips are each accused of having an affair by their spouses, they use the innocent and unsuspecting Featherstones as an alibi, dragging them into a web of chaos and confusion. As the lives of the three couples intertwine, the lies deepen and mayhem ensues in one of theatre’s most entertaining comedies. </p>

<p>The play that cemented Alan Ayckbourn as one of Britain’s greatest modern playwrights, <em>How the Other Half Loves</em> is a raucous take on sex, class and marital misunderstanding.  Further details and full casting will be announced soon.</p>

<p>Matthew Warchus, Artistic Director of The Old Vic said: “I grew up in North Yorkshire with Alan Ayckbourn’s theatre on my doorstep and he has long been one of my favourite writers.</p>

<p>“The Old Vic’s current In The Round configuration was in fact originally created for my production of <em>The Norman Conquests</em> 18 years ago so I’m delighted that one of his most celebrated plays will feature in my final season. Phillip Breen is a director well known for delivering comedic excellence and I’m delighted to welcome him to The Old Vic.”</p>

<p><em>How The Other Half Loves</em> is one of Alan Ayckbourn’s enduring plays having premiered in 1969 before transferring for a two year West Run in 1970. This will be its fourth major London production and in the past six decades it has gone onto national and international success.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn is one of the most significant playwrights of the late 20th century and is the only playwright to have received both the Olivier and Tony Special Awards. He has won countless awards, his work has been translated into more than 30 languages and is performed around the world. His plays have been adapted for film, television, radio and streaming and he is considered one of the UK’s most popular playwrights. Still writing and directing at the age of 87, his new play <em>The Trial of Romeo Oscar</em> will open in Scarborough in September.</p>

<p>The Old Vic previously staged the award-winning and highly acclaimed revival of Alan Ayckbourn’s <em>The Norman Conquests</em> in-the-round in 2008, directed by Matthew Warchus. This seminal production then transferred to Broadway with its original cast. It is considered one of the defining Ayckbourn productions of the ’00s and an almost definitive revival of the trilogy.</p>

<p><em>How The Other Half Loves</em> is at The Old Vic from 29 July – 19 September and tickets are on sale now. For further details and bookings, visit The Old Vic website <strong><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/">here</a></strong>.</p>
