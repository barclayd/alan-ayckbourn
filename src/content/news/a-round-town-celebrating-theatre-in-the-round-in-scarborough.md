<p>I’m delighted to announce the news that a new web-site has launched today celebrating the history of theatre in the round in Scarborough.</p>

<p><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">A Round Town</a> is a new website – <a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk</a> – which celebrates the contribution theatre in the round, Stephen Joseph and Alan Ayckbourn have made to the cultural heritage and history of the seaside town of Scarborough in North Yorkshire.</p>

<p>There, in 1955, Stephen Joseph created the UK’s first professional theatre in the round company which was to have a significant impact on the town in the decades to come.</p>

<p><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">A Round Town</a> explores the impact theatre on the round has made on the town as well as the stories behind thew tow most famous people connected to it, Stephen Joseph and Alan Ayckbourn.</p>

<p>Focusing on the period between 1955, when Stephen Joseph created a theatre in the round company in the town, to 2009, when Stephen’s protégé Alan Ayckbourn stepped down as Artistic Director of the company, the website looks at the venues and history of the company, the writers and the significant plays from this period as well as some of the many people involved in the company.</p>

<p>From Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre to the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round to the Stephen Joseph Theatre (SJT) – where Stephen Joseph’s creation continues to thrive to this day, the website is a celebration and in-depth look at a company which is historically significant both in cultural as well as social terms to the town.</p>

<p>The website also includes images and documents drawn from archives offering a glimpse into why theatre in the round is so significant to Scarborough and why Scarborough can lay claim to a theatrical heritage that not only includes the UK’s first professional theatre company, but also one of the longest running new writing companies in the country.</p>

<p>The website has been created by Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., and is a labour of love bringing together more than 20 years of research into the subject. The website will continue to be expanded with new material in the future.</p>

<p>More details about <a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">A Round Town</a> can be found in the video below. <a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">A Round Town</a> is now live and has been launched to coincide with International Archives Day.</p>
