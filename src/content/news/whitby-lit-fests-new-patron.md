<p>Sir Alan Ayckbourn has been revealed as an Honorary Patron of the annual Whitby Lit Fest.</p>

<p>The announcement was made with the launch of the festival’s 2026 programme – following what the organisers described as “a highly successful inaugural year.”</p>

</div>

<p>Sir Alan closed the 2025 Festival with a sold-out talk about his life and career with festival patron Kate Fenton. So pressed was he by the reaction to the talk and the festival itself that he agreed to become its Honorary Patron.</p>

<p>“I’m delighted to become co-patron of this exciting new venture. The festival promises to inject fresh literary energy into Whitby in a way unequalled since the days of Bram Stoker,” said Sir Alan.</p>

<p>The 2026 festival was launched in Whitby last week with a first wave of high profile authors announced amongst them Ann Cleeves OBE, Joanne Harris OBE, Dr Sian Williams and Mark Billingham.</p>

<p>Further authors will be announced in the coming months which will reflect the festival’s commitment to showcasing both established and bestselling authors as well as emerging authors and writers from around the region.</p>

<p>The Chair of Whitby Lit Fest, Lois Kirkland, said the reaction to the inaugural festival had been overwhelming and far beyond expectations setting a high bar for future festivals but work had already begun on an expanded and equally ambitious and diverse programme of events for 2026.</p>

<p>“We were thrilled by the response to our inaugural festival in 2025,” said Lois. “The enthusiasm from audiences, authors, and the local community was extraordinary.”</p>

<p>Whitby Lit Fest will run from 19 – 22 November in venues throughout Whitby. A full programme and tickets will be available during the summer and further details about the festival can be found at <a href="http://www.whitbylitfest.org.uk"><strong>www.whitbylitfest.org.uk</strong></a>.</p>
