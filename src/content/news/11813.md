<p>The Old Vic has announced the full cast for&nbsp;Alan Ayckbourn’s 1969 play&nbsp;<strong><em><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/">How the Other Half Loves</a></em></strong>&nbsp;directed by Phillip Breen&nbsp;(<em>Anna Karenina</em>,&nbsp;<em>Red or Dead</em>), from&nbsp;29 July to 9 September 2026.</p>

<p>Joining the previously announced&nbsp;Roger Allam&nbsp;(<em>Endeavour</em>,&nbsp;<em>The Thick of It</em>) as Frank Foster and&nbsp;Dorothy Atkinson (<em>Ludwig</em>,&nbsp;<em>Mum</em>) as Fiona Foster, is&nbsp;Ayesha Antoine&nbsp;as Teresa Phillips,&nbsp;Laura Elsworthy&nbsp;as Mary Featherstone, Adam Gillen&nbsp;as William Featherstone and&nbsp;Rowan Polonski&nbsp;as Bob Phillips.</p>

<figure><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/"><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2026/htohl-headshots-lan-w1024.jpg" alt="" width="1024" height="315" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a></figure>

<p>Director&nbsp;Phillip Breen&nbsp;said, “I’m so excited about a new generation of audiences experiencing Ayckbourn for the first time, with its big laughs and its deep sadnesses. Alan Ayckbourn is not only one of the English stage’s most brilliant comedians, but one of its great formal pioneers, too.&nbsp;<em>How the Other Half Loves</em>&nbsp;is the first time Ayckbourn plays around with theatre’s two essential building blocks, space and time – and the result is thrilling.”</p>

<p>In Artistic Director Matthew Warchus’ final Season, all productions will take place completely In The Round, forming an intimate seating configuration that surrounds the stage. Written for and originally produced in-the-round in 1969, this will mark the first time <em>How The Other Half Loves</em> has been staged as intended in the West End.</p>

<p>Premiered in Scarborough in 1969 before opening in the West End in 1970 for a two-year run, <em>How The Other Half Loves</em> is considered the play that cemented&nbsp;Alan Ayckbourn&nbsp;as one of Britain’s greatest modern playwrights. The play has been produced around the world during the past six decades and is one of his most produced and popular works. This will mark its fourth West End production since 1970.</p>

<p><em>How The Other Half Loves</em> is running at The Old Vic from 29 July – 19 September and tickets are on sale now. Further details and bookings can be found <strong><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/">here</a></strong>.</p>
