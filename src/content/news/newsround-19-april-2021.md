<p>Today I’m launching the first NewRound video on the Ayckbourn channel on Youtube.</p>

<p>NewsRound will take a look at the latest news regarding Alan Ayckbourn and his plays. The first edition focuses on the re-opening of the UK’s theatres on 17 May and the already announced Ayckbourn productions.</p>

<p>I take a look at the world premiere of <em>The Girl Next Door</em> at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Robin Herford’s revival of <em>Relatively Speaking</em> at the Mill at Sonning and London Classic Theatre’s UK tour of <em>Absurd Person Singular</em>.</p>
