<p>The <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> continues its look into The Bob Watson Archive at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in its latest <a href="https://youtu.be/byQ8CfRR6V8">video</a>.</p>

<p>Following on from the <a href="https://youtu.be/VV_Tr3kn8HA">introduction</a> to the archive, <a href="https://youtu.be/byQ8CfRR6V8">part 2</a> offers a look at the small exhibition within the archive, several key historical manuscripts, some of the photographs held within the largest collection of material relating to Alan Ayckbourn’s career as an actor and a selection of posters since 1959.</p>

<p>The video is narrated and researched by Simon Murgatroyd M.A., Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist and Archivist for The Bob Watson Archive.</p>

<p>The video can be found <a href="https://youtu.be/byQ8CfRR6V8">here</a> and part 1 can be found <a href="https://youtu.be/VV_Tr3kn8HA">here</a> on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>. Don’t forget to subscribe if you enjoyed the video.</p>
