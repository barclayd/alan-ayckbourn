<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s fourth play, Standing Room Only, is the next play to come under the <a href="https://youtu.be/h1gAUzc5J4I">SpotLight</a> on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn Youtube channel</a>.</p>

<p>The playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, explores the significance of this early, withdrawn work which is rather more important than it is given credit for in <em><a href="https://youtu.be/h1gAUzc5J4I">SpotLight on Ayckbourn: Standing Room Only</a></em>.</p>

<p>As well as being his first work of speculative fiction, it is the earliest of his plays to be directed by the playwright himself, the first play to be optioned for both the West End and for television and, arguably, it is the first time his true voice as a playwright can be heard.</p>

<p>Find out more in <em><a href="https://youtu.be/h1gAUzc5J4I">SpotLight on Alan Ayckbourn: Standing Room Only</a></em> now streaming via the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube channel</a> and researched, written and presented by Simon Murgatroyd.</p>
