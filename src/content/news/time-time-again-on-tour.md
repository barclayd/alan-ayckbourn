<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s classic 1971 play <em>Time &amp; Time Again</em> is touring this summer.</p>

<p>Phil &amp; Ben Productions are producing the play in association with Eastbourne Productions. It is being toured alongside Frederick Knott’s <em>Dial M For Murder</em>; previously the company has toured productions of Ayckbourn’s <em>Bedroom Farce</em>, <em>Table Manners</em> and <em>Absent Friends</em>.</p>

<p><em>Time and Time Again</em> is currently announced for Devonshire Park Theatre in Eastbourne, the Lyceum Theatre, Crewe and the Wyvern Theatre, Swinburn, between July and August.</p>

<p>The production will star Pete Ashmore as Leonard, Katy Dean as Anna, Phil Stewart as Graham, Tanya-Loretta Dee as Joan and Simon Pothecary as Peter.</p>

<p>The play centres on Leonard, an ambivalent teacher who lives with his sister Anna and husband Graham. When he is introduced to the sports-mad Peter and his attractive fiancee, Joan, all their lives are changed.</p>

<p>As Leonard tries to win the affection of Joan, Peter tries to engage him in the local sports scene for which he is terminally unsuited whilst Graham looks on jealously.</p>

<p>Premiered in Scarborough in 1971, the play went into the West End in 1972 and was later adapted for television with Tom Courtney and Michael Robbins. It is regarded as the first of Ayckbourn’s tragicomedies and the playwright himself regards it as a favourite. He was inspired by his desire to write a play around “a central character who took no decisions, did nothing, everything was done for him and by simply taking no decisions he affects the whole course of the play.”</p>

<p>Tanya-Loretta Dee has worked with Alan Ayckbourn on several occasions including the world premieres of three of his plays. She has also appeared in <em>EastEnders</em>, <em>Peaky Blinders</em> and <em>The Bill</em>.</p>

<p>Pete Ashmore’s credits include <em>Strangers On A Train</em>, <em>Bedroom Farce</em>, <em>Murder By Misadventure</em>, <em>The Rivals</em>, <em>Romeo and Juliet </em>and <em>The Crown</em>.</p>

<p>Simon Pothecary has worked in <em>The Woman in Black</em>, <em>Not About Heroes</em> and <em>Romeo and Juliet</em> and appeared in <em>Fantastic Beasts: The Secret’s of Dumbledore</em> and <em>Love, Death and Robots</em>.</p>

<p>Katy Dean is familiar to Eastbourne audiences, having appeared in <em>An Inspector Calls</em> and <em>Table Manners</em> and Phil Stewart’s West End credits include <em>The Curious Incident of The Dog In The Night-Time</em> and <em>Blood Brothers</em>.</p>

<p><em>Time and Time Again</em> can be seen at <strong><a href="https://trafalgartickets.com/eastbourne-theatres-eastbourne/en-GB/event/play/time-and-time-again-tickets">Devonshire Park Theatre</a></strong>, Eastbourne, from 28 July to 1 August; the <strong><a href="https://trafalgartickets.com/lyceum-theatre-crewe/en-GB/event/play/time-and-time-again-tickets">Lyceum Theatre</a></strong>, Crewe, from 5 – 8 August; the <strong><a href="https://trafalgartickets.com/wyvern-theatre-swindon/en-GB/event/play/time-and-time-again-tickets">Wyvern Theatre</a></strong>, Swinburn, from 20 – 22 August. Further details are also available at the <strong><a href="https://philandben.co.uk">Phil &amp; Ben website</a></strong>.</p>
