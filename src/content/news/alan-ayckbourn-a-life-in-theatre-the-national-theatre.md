<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s five decade relationship with the National Theatre comes under the spotlight in the final <a href="https://youtu.be/6wNOYvu1TbI">Alan Ayckbourn – A Life in Theatre</a> video.</p>

<p>Since 1977, Alan Ayckbourn has enjoyed a significant relationship with the National Theatre and has seen more than a dozen of his plays and productions performed at the London venue.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, narrates an in-depth look at the history of this relationship alongside exclusive audio interview extracts by the playwright himself, Alan Ayckbourn.</p>

<p>With rarely seen images and photographs, the video charts Sir Peter Hall’s early attempts to bring Alan into the National Theatre resulting in its first major hit on the South Bank, <em>Bedroom Farce</em>.</p>

<p>From there, plays such as <em>Sisterly Feelings</em>, <em>Way Upstream</em>, <em>A Chorus of Disapproval</em> and <em>A Small Family Business</em> are explored as well as his acclaimed production of Arthur Miller’s <em>A View From The Bridge</em>.</p>

<p>The video also looks at Alan Ayckbourn’s two year tenure as a Company Director at the National Theatre during the late 1980s.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/6wNOYvu1TbI">Alan Ayckbourn – A Life in Theatre: The National Theatre</a> is the last of six videos exploring the different aspects of Alan Ayckbourn’s six decades in theatre. It can be found on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/6wNOYvu1TbI">here</a>.</p>
