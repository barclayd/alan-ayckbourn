<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s first play, <em><a href="/plays/the-square-cat">The Square Cat</a></em>, kicks off a new feature on the playwright’s <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">YouTube channel</a>.</p>

<p><em><a href="https://youtu.be/rFAiloiYhMA">SpotLight on Ayckbourn: The Square Cat</a></em> is the first in a series of videos in which the playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, will look at some of the significant plays in the Ayckbourn play canon.</p>

<p>The videos offer a look into the history and creation of the plays drawing on material from the Ayckbourn Archive – including, in this first video, a glimpse of an actual manuscript for the first play.</p>

<p><em>SpotLight on Ayckbourn: The Square Cat</em> can be found on <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">The Official Alan Ayckbourn Website YouTube</a> channel by clicking here or watching below. And if you enjoy the video, please subscribe to the YouTube channel.</p>

<p>Even more information about <em>The Square Cat</em> can be found at Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website by clicking <a href="/plays/the-square-cat">here</a>.</p>
