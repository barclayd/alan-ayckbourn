<p>Today marks the 50th anniversary of the world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn’s trilogy <em>The Norman Conquests</em>.</p>

<p><em>Table Manners</em> premiered at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, on 18 June 1973 to be quickly followed by<em> Living Together</em> and <em>Round and Round the Garden</em>.</p>

<p>After an enormously successful run in Scarborough, it would then have an award-winning run in the West End in 1973 and then play on Broadway in 1974. Subsequently, it has become one of the most performed and popular of Alan Ayckbourn’s works.</p>

<p>To mark the anniversary, Alan Ayckbourn’s Simon Murgatroyd has produced a 50th anniversary <a href="https://youtu.be/k9XLcSVdSjo">video</a> looking at the history and the achievements of the trilogy.</p>

<p>You can find it at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn You Tube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/k9XLcSVdSjo">here</a> or by clicking on the video below. There’s also more videos and in-depth details about the play at Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website by clicking <a href="/plays/the-norman-conquests">here</a>.</p>
