<p>An updated guide to essential Alan Ayckbourn facts has been launched on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg/featured">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/7tP4SlRgFIQ">Alan Ayckbourn: A Life In Facts</a> (2022 edition) is an updated guide to the playwright and his work, researched and narrated by the playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A.</p>
