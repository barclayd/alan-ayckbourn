<p>The latest <a href="https://youtu.be/oX76FWJ974k">Ask The Archivist</a> video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> looks at which of Alan Ayckbourn’s plays have not premiered in his adopted hometown of Scarborough.</p>

<p>With the announcement that Alan Ayckbourn’s 86th play – <em><a href="/plays/all-lies">All Lies</a></em> – will premiere at The Old Laundry Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere, from 6 – 22 May 2022, the playwright’s Archivist – Simon Murgatroyd – looks at which other of the playwright’s works have not premiered in Scarborough since 1959.</p>

<p>The answer can be found in the <a href="https://youtu.be/oX76FWJ974k">Ask The Archivist</a> video by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/oX76FWJ974k">here</a> at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>. If you have a question for Simon, you can email him at: <a href="mailto:enquiries@alanayckbourn.net">enquiries@alanayckbourn.net</a> or via <a href="/">www.alanayckbourn.net</a>.</p>
