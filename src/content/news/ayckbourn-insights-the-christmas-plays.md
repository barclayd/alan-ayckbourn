<p>Christmas is coming! It’s also coming to the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> with the latest Ayckbourn InSights video.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/H2q9tuVvaZ0">Ayckbourn InSights: The Christmas Plays</a> sees the playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, looking at the playwright’s many plays set at Christmas and asks why the playwright thinks it’s such an ideal time to set his works.</p>

<p>How many plays do you think the playwright has set at Christmas? There’s more than you think from the most obvious to the very obscure. Join Simon as he lists all the plays and even has Alan Ayckbourn himself speaking on his fondness for writing plays set at Christmas.</p>
