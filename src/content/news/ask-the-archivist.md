<p>I’ve launched a new feature today on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> called <a href="https://youtu.be/6RAVM2Sar5Q">Ask The Archivist</a>.</p>

<p>This is a chance to answer some frequently asked – and occasionally very obscure – questions about Alan Ayckbourn and his plays.</p>

<p>It kicks off with <a href="https://youtu.be/6RAVM2Sar5Q">Who Was Roland Allen?</a> This is a name Ayckbourn fans will recognise from his early plays and the answer as to who he is and what the name means can be found in this first video.</p>

<p>This will be a regular feature on the channel and you can email questions about Alan and his plays to me and I’ll endeavour to answer them in person and – at times – via the Ask The Archivist videos.</p>

<p>Head over to the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> for the new video by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/6RAVM2Sar5Q">here</a> and don’t forget to subscribe to the channel if you’ve enjoyed the video.</p>
