<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s classic tragi-comedy <em>Just Between Ourselves</em> is the subject of the new <a href="https://youtu.be/LvKnzzVnw9g">SpotLight on Ayckbourn</a> video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>The playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., goes behind the scenes of this hugely significant work in the playwright’s canon to explore how it came to be created.</p>

<p>Featuring contemporary interview recordings with the playwright as well as never before seen notes and photographs from the Ayckbourn Archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives at the University of York, this is a fascinating behind-the-scenes look at a pivotal Ayckbourn play.</p>

<p><em><a href="https://youtu.be/LvKnzzVnw9g">SpotLight on Ayckbourn: Just Between Ourselves</a></em> can be found on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/LvKnzzVnw9g">here</a>.</p>
