<p>The recently announced donation of The Ayckbourn Collection to Scarborough Museums &amp; Galleries is the subject of the latest video on the <a href="https://youtu.be/PW14lui-Olk">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist Simon Murgatroyd M.A. looks at why and how the collection was assembled, what it contains and its donation to Scarborough Museums &amp; Galleries.</p>

<p>Simon curated the collection and also facilitated the donation, which also includes several items drawn from his own personal Ayckbourn collection.</p>

<p>Items from The Ayckbourn Collection will also be going in display at Scarborough Art Gallery from 16 September 2023. Find out more on the <a href="https://scarboroughmuseumsandgalleries.org.uk">Scarborough Museums &amp; Galleries website</a>.</p>

<p>The Ayckbourn Collection video can be watched via the video below or on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/PW14lui-Olk">here</a>. If you enjoy the video, please subscribe to the channel.</p>
