<p><strong>◦&nbsp;<a href="https://victoriawoodtheatre.com/whats-on/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar">The Trial of Romeo Oscar</a></strong> at The Victoria Wood Theatre, Bowness-under-Windermere <em>(13 – 17 October)</em></p>

<p><strong>◦&nbsp;<a href="https://www.newvictheatre.org.uk/productions/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar/">The Trial of Romeo Oscar</a></strong> at The New Vic, Newcastle-under-Lyme <em>(21 – 31 October)</em></p>

<p><strong>◦&nbsp;<a href="https://trafalgartickets.com/eastbourne-theatres-eastbourne/en-GB/event/play/time-and-time-again-tickets">Time and Time Again</a> </strong>at Devonshire Park Theatre <em>(28 July – 1 August 2026)</em></p>

<p><strong>◦&nbsp;<a href="https://trafalgartickets.com/lyceum-theatre-crewe/en-GB/event/play/time-and-time-again-tickets">Time and Time Again</a> </strong>at Lyceum Theatre, Crewe <em>(5 – 8 August 2026)</em></p>

<p><strong>◦&nbsp;<a href="https://trafalgartickets.com/wyvern-theatre-swindon/en-GB/event/play/time-and-time-again-tickets">Time and Time Again</a> </strong>at the Wyvern Theatre, Swindon (20 – 22 August 2026)</p>

<p><strong>Talks &amp; Events</strong></p>

<p><strong>◦&nbsp;<a href="https://theatre-in-the-round.com/page-41/page-118/">The Library Theatre at 70 exhibition</a></strong> at Scarborough Library <em>(1 May to 27 June 2026)<br></em>An exhibition of 90 photographs celebrating the UK’s first theatre in the round company, founded by Stephen Joseph, and its first home at Scarborough library between 1955 and 1976. Curated by Simon Murgatroyd.</p>

<p><strong>◦&nbsp;<a href="https://theatre-in-the-round.com/page-41/page-118/">A Round 50: Theatre in the Round at Westwood</a> </strong>at Woodend Gallery and Studios<em> (20 June 2026 at 1pm)<br></em>A talk celebrating the 50th anniversary of the opening of the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round – the second home of Scarborough’s famed theatre-in-the-round company.</p>
