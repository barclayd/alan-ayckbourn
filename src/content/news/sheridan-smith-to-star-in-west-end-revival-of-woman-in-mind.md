<p>Sheridan Smith is to star in Alan Ayckbourn’s <em><strong><a href="/plays/woman-in-mind">Woman in Mind</a></strong></em> in the West End, directed by Michael Longhurst.</p>

<p>The play will run from December 9 to February 28 at the Duke of York’s Theatre, following which it will visit the Sunderland Empire from 4 March to 7 March 2026 and Theatre Royal, Glasgow from 10 March to 14 March 2026.</p>

<p>Further casting is to be announced.</p>

<p>It has set and costume design by Soutra Gilmour, lighting design by Lee Curran, sound design by Paul Arditti and casting by Jim Carnahan CSA and Liz Fraser CSA.</p>

<p>The play is produced by Wessex Grove and Gavin Kalin Productions.</p>

<p>Further details and bookings can be found at <strong><a href="https://www.womaninmindplay.com">www.womaninmindplay.com</a></strong>.</p>
