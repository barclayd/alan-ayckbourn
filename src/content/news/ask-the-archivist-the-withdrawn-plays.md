<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, is back with another <a href="https://youtu.be/b-Prp6EXZnY">Ask The Archivist</a> video answering the question, which Ayckbourn plays have been withdrawn and why?</p>

<p>Find out the answer as Simon looks at the nine of the playwright’s 89 plays (as of 2023) which have been withdrawn, why and where you can actually access them.</p>

<p>The video can be found by clicking on the video below or by visiting <a href="https://youtu.be/b-Prp6EXZnY">here</a> on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>If you have a question for Simon about Alan Ayckbourn or his plays, why not email it to him for a future video by writing to <a href="mailto:enquiries@alanayckbourn.net">enquiries@alanayckbourn.net</a>.</p>
