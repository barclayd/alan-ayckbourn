<p>This year marks the 70th anniversary of Stephen Joseph founding the UK’s first professional theatre-in-the-round company at Scarborough Library in 1955.</p>

<p>To celebrate this, theatre historian and Alan Ayckbourn’s archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, is holding an exhibition on the first floor of Scarborough Library from 10 – 12 July 2025. In this, the first of two articles, we’re taking a more in-depth look at what to expect in the exhibition.</p>

<p>The exhibition’s aim is to celebrate Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, which operated from 1955 to 1976 under Stephen Joseph and then Alan Ayckbourn. It aims to draw attention to the significance of Scarborough Library in British theatre history and to highlight some of the people who helped to establish the company.</p>

<p>In the first of two articles, we’re looking at the foyer area of the exhibition on the Library’s first floor and what will be on display.</p>

<p><strong>The Photograph Display</strong></p>

<p>The centre-piece of the exhibition is a display of 70 A4-sized photographs drawing on images from the history of the Library Theatre from 1955 to 1976. The photographs have been predominantly drawn from the Ayckbourn Archive, Scarborough LIbrary’s Stephen Joseph Collection and The Bob Watson Archive at the Stephen Joseph Theatre. Many have not been seen in public for many years, if at all.</p>

<p>The exhibition is broadly divided into seven areas:</p>

<p><strong>The Plays (1955 – 1976): </strong>There is a production photograph for each year of the Library Theatre (22 in all) highlighting a production from each year. It’s an opportunity to see the wide variety of plays – both new and established – over the two decades as well as a chance to spot some now famous faces and plays.</p>

<p><strong>The Library Theatre:</strong> A series of photographs illustrating the different areas of the Library Theatre such as the Concert Room – where performances took place – and dressing room as well as behind-the-scenes pictures of life at the theatre. Simon has created a new ground-plan – drawn up from original designs – to accompany this section and illustrate how the theatre was laid out and operated.</p>

<p><strong>The Companies:</strong> A selection of images of the seasonal companies at the Library Theatre including the inaugural company of 1955 with Stephen Joseph and Alan Ayckbourn’s first company as Artistic Director in 1972.</p>

<p><strong>People:</strong> A highlight of some of the most significant people associated with the Library Theatre from 1955 to 1976 such as Stephen Joseph, Ken Boden, Alan Ayckbourn and the librarian William Smettem.</p>

<p><strong>Stephen Joseph:</strong> A series of portraits looking at the founder of the Library Theatre, Stephen Joseph, illustrating the wide variety of work he did in association with the company.</p>

<p><strong>The Final Night:</strong> Photographs taken on the final night of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre on 11 September 1976 including the audiences, the company and the dismantling of the theatre.</p>

<p><strong>The Seasons / Posters</strong></p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2025/1957w-a4-w300.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="378" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
</div>

<p>For the exhibition, Simon has scanned and blown up the schedules for every season between 1955 and 1976, which will be displayed on the walls of the exhibition area. These offer a complete guide to the plays and seasons of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre.</p>

<p>They include all 17 of Alan Ayckbourn’s plays which premiered at the theatre as well as an opportunity to see what classic plays were produced alongside the plethora of new work encouraged by Stephen Joseph.</p>

<p>A selection of original mounted posters will also be on display including from the world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn’s break-out hit <em>Relatively Speaking</em> and the company’s first example of star casting with Margaret Rawlings in <em>Phèdre</em>.</p>

<p>The exhibition will also see the launch of an accompanying book – <em><strong><a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-187/page-144/page-176/">A Theatrical Revolution</a></strong></em> – looking at the creation of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre in 1955 and written by Simon Murgatroyd. Limited to just 70 signed and numbered copies and priced at £5, this will be available to buy in the foyer with a chance to meet and talk to Simon about your own memories of the Library Theatre.</p>

<p>In part 2, we’ll be looking at the other half of the exhibition in the Lecture Room and The Scarborough Room.</p>

<p><em>The Library Theatre at 70 – Celebrating Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre (1955 – 1976)</em> can be seen on the first floor of Scarborough Library on Thursday 10 and Friday 11 July from 10am – 5pm and on Saturday 12 July from 10am to 2pm. Entry is free.</p>
