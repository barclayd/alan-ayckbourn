<p>For this year’s inaugural <em>Big Ideas by the Sea</em> festival in Scarborough, the world premiere of a new filmed interview with Alan Ayckbourn was unveiled.</p>

<p>This interview, conducted by Dr Paul Elsam, is now available to stream for free via the Big Ideas by the Sea YouTube channel and the <a href="http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/page-6/page-7/page90.html">official Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>Recorded at the playwright’s home in June this year, the interview touches on a variety of subjects including his latest play, <em>The Girl Next Door</em>, his childhood during the Blitz as well as his thought on theatre both now and post-Covid.</p>

<p>The video also marks the launch of the new <a href="http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/page-6/page89.html">Video Interviews</a> section of Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website, which will incorporate some of the most notable interviews with the playwright currently available to stream.</p>

<p>The video can be streamed from the playwright’s website <a href="http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/page-6/page-7/page90.html">here</a> and you can find out more about the <em>Big Ideas by the Sea</em> festival <a href="https://bigideasbythesea.com/festival/">here</a>.</p>
