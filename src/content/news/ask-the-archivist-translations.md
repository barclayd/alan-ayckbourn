<p>Have Alan Ayckbourn’s plays been translated into other languages? is the question posed in today’s <a href="https://youtu.be/uVnAbQrn6cY">Ask The Archivist</a> video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>The playwright’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., looks into the history of translations of Alan’s plays and what languages they have been performed in over the decades.</p>

<p>It also marks the first location shoot for the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> with Simon visiting the Welsh resort of Portmeirion – and, yes, that’s a clue as to one of the languages Alan’s plays have been translated into!</p>
