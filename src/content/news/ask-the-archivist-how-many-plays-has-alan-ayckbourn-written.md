<p>The latest <a href="https://youtu.be/WOAib9YA6L4">Ask The Archivist</a> video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> answers the most frequently asked question of the playwright’s Archivist.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/WOAib9YA6L4">How many plays has Alan Ayckbourn written?</a> is the question being answered by Simon Murgatroyd in the video which will no doubt have to be updated in 2023 when the playwright’s next work is announced!</p>

<p>Along the way, Simon cautions about checking your sources given not everyone has the correct number of plays listed on their websites – amazing when a quick click on <a href="/">www.alanayckbourn.net</a> will always give you the playwright’s definitive answer!</p>
