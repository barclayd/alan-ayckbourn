<p>No so much an article today, but an anniversary. For today is the anniversary of Alan Ayckbourn’s play <em><a href="/plays/relatively-speaking">Relatively Speaking</a></em> opening in the West End.</p>

<p>The play premiered at the Duke of York’s Theatre on 29 March 1967 and was Alan’s break-out play. It had a phenomenally successful run in the West End and launched the playwright into the public eye.</p>
