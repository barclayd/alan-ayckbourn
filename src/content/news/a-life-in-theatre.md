<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s life in theatre comes under the spotlight in a new series of videos which launch today on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">official Ayckbourn YouTube channel</a>.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/_zf1bpCfkHM">Alan Ayckbourn: A Life In Theatre</a> will look at each of Alan Ayckbourn’s major theatre careers in depth in each video, beginning with his formative career as an actor.</p>

<p>The series is researched and narrated by Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, and features quotes by the playwright as well as an extensive range of images and material drawn from the Ayckbourn Archive.</p>

<p>Future videos will look at his playwriting and directing careers as well as his roles as Artistic Director of the Stephen Joseph Theatre and his five years as a Radio Drama Producer for the BBC.</p>

<p>You can find the first video, <a href="https://youtu.be/_zf1bpCfkHM">A Life In Theatre – The Actor</a> at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">official Ayckbourn YouTube channel</a>.</p>
