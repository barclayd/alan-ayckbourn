<p>It’s time for a new question for Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist in the latest <a href="https://youtu.be/iOsHxQO7tXo">Ask The Archivist</a> video on the A<a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">yckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>This time, the question which has been submitted by long-standing supporters of Alan Ayckbourn’s website, is which actor has appeared in the most Ayckbourn productions in his home town of Scarborough where the majority of his plays have premiered?</p>

<p>If you’re a fan of Ayckbourn who travels to Scarborough to see his productions, you’ve probably got your own ideas about who’s appeared in the most productions and the most world premieres.</p>

<p>Here’s the chance to see if you’re right or just to discover a new Ayckbourn fact in the new <a href="https://youtu.be/iOsHxQO7tXo">Ask The Archivist</a> video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>, which can be found <a href="https://archivingayckbourn.home.blog/?page_id=4003">here</a>.</p>
