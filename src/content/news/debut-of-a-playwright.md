<p>Today marks the anniversary of Alan Ayckbourn’s debut as a professional playwright on 30 July 1955.</p>

<p>On that day, his first play <em><a href="/plays/the-square-cat">The Square Cat</a></em> opened at the Library Theatre in Scarborough, launching a playwriting career which has been more successful than Alan could ever have imagined.</p>

<p>If you want to learn more about this povital moment in Alan Ayckbourn’s life and career, why not check out <a href="https://youtu.be/rFAiloiYhMA">SpotlIght on The Square Cat</a> on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> where I talk about the impetus behind the play, the work itself and the reaction to it.</p>
