<p><a href="https://youtu.be/fn8APONUYIg">SpotLight on Ayckbourn</a> continues its looks at one of Alan Ayckbourn’s most successful and popular works with part 2 of its look at <em>A Chorus of Disapproval</em>.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, tells the story of the less than smooth transition of the play from Scarborough to the National Theatre and its particular casting issues.</p>

<p>From there, he explores a contentious transfer into the West End which marked the final stage role of the actor Colin Blakely.</p>

<p>Using material exclusively drawn from the Ayckbourn Archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives, SpotLight on Ayckbourn sheds new light on a previously little known aspect of A Chorus of Disapproval’s journey. </p>
