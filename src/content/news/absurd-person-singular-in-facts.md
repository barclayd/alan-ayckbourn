<p>We’re just a week away from the 50th anniversary of Alan Ayckbourn’s play <em>Absurd Person Singular</em> on 26 June.</p>

<p>As part of the ongoing celebration of the play, the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> has just launched a video <a href="https://youtu.be/SxD1opC7zy0"><em>Absurd Person Singular</em> – In Facts</a>.</p>

<p>The playwright’s archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., narrates the video which features illustrations drawn from the Ayckbourn Archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives at the University of York.</p>

<p>Check out the short video for some facts about <em>Absurd Person Singula</em>r by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/SxD1opC7zy0">here</a> and don’t forget to subscribe to the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">channel</a>.</p>
