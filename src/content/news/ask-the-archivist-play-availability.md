<p>Back with Ask The Archivist and a question that just keeps coming up consistently – and has a quick and simple answer.</p>

<p>The website / blog is frequently asked, where can you buy specific Ayckbourn plays online? The main issue appears to be people trying to obtain them through Amazon.</p>

<p>OK, as most of us are aware, Amazon may be an easy shopping option, but it’s not a great option for anything remotely out of the mainstream – and that’s before we even get into the ethical issues .</p>

<p>Given that, currently, more than 70 of Alan Ayckbourn’s plays are in print and kept in publication – you’d be lucky to find more than a third of them in stock on Amazon and many have inflated prices or those very strange several week postage times which you know just means they’ll never arrive within your lifetime.</p>

<p>So, in the UK, there’s two answers. The first is go direct to the shop on the Concord International website <strong><a href="https://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/shop">here</a></strong>. As publishers of individual play texts, they have every single Ayckbourn play that’s in print (generally on a print to order basis) and even have a specific Alan Ayckbourn section <strong><a href="https://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/collections/perform/1495">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p>If it’s been published (which is essentially about 75 of his 91 plays), then it will be available from these pages and – as the published editions are generally printed to order – always in stock. So the simple answer is head <strong><a href="https://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/collections/perform/1495">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p>Also worth noting that many of the more recent plays are also exclusively available as ebook editions via Concord Theatricals, so that’s also the destination for digital options.</p>

<p>I’d also advocate supporting independent online bookshops and actual stores. If you want to take this path – and knowing that Concord International / Samuel French always have them available – send or give your local bookshop the details of the scripts from the Concord site. I have done this with local booksellers and had no problem in obtaining the scripts – there’s obviously just the time it takes to order and ship them. But you are supporting local bookstores, which is obviously increasingly important.</p>

<p>There is a slight proviso for customers in the USA. The Concord Theatricals shop for North America does stock the vast majority of Alan’s plays and should be the first port of call. But there are several exceptions – for reasons we’ve not actually been able to ascertain – which includes the very popular <em>Damsels in Distress</em> trilogy not being available.</p>

<p>Sadly, the only current way to get these specific play scripts is to order them from Europe, but that does come with increased costs (and likely to become even more expensive in the coming months). Should this change in the future, the blog will publish details.</p>

<p>However, generally speaking, if you’re having difficulty in obtaining a specific Ayckbourn play texts – head directly to the publisher itself. None of Alan Ayckbourn’s published individual plays are considered currently out of print – they’re all available so you should be able to get whatever you need.</p>

<p>You can also find a full list of which Ayckbourn plays have been published in the <strong><a href="/publications">Publications</a></strong> section of <strong><a href="/">Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website</a></strong> and there’s a even a page with specific links to the shop pages for all currently available plays <strong><a href="/publications/buy-the-plays">here</a></strong>.</p>
