<p>The Bob Watson Archive at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in Scarborough is the subject of the latest <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> video.</p>

<p><a href="https://youtu.be/VV_Tr3kn8HA">Archiving Ayckbourn: The Bob Watson Archive Part 1</a> sees Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., introduce the Archive and its history.</p>

<p>Based within the theatre but little known, the Archive holds material pertaining to the entire history of the company from 1955 to the present day. It also holds a significant collection of material relating to Alan Ayckbourn, who has been associated with the company since 1957.</p>

<p>Part 2 – coming soon – will have a little more in-depth look at some of the key pieces within The Bob Watson Archive.</p>

<p>You can find the <a href="https://youtu.be/VV_Tr3kn8HA">Archiving Ayckbourn</a> video at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/VV_Tr3kn8HA">here</a>.</p>
