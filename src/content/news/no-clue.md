<p>It’s a little known Ayckbourn fact but the playwright nearly wrote the screenplay for the movie <em>Clue</em>, based on the boardgame of the same name.</p>

<p>Back in 1981, he was approached by a Hollywood Studio to write the screenplay for a movie based on <em>Clue</em> – or <em>Cluedo</em> as it’s known in the UK. It’s a fascinating piece of trivia with the correspondence relating to it held in the Ayckbourn Archives in the Borthwick Institute for Archives at the University of York.</p>

<p>You can find out all about Alan’s aborted involvement in the movie in the latest video – <em><a href="https://youtu.be/4VzOYTJ64vw">Unseen Ayckbourn: Clue the Movie</a></em> – on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>, including rarely seen documents pertaining to the proposal.</p>

<p>This is the first of a series of videos looking at little known aspects of Alan’s writing career drawn from my book, <em><a href="http://publications.alanayckbourn.net/iframe/page22.html">Unseen Ayckbourn</a></em>. You can see the video <a href="https://youtu.be/4VzOYTJ64vw">here</a> and please don’t forget to subscribe if you enjoy the video or any other material on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">channel</a>.</p>

<p><em>Clue</em> is also showing at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, this week on 245 – 25 September. Click <a href="https://www.sjt.uk.com/event/1223/wea_film__clue_1985_pg">here</a> for more details.</p>
