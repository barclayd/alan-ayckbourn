<p>Scarborough Library will be celebrating Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre as part of this year’s <strong><a href="https://www.heritageopendays.org.uk">Heritage Open Days</a></strong> event.</p>

<p>The venue is teaming up with Alan Ayckbourn’s archivist and theatre historian Simon Murgatroyd to present three events offering a behind the scenes look to tie in with a special anniversary during the week.</p>

<p>On 11 September 1976, the final performance of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre took place with Alan Ayckbourn’s <em>Just Between Ourselves</em>. That same evening, the theatre was dismantled as the company rushed to open its new venue, the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, the following month.</p>

<p>On the exact 50th anniversary, Simon will be giving a talk celebrating the achievements of The Library Theatre and telling the story of both the final night and what led the company to have to leave the library after a bruising encounter with a rival theatre project in the town.</p>

<p><em>The Final Night of The Library Theatre – 50 Year On</em> will take place on Friday 11 September at 1.30pm in the Concert Room at Scarborough Library; the same room where the final performance took place. Admission is free, although tickets should be booked <strong><a href="https://www.heritageopendays.org.uk/submission-event/the-final-night-of-the-library-theatre-50-years-on.html">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p>To accompany the talk, Simon will also be offering a behind-the-scenes peek at The Library Theatre with guided tours of the surviving spaces of The Library Theatre on Thursday 17 September at 6pm and Saturday 19 September at 10.30am.</p>

<p>Places on these tours are free but strictly limited and tickets must be booked in advance. For the 17 September event, book <strong><a href="https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/behind-the-scenes-of-the-library-theatre-tickets-1992569825988?aff=oddtdtcreator&amp;keep_tld=true">here</a></strong> and for the 19 September event, book <strong><a href="https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/behind-the-scenes-of-the-library-theatre-tickets-1992569906228?aff=erellivmlt">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p>If you are interested in the history of The Library Theatre, a new edition of Simon’s book <strong><a href="https://www.theatre-in-the-round.com/page-120/page-121/page22.html"><em>A Theatrical Revolution</em></a></strong> has just been published and will be available at these events. It is also available via the A Round Town website by clicking <strong><a href="https://www.theatre-in-the-round.com/page-120/">here</a></strong>.</p>

<p><em>A Theatrical Revolution</em> traces the creation and inaugural year of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, founded by Stephen Joseph as the UK’s first professional theatre-in-the-round company at Scarborough Library in 1955.</p>

<p>It also includes an exclusive interview and preface with Sir Alan Ayckbourn as well as never previously published photographs from the first season.</p>
