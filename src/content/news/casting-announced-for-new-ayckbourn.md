<p>The cast for Alan Ayckbourn’s latest play <em>The Trial of Romeo Oscar</em> has been announced by the Stephen Joseph Theatre.</p>

<p>The production, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, will feature Russell Richardson, Frances Marshall, Leigh Symonds, Hayden Wood, Elizabeth Boag, Richard Stacey and Olivia Woolhouse.</p>

<p>Set and costume design is by Kevin Jenkins, lighting Designer by Mark ‘Tigger’ Johnson, associate sound design by Ernest Acquah; wardrobe supervision by Julia Perry-Mook with casting by Sarah Hughes CDG.</p>

<p><em>The Trial of Romeo Oscar</em> is Alan Ayckbourn’s 92nd play and continues his exploration of the themes of artificial intelligence, the value of culture and, ultimately, what defines us as human as the lines are increasingly blurred. </p>

<p>In space, no-one can hear you dream… In the year 2194, humanity is scattered around the solar system and day-to-day life on earth looks very different. Preparations are underway for a landmark legal case.</p>

<p>Romeo Oscar, a military issue android, is on trial for murder, the first non-human to be tried in a court of law. The circumstances surrounding the incident are unclear, the evidence is polarising, and selecting an impartial jury is proving a challenge.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s latest play is also his first courtroom drama exploring a question that artists have been asking for centuries – what makes us human?</p>

<p><em>The Trail of Romeo Oscar</em> will premiere play at the <strong><a href="https://sjt.uk.com/events/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar">Stephen Joseph Theatre</a></strong>, Scarborough, and run from 4 September – 3 October 2026. It will then. be followed by a short in-thwe-round tour to <strong><a href="https://victoriawoodtheatre.com/whats-on/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar">The Victoria Wood Theatre</a></strong>, Bowness-on-Windermere, from 13 – 17 October and <strong><a href="https://www.newvictheatre.org.uk/productions/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar/">The New Vic</a></strong>, Newcastle-under-Lyme, from 21 – 31 October.</p>

<p>Further details and bookings can be found on the Stephen Joseph Theatre website <strong><a href="https://sjt.uk.com/events/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar">here</a></strong>. Further details about the play itself will be carried on Alan Ayckbourn’s official website <strong><a href="/plays/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar">here</a></strong>.</p>
