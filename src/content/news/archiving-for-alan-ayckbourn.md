<p>Today is International Archives Day in which archives around the world celebrate their contribution to culture and society.</p>

<p>I’m marking the day with a <a href="https://youtu.be/cFnwzsRlers">video</a>, <a href="https://youtu.be/cFnwzsRlers">Archiving For Alan Ayckbourn</a>, looking at my own role as Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, what I do and what it means to me. From working in the archive to administering Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website to what I feel I contribute to theatre culture, this is an insight into the job I adore.</p>

<p>You can find the video on the official <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/cFnwzsRlers">here</a>.</p>

<p>I hope you enjoy the video and it offers an idea of why archiving and my work is such a passion for me.</p>
