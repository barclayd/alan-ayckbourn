<p>Simon gave the talk at Filey Library on 19 October 2023 and it was initially live-streamed. North Yorkshire Libraries have now kindly made it available to the general public online for free.</p>

<p>The talk looks at the extraordinary early achievements of Alan Ayckbourn at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, between 1957 and 1972.</p>

<p>In 1957, Alan joined the company as an acting stage manager before being promoted to actor the following year. By 1959, he had had his first play commissioned and produced and was soon a co-resident writer with David Campton.</p>

<p>In 1961, he directed for the first time and quickly gave up on acting to concentrate on writing and directing. And then, in 1972, he became the Artistic Director of the Scarborough company. An extraordinary journey in a short length of time.</p>

<p>You can hear the stories behind this journey as Simon discusses these formative years.</p>

<p>To access the livestream, click <a href="https://www.youtube.com/live/7MOEQk-lKvU?si=x3KwjhxzSwPi-bkp">here</a>, or the YouTube link below. Simon hopes to return to Filey Library next year with the second part of the talk discussing Alan Ayckbourn’s four decades as Artistic Director of what is now the Stephen Joseph Theatre.</p>
