<p>Have you ever wondered what inspired Alan Ayckbourn to write his fame trilogy of plays, <em>The Norman Conquests</em>?</p>

<p>The latest <a href="https://youtu.be/fumXgka0NYs">Ayckbourn InSights</a> video on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> has the answer – and, as is frequently the case with Alan Ayckbourn – it’s probably not what you would imagine.</p>

<p>Find out how a misjudged comment became one of the most highly-regarded theatrical works of the ’70s and the latter half of the 20th century in this video, researched and presented by Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd. <a href="https://youtu.be/fumXgka0NYs">Ayckbourn InSights: What Inspired Alan Ayckbourn to Write The Norman Conquests</a> can be found <a href="https://youtu.be/fumXgka0NYs">here</a> on the Ayckbourn YouTube Channel.</p>

<p>And if you enjoy this video, there’s an insight into why Alan chose the strange title <em>Absurd Person Singular</em> for another of his most famous works by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/rPTLQOGK4nM">here</a>.</p>
