<p>The recently announced donation of The Ayckbourn Collection to Scarborough Museums and Galleries was featured on BBC Radio York last night.</p>

<p>Bek Homer interviewed Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, about the collection, its creation and the forthcoming exhibition of items drawn from The Ayckbourn Collection at Scarborough Art Gallery.</p>

<p>The interview can be heard on the <a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/sounds/play/p0g43qn1">BBC Sounds</a> website by clicking <a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/sounds/play/p0g43qn1">here</a> and commences at approximately 45 minutes into the programme.</p>

<p>The Ayckbourn Collection is a small curated collection of material relating to Alan Ayckbourn, Stephen Joseph and theatre in the round in Scarborough donated by Sir Alan Ayckbourn to Scarborough Museums and Galleries for the permanent collections.</p>

<p>The Ayckbourn Collection was curated and facilitated by the playwright’s archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, and items will be on display at Scarborough Art Gallery from 16 September. Admission to Scarborough Museums and Galleries is £3 for an annual pass to all its sites.</p>

<p>Further details about the Ayckbourn Collection can be found on Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website <a href="http://www.alanayckbourn.net/page-10/page18.html">here</a>.</p>
