<p>Justt a quick and huge thanks to everyone who came to my talk at Scarborough Art Gallery yesterday to mark The Ayckbourn Collection display coming to an end.</p>

<div>
<figure><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2024/c2a9-mo-murgatroyd-w350.jpg" alt="" width="350" height="350" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption><sub>Simon Murgatroyd at Scarborough Art Gallery (© Mo Murgatroyd)</sub></figcaption></figure>

<p>It was a pleasure to talk to a full house and to have worked over the past year with Scarborough Museums &amp; Galleries. The talks at the gallery have been a real highlight for me and it’s been wonderful to meet so many people interested in Alan Ayckbourn, Stephen Joseph and theatre in the round.</p>

<p>Thanks to all the staff at Scarborough Museums &amp; Galleries for all their help and enthusiasm – it’s been real joy to work with the team and I look forward to working with them more in the future.</p>

<p>And thank you to everyone who has supported the exhibition over the past year, visited the gallery and come to the talks – it’s been a delight to hear how much interest the display has generated.</p>

<p><em>Simon Murgatroyd, 24 June 2024</em></p>
