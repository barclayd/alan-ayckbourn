<p>If you’re looking to mark Halloween with a few theatrical ghosts and spirits, then take a delve into some of Alan Ayckbourn’s haunted plays.</p>

<p>There’s a video looking at his forays into the supernatural on the Ayckbourn YouTube channel, which can be found by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/Bg5zO2uTr6U">here</a> (or the video below).</p>

<p>If you’re looking to read about the supernatural tinged plays, then visit <a href="/">Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website</a> where you can find out about <em><a href="/plays/haunting-julia">Haunting Julia</a></em>, <em><a href="/plays/snake-in-the-grass">Snake in the Grass</a></em> and <em><a href="/plays/life-and-beth">Life &amp; Beth</a></em>.</p>

<p>Or if you want to be very obscure, you can also looking into the history of his little know <em><a href="/plays/the-fearsome-threesome-ghost-stories">Ghost Stories</a></em> (or <em>The Fearsome Threesome</em>), a late night tale of horror from the ’80s by the playwright.</p>

<p>And if you want something more spooky, not necessarily by Alan Ayckbourn, there’s a whole section dealing with the phenomenal <em><a href="http://thewomaninblack.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">The Woman in Black</a></em> play on the A Round Town website. Find out about the original Scarborough production of the play which has haunted theatres for decades around the world by clicking <a href="http://thewomaninblack.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">here</a>.</p>

<p>Have a good Halloween!</p>
