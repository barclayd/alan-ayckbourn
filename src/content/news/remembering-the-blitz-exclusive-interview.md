<p>Alan Ayckbourn talks exclusively about his memories of growing up during the Blitz to his Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>The playwright was born in April 1939 and his formative memories are of growing up in London during World War 2. In the <a href="https://youtu.be/cbXIWawZB5o">exclusive interview</a>, he talks about his experiences and memories which also served as part of the inspiration for his 85th play, <em>The Girl Next Door</em>.</p>

<p>You can find the interview <a href="https://youtu.be/cbXIWawZB5o">here</a> at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> and <em>The Girl Next Door</em> returns to the S<a href="https://www.sjt.uk.com/event/1131/the_girl_next_door">tephen Joseph Theatre</a>, Scarborough, from 1 – 4 September 2021 before touring to the <a href="https://www.newvictheatre.org.uk/productions/the-girl-next-door/">New Vic</a>, Newcastle-under-Lyme, from 7 – 18 September.</p>
