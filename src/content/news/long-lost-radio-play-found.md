<p>A long thought lost 1977 radio adaptation of Alan Ayckbourn’s classic play<em><strong><a href="/plays/absurd-person-singular"> Absurd Person Singular</a></strong></em> has been rediscovered.</p>

<div>
<figure><figcaption><sub>Christopher Godwin as Ronald in the world premiere of Absurd Person Singular in 1972 (© Haydonning Ltd)</sub></figcaption></figure>
</div>

<p>First broadcast on Saturday Night Theatre on BBC Radio 4 1977, this adaptation of <em>Absurd Person Singular</em> has never been repeated and was presumed lost with next to no details known about it.</p>

<p>It has now been found in archive and will be broadcast on BBC Radio 4 Extra and BBC Sounds from 30 May 2024, having been unheard for more than 45 years.</p>

<p>One of the most significant facts about the radio adaptation is it features Christopher Godwin playing the role of Sidney Hopcroft; Christopher appeared in the world premiere production of the play at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1972 although there he played Ronald.</p>

<p>Considered one of Alan Ayckbourn’s most significant and funniest plays, the radio adaptation stars Judy Parfitt as Marion, Stephen Murray as Ronald and Christopher Godwin as Sidney.</p>

<p><em>Hidden Treasures; Absurd Person Singular</em> can be heard from 30 May on BBC Radio 4 Extra and BBC Sounds with further details <strong><a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m001zm99">here</a></strong>.</p>
