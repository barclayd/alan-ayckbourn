<p>Simon Murgatroyd’s book celebrating the founding of the UK’s first professional theatre-in-the-round in the UK, <em>A Theatrical Revolution</em>, has been republished.</p>

<p>Released last year to celebrate the 70th anniversary of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre in Scarborough as a signed and numbered limited edition, an open edition has now been released to tie in with <em>The Library Theatre at 70</em> exhibition now running at Scarborough Library.</p>

<p><em><strong><a href="https://www.theatre-in-the-round.com/page-41/page-118/page-119/">A Theatrical Revolution – Stephen Joseph and the Creation of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre</a></strong></em> is written by Alan Ayckbourn’s archivist and theatre historian Simon Murgatroyd and includes an exclusive preface and interview with Sir Alan Ayckbourn.<br><br>The book delves into the founding of the UK’s first professional theatre-in-the-round company in 1955 by the pioneer Stephen Joseph. Drawing on archival material held by Scarborough Library and in the Ayckbourn Archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives, the book explores how a significant moment in British Theatre history came to pass in the most unlikely of places on the first floor of Scarborough Library.</p>

<p>The contents include…</p>

<ul>
<li>A preface by Sir Alan Ayckbourn CBE</li>

<li>An interview with Sir Alan Ayckbourn about his early memories of working at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre.</li>

<li>An in-depth account by Simon Murgatroyd of the founding year of the company in 1955 from concept to performance to aftermath.</li>

<li>Contemporary articles by Stephen Joseph explaining and promoting Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre.</li>

<li>Details of the 1955 company and plays.</li>

<li>A brief timeline of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre from 1955 – 1976.</li>

<li>A ground plan illustrating the layout of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre on Scarborough Library’s first floor.</li>

<li>Rarely seen photographs and extensive references.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong><a href="http://www.simon-murgatroyd.com/">Simon Murgatroyd M.A.</a></strong>&nbsp;is a professional writer and Alan Ayckbourn’s archivist who previously wrote <strong><em><a href="http://www.alanayckbourn.net/styled-13/page28.html">Unseen Ayckbourn</a></em></strong>, looking at the unproduced, withdrawn and unwritten works of Alan Ayckbourn. His articles on Alan Ayckbourn and theatre-in-the-round have been published worldwide including regularly for the world premiere and West End productions of Alan Ayckbourn’s plays.<br><br>He is also the founder and creator of both&nbsp;<strong><a href="/">Alan Ayckbourn’s Official Website</a></strong>&nbsp;and the&nbsp;<strong><a href="http://www.a-round-town.com/">A Round Town</a></strong>&nbsp;website, which celebrates the history of theatre-in-the-round in Scarborough.</p>
