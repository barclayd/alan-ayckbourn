<p>Last week, Alan Ayckbourn’s Archivist have a talk called <em>Ayckbourn’s Gardens</em> against the fabulous backdrop of Scarborough’s Italian Gardens on the South Cliff.</p>

<p>This talk with Simon Murgatroyd M.A. was recorded and is now available to stream from the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Official Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a>.</p>

<p>Within the talk, Simon looks at gardens within Alan Ayckbourn plays – why they are used, how gardens are achieved in the round and why since 1984, gardens have become spaces where the fantastic often happens.</p>

<p>The talk can be found below or on the<a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg"> Ayckbourn YouTube Channel</a> by clicking <a href="https://youtu.be/inp3FQOMst8">here</a>. If you want to know more about Simon’s forthcoming talks, visit his <a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk">A Round Town</a> website celebrating theatre in the round in Scarborough by clicking <a href="http://www.theatre-in-the-round.co.uk/page-46/">here</a>.</p>
