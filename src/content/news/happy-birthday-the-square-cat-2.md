<p>On 30 July 1959, Alan Ayckbourn made his professional playwriting debut with The Square Cat.</p>

<p>This comedy’s starring the playwright himself in dual roles premiered at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan’s most influential mentor Stephen Joseph.</p>
