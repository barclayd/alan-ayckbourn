<p><a href="https://youtu.be/fQ2cSb4TuV0">The Alan Ayckbourn: A Life in Theatre</a> video series continues this week exploring Alan Ayckbourn’s career as a director.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://youtu.be/fQ2cSb4TuV0">latest video</a> concentrates on the career which has run practically simultaneously with his playwriting career and which Ayckbourn himself considers to be the main strand to his work in theatre.</p>

<p>Alan Ayckbourn: The Director explores his path into directing as the result of Stephen Joseph’s encouragement and its development which has led to him directing more than 350 productions in Scarborough, London and New York among others – including more than 40 productions in the West End or at the National Theatre.</p>

<p>The video can be found on the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg">Official Ayckbourn YouTube</a> channel <a href="https://youtu.be/fQ2cSb4TuV0">here</a>.</p>
