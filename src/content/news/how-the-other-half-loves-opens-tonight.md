<p>Alan Ayckbourn’s classic play <em>How The Other Half Loves</em> opens tonight at The Old Vic – presented in-the-round for the first time in the West End.</p>

<p>The revival of the 1969 play is being directed by Phillip Breen (<em>Anna Karenina</em>,&nbsp;<em>Red or Dead</em>) and stars Roger Allam as Frank Foster,&nbsp;Dorothy Atkinson as Fiona Foster, is&nbsp;Ayesha Antoine&nbsp;as Teresa Phillips,&nbsp;Laura Elsworthy&nbsp;as Mary Featherstone, Adam Gillen&nbsp;as William Featherstone and&nbsp;Rowan Polonski&nbsp;as Bob Phillips.</p>

<figure><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/"><img src="https://pub-4c23c36058c0491eaa4d6d55c25b33de.r2.dev/news/2026/htohl-headshots-lan-w1024.jpg" alt="" width="1024" height="315" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a></figure>

<p>Director&nbsp;Phillip Breen&nbsp;said, “I’m so excited about a new generation of audiences experiencing Ayckbourn for the first time, with its big laughs and its deep sadnesses. Alan Ayckbourn is not only one of the English stage’s most brilliant comedians, but one of its great formal pioneers, too.&nbsp;<em>How the Other Half Loves</em>&nbsp;is the first time Ayckbourn plays around with theatre’s two essential building blocks, space and time – and the result is thrilling.”</p>

<p><em>How The Other Half Loves</em> premiered at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1967 before its West And premiere in 1970, which was Alan’s second major West End hit and cemented his reputation as one of the most exciting playwrights of the period.</p>

<p>Performed around the world and translated into more than 20 languages subsequently, this will be the fourth major West End production of the play but the first to present it in-the-round as it was originally conceived and performed in Scarborough.</p>

<p>Given Alan Ayckbourn himself believes this is the best way to direct and see the play, it promises to make this an essential production whether as a newcomer to Alan Ayckbourn’s classic work or an existing fan.</p>

<p>This witty comedy of sex, class and material misunderstanding centres on Fiona Foster and Bob Phillips each accused of having an affair by their spouses. They use the innocent and unsuspecting Featherstones as an alibi, dragging them into a web of chaos and confusion. As the lives of the three couples intertwine, the lies deepen and mayhem ensues in one of theatre’s most entertaining comedies.</p>

<p><em>How The Other Half Loves</em> is running at The Old Vic from 29 July – 19 September and further details and bookings can be found <strong><a href="https://www.oldvictheatre.com/stage/other-half/">here</a></strong>.</p>
