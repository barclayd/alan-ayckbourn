This page contains details of recordings and adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical *By Jeeves* in media other than the stage (such as TV, film and radio). To find out more about *By Jeeves*, visit the Plays section **[here](/plays/jeeves)**.

**Note:** Most of these recordings are not currently available.

**Bertie Wooster** Steven Pacey  
**Jeeves** Malcolm Sinclair  
**Watkyn Bassett** Robert Austin  
**Madeleine Bassett** Diana Morrison  
**Gussie Fink-Nottle** Simon Day  
**Bingo Little** Nicholas Haverson  
**Honoria Glossop** Lucy Tregear  
**Stiffy Byng** Cathy Sara  
**Cyrus Budge III** Nicolas Collicos  
**'Stinker' Pinker** Richard Long  
**Chorus** Denise Silvey / Giles Taylor / Mike Windsor

**Note:** This production was specially adapted for BBC Radio by Alan Ayckbourn and includes spoken dialogue exclusive to this version. Of particular note is that, despite Alan Ayckbourn being a professional Radio Drama Producer at the BBC between 1965 and 1970, this production marked the first time Alan Ayckbourn had ever adapted one of his own works for the radio.
