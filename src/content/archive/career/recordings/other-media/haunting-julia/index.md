### Haunting Julia

This page contains details of recordings and adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn's play *Haunting Julia* in media other than the stage (such as TV, film and radio). To find out more about the play *Haunting Julia*, visit the Plays section **[here](/plays/haunting-julia)**.

### Audio Play (2020)

**Streaming Premiere:** 1 December 2020  
**Streaming End:** 31 January 2021  
**Format:** Audio Stream  
**Company:** Haydonning Ltd  
**Website:** www.sjt.uk.com

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Sound:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Final Mix:** Paul Stear

**Cassette:** Not available  
**CD:** Not available  
**Digital:** Not available

**Character** **Actor**  
**Joe** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Andy** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Ken** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Voices** Naomi Petersen

**Note:** During 2020, all theatres in the UK were closed as a result of the Covid-19 pandemic. This resulted in Alan Ayckbourn taking the decision to premiere a new play, ***[Anno Domino](/career/recordings/other-media/anno-domino)***, as an audio stream with him and his wife, Heather Stoney, taking on all the roles.  
The success of *Anno Domino* led Alan to record *Haunting Julia* as an audio play to be streamed during the Christmas 2020 period - when most theatres were still either closed or opening on a very limited basis.  
The Haydonning Ltd production was initially streamed for an exclusive period on the Stephen Joseph Theatre's website from 1 December 2020 to 31 January 2021.
