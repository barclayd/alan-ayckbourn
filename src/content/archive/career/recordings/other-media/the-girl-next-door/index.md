This page contains details of recordings and adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn's play *The Girl Next Door* in media other than the stage (such as TV, film and radio). To find out more about the play *The Girl Next Door*, visit the Plays section **[here](/plays/the-girl-next-door)**.

**Note:** *The Girl Next Door* was recorded in front of a live audience during its world premiere run at the Stephen Joseph Theatre during June 2021. This was then streamed from the SJT website from 28 June to 3 July; concurrently with the final week of the live production. This also marked the first stream of a staged Ayckbourn production directed by Alan Ayckbourn himself.
