This page contains details of recordings and adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn's play *The Revengers' Comedies* in media other than the stage (such as TV, film and radio). To find out more about the play *The Revengers' Comedies*, visit the Plays section **[here](/plays/the-revengers-comedies)**.

**Note:** None of these recordings are available.

**Note:** *The Revengers' Comedies* runs for just 82 minutes; in context the original stage production of *The Revengers' Comedies* ran longer than 5 hours. This production is frequently mistaken for a television adaptation, but initially it had a limited cinema release in the UK, France and North America.
