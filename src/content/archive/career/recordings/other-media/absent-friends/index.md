### Absent Friends

This page contains details of recordings and adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn's play *Absent Friends* in media other than the stage (such as TV, film and radio). To find out more about the play *Absent Friends*, visit the Plays section **[here](/plays/absent-friends)**.

**Note:** None of these recordings are available.

Unhappy with the dramatic cuts to the play, he requested the play be withdrawn and not broadcast again. It has subsequently been broadcast but the BBC has now officially been requested to not broadcast the adaptation in the future.
