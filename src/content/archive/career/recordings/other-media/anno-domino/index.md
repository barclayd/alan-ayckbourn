This page contains details of recordings and adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn's play *Anno Domino* in media other than the stage (such as TV, film and radio). To find out more about the play *Anno Domino*, visit the Plays section **[here](/plays/anno-domino)**.

### Audio Play (2020)

**Streaming Premiere:** 25 May 2020  
**Format:** Audio Stream  
**Production:** Haydonning Ltd

**Availability**  
**Cassette:** Not available  
**CD:** Not available  
**Digital:** Not available

**Director** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Sound** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Associate Sound** Paul Stear

**Craig Dean** Alan Ayckbourn

**Raz Wray** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Sam Martin** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Amelia Wilkes-Martin (Milly)** Heather Stoney

**Note:** *Anno Domino* was premiered during the Covid-19 theatre lockdown during 2020. Alan Ayckbourn recorded the play at his own home with he and his wife, Heather Stoney, playing all roles. The play was then premiered as an audio-stream by the Stephen Joseph Theatre.  
The Haydonning Ltd production was initially streamed exclusively on the SJT's website from 25 May - 2 July 2020.
