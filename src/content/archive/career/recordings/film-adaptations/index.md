This page contains basic details of the English language film adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn's plays. Click on a title below to find out more details about the adaptation.

**NOTE:** Most of these adaptations have not been made commercially available and are otherwise unavailable to obtain. Please do not contact the website or The Ayckbourn Archive about obtaining them as neither holds copies of the adaptations.

Alain ResnaisThe most significant of the film adaptations of Alan Ayckbourn’s plays are those directed by Alain Resnais - *Smoking / No Smoking*, *Private Fears In Public Places* and *Life Of Riley*. In 2015, *Positif* magazine published a tribute by Alan Ayckbourn to the French film auteur, which can be found [here](/career/recordings/film-adaptations/alain-resnais).
