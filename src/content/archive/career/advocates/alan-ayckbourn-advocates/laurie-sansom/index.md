*Laurie Sansom is a theatre director who has also been Artistic Director of the National Theatre of Scotland, the Royal & Derngate in Northampton and is currently the Artistic Director of the Northern Broadsides. He was Associate Director of the Stephen Joseph Theatre where he worked with Alan Ayckbourn.*

### Quotes

"Probably our greatest living playwright. This great man has meant so much to so many people in the British Theatre over the last 50 years."
