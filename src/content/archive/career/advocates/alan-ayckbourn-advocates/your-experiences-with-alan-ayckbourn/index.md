This website welcomes the chance to hear from anyone who has worked with Alan Ayckbourn over the decades. Our aim is to create a permanent resource of memories of people who have worked with Sir Alan from 1957 to the present day. All memories will be placed in the Alan Ayckbourn Archive with selected memories being reproduced on this website.

### How To Submit Your Experiences & Memories

If you would like to submit a memory, you can either do it via the **[Experiences / Memories Contact](/career/advocates/alan-ayckbourn-advocates/experiences-memories-contact-form)** page (click on the link in the left-hand column) or just contact Simon Murgatroyd at **[admin@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:admin@alanayckbourn.net)**. You can use this address just to send a memory of your experiences or use the following questions as a guide-line.

Please indicate in correspondence whether you are happy to have your memory reproduced on the website or would just like it held in archive.

### Example Questions

○ What is your first experience of Alan Ayckbourn or his plays and what are your memories of it?

○ What are your thoughts on working with Alan Ayckbourn?

○ What is your favourite memory of working with Alan Ayckbourn?

○ What do you feel Alan Ayckbourn has contributed to theatre?

If you have any queries, please contact Simon via [admin@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:admin@alanayckbourn.net).
