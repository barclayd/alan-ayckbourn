*Alan Strachan is a director who has produced a number of Alan Ayckbourn's plays to great acclaim and success in the West End. He has also worked with the playwright at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in Scarborough.*

### Quotes

"I think the great thing about Alan is his ability to write very funny plays but plays which also reveal and probe the English middle-class psyche, particularly the battleground which is English middle-class marriage. This is usually expressed in scenes of hysterical funniness for the audience but for extreme pain to the characters.”  
*(1995)*

"The content of Alan's work is surprisingly sharp, very shrewd, very tough and genuinely subversive.”
