*Jack Thomas was one of Alan Ayckbourn's tutors at Haileybury.*

### Quotes

"'He is a bright boy and should go far. I wish him every success.' I had no idea when I wrote those words in 1956 about a thin, nervously intelligent schoolboy whom I taught, just how far he would indeed go. True, I knew that he was clever for he had passed with flying colours at A-Level in English and History."  
*(1988)*
