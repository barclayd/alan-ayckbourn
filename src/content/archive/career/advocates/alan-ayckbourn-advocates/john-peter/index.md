*John Peter is a theatre critic and journalist who has worked extensively on The Times newspapers. As a critic he was responsible for reviewing a substantial number of Alan Ayckbourn's West End premieres for The Times.*

### Quotes

"Comedy ends where tragedy begins. Alan Ayckbourn is different. He hops across the border and capers around like a clown in a minefield while you in the audience hold your breath."
