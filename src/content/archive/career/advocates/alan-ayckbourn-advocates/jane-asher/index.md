*Jane Asher is an actress, broadcaster and author. She has worked with Alan Ayckbourn in the West End, notably the London premieres of Henceforward…, Things We Do For Love and House & Garden.*

### Quotes

"Rehearsing with Alan is always a day of fun and laughter after which you come out feeling you have actually learnt a lot. He is a man who teaches by nurturing people rather than putting them down."  
*(1991)*
