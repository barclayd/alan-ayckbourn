*Elizabeth Boag is a Yorkshire-born actress who has appeared in a number of Alan Ayckbourn's world premieres of Alan Ayckbourn's plays as well as the New York premieres of Arrivals & Departures, Confusions and Hero's Welcome.*

### Quotes

"The key to Alan’s plays is that he writes really good parts for men and women. He just writes people brilliantly and he gifts actors with real people to play."  
*(2013)*
