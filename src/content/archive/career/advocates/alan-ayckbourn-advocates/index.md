Over the decades, Alan Ayckbourn has worked with hundreds of actors and numerous other people working in the theatre industry. This section of the website aims to collect people's experiences of working with Alan Ayckbourn for posterity as well as to offer another perspective on the playwright and director.

If you are interested in submitting your thoughts and experiences about working with Alan Ayckbourn - no matter how old or recent, you can find out how by clicking [here](/career/advocates/alan-ayckbourn-advocates/your-experiences-with-alan-ayckbourn). It will added to the Ayckbourn Archive and, where possible, we will add it to this section of the website.
