## The Rainmaker (1957)

### Production Details

**Play:** The Rainmaker  
**Author:** N. Richard Nash  
**First performance:** 13 May 1957  
**Venue:** Leatherhead Theatre Club  
**Staging:** End-stage  
**Director:** Stafford Byrne

**Character** **Actor**  
**H.C. Curry** Richard Vernon  
**Noah Curry** George Cooper  
**Jim Curry** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Lizzie Curry** Phyllis Partridge  
**File** Derek Martinus  
**Sherriff Thomas** David Crosse  
**Bill Starbuck** Michael Allinson

### Quotes & Notes

"I did quite a lot of acting \[at the **[Leatherhead Theatre Club](/career/actor/connaught-theatre)**\]: Percy in *Flare Path*, that boy in *The Rainmaker*, Jimmy Curry, and the servant, Sanyamo, in *South Sea Bubble*. It was weekly rep again, but I worked with a lot of good people there \[at Leatherhead\]. Actors are quite generous people, really: they do tend to help. Sometimes it's terribly misguided help, but they're always there to say, 'If you take my tip, kid, don't enter that way, it doesn't look so good.' So I was learning."  
*(Conversations With Ayckbourn', 1981)**All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
