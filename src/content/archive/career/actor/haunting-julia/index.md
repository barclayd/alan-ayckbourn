### Quotes & Notes

Following the success of the world premiere of ***[Anno Domino](/career/actor/anno-domino)*** as an audio play earlier in the year, Alan Ayckbourn followed it up with a recording of his 1994 supernatural play ***[Haunting Julia](/plays/haunting-julia)*** for Christmas.  
The decision to create audio plays for both works being as a result of the Covid-19 pandemic during 2020 which closed all the UK's theatres. For *Haunting Julia*, Alan directed, produced, created the sound-design and recorded all the male voices with the final mix created by Paul Stear.  
*Haunting Julia* was produced by Haydonning Ltd and streamed for an exclusive period by the Stephen Joseph Theatre via its website.
