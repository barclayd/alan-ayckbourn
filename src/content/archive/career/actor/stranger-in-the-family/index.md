## Stranger In The Family (1961)

### Production Details

**Play:** Stranger In The Family  
**Author:** Norman Horner  
**First performance:** 27 July 1961  
**Final performance:** 23 August 1961  
**Venue:** The Library Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round  
**Director:** Terry Lane

**Character** **Actor**  
**Peggy Burton** Hazel Burt  
**Ken Burton** Stanley Page  
**Edna Burton** Rosamund Dickson  
**Toby Hart** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Dick Letts** David Jarrett

### Quotes & Notes

1961 saw Alan Ayckbourn's professional career began to move away from acting to what he is most well-known for. Although still predominantly an actor with Studio Theatre Ltd at **[Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/theatre-in-the-round-at-the-library-theatre)**, Scarborough, he was also writing and directing for the company. 1961 saw the premiere of his fourth play and his debut as a director with a production of ***[Gaslight](/career/directing-career/gaslight)***.

**Review extract from The Stage** (3 August 1961)  
"Alan Ayckbourn hovers on the edge of the play, contributing genial touches of sarcasm."*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
