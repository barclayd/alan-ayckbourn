This section offers an in-depth look at Alan Ayckbourn's many careers within professional theatre. Although primarily known as a playwright & director, Alan Ayckbourn's career in theatre has been wide-ranging and his position as Artistic Director of the Stephen Joseph Theatre is as crucial to understanding Ayckbourn as his playwriting & directing careers.

### Alan Ayckbourn: A Careers Timeline

**○ 1956:** **[Actor](/career/actor)** with **[Sir Donald Wolfit](/career/actor/donald-wolfit)**'s company  
**○ 1956:** **[Student Assistant Stage Manager](/career/stage-manager)** & **[Actor](/career/actor)** at **[Connaught Theatre](/career/actor/connaught-theatre)**, Worthing  
**○ 1957:** **[Stage Manager](/career/stage-manager)** & **[Actor](/career/actor)** at **[Leatherhead Theatre Club](/career/actor/connaught-theatre)**  
**○ 1957:** **[Stage Manager](/career/stage-manager)** & **[Actor](/career/actor)** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough  
**○ 1957:** **[Actor](/career/actor)** at the **[Oxford Playhouse](/career/actor/connaught-theatre)**  
**○ 1958:** **[Stage Manager](/career/stage-manager)** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough  
**○ 1958 - 1962:** **[Actor](/career/actor)** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough  
**○ 1959 - present:** **[Playwright](/career/playwright)**  
**○ 1961 - present:** **[Director](/career/directing-career)**  
**○ 1962 - 1964:** **[Director](/career/directing-career)**, **[writer](/career/playwright)** and **[actor](/career/actor)** at the **[Victoria Theatre](/career/victoria-theatre)**, Stoke-on-Trent  
**○ 1965 - 1970:** **[Radio Drama Producer](/career/bbc-career)** at the BBC  
**○ 1969 - 1970:** **[Director Of Productions](/career/artistic-director)** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough  
**○ 1969 - 1970:** Screenplay writer for ***[Hark At Barker](/career/recordings)*** on ITV (using the pseudonym Peter Caulfield)  
**○ 1972 - 2009:** **[Artistic Director](/career/artistic-director)** of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre (later the Stephen Joseph Theatre)  
**○ 1986 - 1988:** **[Company Director](/career/national-theatre)** at the National Theatre, London  
**○ 1992:** Cameron Mackintosh **[Professor of Contemporary Theatre](/career/st-catherines-college)** at Oxford University  
**○ 2009 - present:** Guest **[Director](/career/directing-career)** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre and other venues  
**○ 2018 - present:** **[Director Emeritus](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/director-emeritus-sjt)** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre  
**○ 2019:** Author of ***[The Divide](/plays/the-divide)*** - his first prose novel
