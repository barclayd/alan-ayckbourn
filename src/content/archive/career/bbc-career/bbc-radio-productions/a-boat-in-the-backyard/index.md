*Between 1965 and 1970, Alan Ayckbourn was employed as a Radio Drama Producer by the BBC. He was responsible for directing dozens of works for the radio, sadly details of the majority of which have been lost. These pages contain details for specific productions where known.*

### Production Details

**Author:** David Bramley  
**Broadcast:** 20 September 1969 @ 2pm  
**Channel:** BBC Radio 4

**Director:**  
Alan Ayckbourn

**Character** **Actor**  
**Arthur** Wilfred Pickles  
**Cora** Fiona Walker  
**Harry** Brian Peck  
**Jack Dandy** Sam Kelly

### Notes

○ *A Boat In The Backyard* was broadcast in the *Afternoon Theatre* slot.  
○ *A Boat In The Backyard* received its theatrical world premiere at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1968; the Library Theatre being Alan Ayckbourn's home theatre.
