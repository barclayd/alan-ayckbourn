*Between 1965 and 1970, Alan Ayckbourn was employed as a Radio Drama Producer by the BBC. He was responsible for directing dozens of works for the radio, sadly details of the majority of which have been lost. These pages contain details for specific productions where known.*

### Production Details

**Author:** Pam Tickell  
**Broadcast:** 17 April 1966  
**Channel:** North of England Home Service

**Director:**  
Alan Ayckbourn

**Character** **Actor**  
**Violet** Elizabeth Ashton  
**Pauline** Helen Fraser  
**Mr Helliwell** David Mahlowe  
**Bert** Harry Markham

### Notes

○ *Them And Us* was repeated on the BBC Home Service on 5 April 1967 as part of a series called *Five From The North* highlighting northern writers.
