*Between 1965 and 1970, Alan Ayckbourn was employed as a Radio Drama Producer by the BBC. He was responsible for directing dozens of works for the radio, sadly details of the majority of which have been lost. These pages contain details for specific productions where known.*

### Production Details

**Author:** John Pennington  
**Broadcast:** 27 January 1965 @ 3pm  
**Channel:** BBC Home Service

**Director:**  
Alan Ayckbourn

**Character** **Actor**  
**Syd** Arthur Lowe  
**Maureen** Helen Fraser  
**Arnold** Brian Peck  
**Charlie** Colin Edwynn  
**First railwayman** Harry Markham  
**Second railwayman** Peter Schofield  
**Mrs Fletch** Mary Quinn  
**Depot Superintendent** Robert Wallace
