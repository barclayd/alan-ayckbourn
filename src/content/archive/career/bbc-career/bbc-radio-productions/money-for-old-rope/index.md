*Between 1965 and 1970, Alan Ayckbourn was employed as a Radio Drama Producer by the BBC. He was responsible for directing dozens of works for the radio, sadly details of the majority of which have been lost. These pages contain details for specific productions where known.*

### Production Details

**Author:** David Ellis  
**Broadcast:** 22 May 1967 @ 7.30pm  
**Channel:** BBC Light Programme

**Director:**  
Alan Ayckbourn

**Character** **Actor**  
**Carol** Heather Stoney  
**Gordon Marsh** Anthony Kenyon  
**Stanley Price** Colin Edwynn  
**Sam Ogden** Roy Barraclough  
**Det-Con Evans** Paul Webster  
**Mabel Yardley** Ruth Holden  
**Det-Sgt Crosby** Peter Schofield  
**Harry** Graham Rigby

### Notes

○ *Money For Old Rope* was broadcast as part of a series of plays called *Personal Column*.
