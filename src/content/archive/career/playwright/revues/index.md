This page includes all of Alan Ayckbourn's revues and musical entertainments. To find out more details about each revue, click on the revue title.

Alan Ayckbourn has written 10 musical revues, the first 9 with the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round's Musical Director and composer Paul Todd. He then returned to the form more than a decade later in a collaboration with the composer Denis King.

Of all Alan Ayckbourn's revues, only ***[Me, Myself and I](/plays/me-myself-and-i)*** has been published although all the revues are available to produce (contact **[Casarotto Ramsay](http://www.casarotto.co.uk/clients/sir-alan-ayckbourn/)** for details).

### The Revues

○ ***[Men On Women On Men](/plays/men-on-women-on-men)*** (1978): A revue - originally performed in two parts, later combined - written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[First Course](/plays/first-course)*** (1980): One part of an unrelated duology - intended to be performed separately - written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[Second Helping](/plays/second-helping)*** (1980): One part of an unrelated duology - intended to be performed separately - written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[Me, Myself & I](/plays/me-myself-and-i)*** (1981): A revue - originally performed in three parts, later combined - written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[Incidental Music](/plays/incidental-music)*** (1983): A revue written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[The 7 Deadly Virtues](/plays/the-7-deadly-virtues)*** (1984): A revue - with seven substantial narrative songs - written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[Boy Meets Girl](/plays/boy-meets-girl)*** (1985): One part of a duology - intended to be performed separately - written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[Girl Meets Boy](/plays/boy-meets-girl)*** (1985): One part of a duology - intended to be performed separately - written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[Mere Soup Songs](/plays/mere-soup-songs)*** (1985): A revue written with Paul Todd.  
○ ***[Cheap And Cheerful](/plays/cheap-and-cheerful)*** (1998): A revue inspired by seaside end-of-the-pier shows written with Denis King.
