*These pages offer a number of downloadable resources drawn from the work of Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd M.A. and the Ayckbourn Archive.*

*These resources are free to use and reproduce for* ***non-commercial*** *purposes providing they are credited to the author, Simon Murgatroyd, and this website www.alanayckbourn.net.*

### Alan Ayckbourn Resources

Click on a title below or in the right-hand column to download the specific resource.

**◦ [Alan Ayckbourn: A Biography (2026)](http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/resources/Biography---Definitive.pdf)**  
A short official biography about Alan Ayckbourn, suitable for reproduction in programmes and commentaries.

**◦ [The Plays & Other Works Index (2026)](http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/resources/Play-Index---Complete.pdf)**  
A complete index to all Alan Ayckbourn full-length plays as well as his other plays and written works.

**◦ [Alan Ayckbourn: A Timeline](http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/resources/Timeline-%C2%A9-Haydonning-Ltd.pdf)**  
A timeline of key events in Alan Ayckbourn's lifer from 1939 to the present day including play premieres.

**◦ [Alan Ayckbourn: 20 Facts (2026)](http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/resources/20-Facts---Definitive.pdf)**  
A guider to Alan Ayckbourn's life and career in 20 facts.

**◦ [Alan Ayckbourn: The Playwrigh](http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/resources/Playwriting.pdf)t**  
A biography of Alan Ayckbourn's career as a playwright.

**◦ [Playwriting: 20 Facts](http://interviews.alanayckbourn.net/resources/20-Facts---Playwriting.pdf)**  
A guide to Alan Ayckbourn's playwriting career in 20 facts.
