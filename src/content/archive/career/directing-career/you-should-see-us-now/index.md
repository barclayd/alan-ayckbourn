### Notes

○ The success of *You Should See Us Now* led to Peter Tinniswood writing a sequel in 1983 - again directed by Alan Ayckbourn and premiered at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round - called ***[At The End Of The Day](/career/directing-career/at-the-end-of-the-day)***.
