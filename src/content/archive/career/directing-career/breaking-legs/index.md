## Breaking Legs (1991)

### Production Details

**Author:** Tom Dulack  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round  
**Location:** Studio  
**Staging:** End-stage

**First performance:** 19 July 1991  
**Opening night:** 26 July 1991  
**Final performance:** 21 September 1991

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn **Character** **Actor**  
**Lighting:** Jackie Staines **Terence O'Keefe** Peter Bourke  
**Design:** Juliet Nichols **Lou Graziano** Jeffrey Chiswick  
**Dialect Coach:** Andrew Jack **Angie** Susan Denaker  
**Stage Manager:** Jane Eliot-Webb **Mike Fransisco** Glyn Grain  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Erica Jane Fletcher **Tino de Felice** Robert Austin  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Kevin Riley **Frankie Salvucci** Barry McCarthy

### Notes

○ *Breaking Legs* was a late-night show performed in the Studio at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round following the main-house production.  
○ This production marked the British premiere of *Breaking Legs*.
