## The Mating Season (1963)

### Production Details

**Author:** Alan Plater  
**New Play:** Yes  
**Venue:** Victoria Theatre, Stoke-on-Trent  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 21 January 1963  
**Opening night:** 21 January 1963  
**Final performance:** To be confirmed, 1963

### Company Details

**Director:**  
Alan Ayckbourn

**Character** **Actor**  
**Stan** Stanley Page  
**Mr Hargreaves** David Halliwell  
**Mrs Hargreaves** Elizabeth Bell  
**Jack** Peter King  
**Doris** Caroline Smith  
**Eileen** Heather Stoney  
**Barman** Arnold Beck

### Notes

○ *The Mating Game* was originally written for and produced on radio and this marked the first stage production of the play.  
○ *The Mating Season* was presented in a double bill with ***[The Referees](/career/directing-career/the-referees)***.
