**New Play:** No

### Notes

○ *Rookery Nook'*s author Ben Travers - aged 92 - visited Scarborough to see this production of his play, marking the first time he had met the playwright and the venue's Artistic Director Alan Ayckbourn.  
○ *Rookery Nook* marked the first time the company had produced a play by Ben Travers.
