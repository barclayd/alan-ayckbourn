### Production Details

**Author:** Leon Katz  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** The Library Theatre  
**Location:** Concert Room, Scarborough Library  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 15 June 1970  
**Opening night:** 15 June 1970  
**Final performance:** 12 August 1970

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Stage Manager:** Philip Randell  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Graham Phoenix

### Notes

○ *Wife-Swapping: Italian Style* was loosely based on the anonymous Commedia dell'Arte scenario *The Three Cuckolds*.  
○ The play was toured to Hull Prison; the actor Richard Derrington has very vivid memories of performing in pink tights at the show!
