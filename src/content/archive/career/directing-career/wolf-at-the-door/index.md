## Wolf At The Door (1989)

### Production Details

**Author:** Henry Becque  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round  
**Location:** Main house (Round)  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 27 September 1989  
**Opening night:** 3 October 1989  
**Final performance:** 21 October 1989
