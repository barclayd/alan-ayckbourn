## They're Playing Our Song (1997)

### Production Details

**Book:** Neil Simon  
**Music:** Marvin Hamlisch  
**Lyrics:** Carole Bayer Sager  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre  
**Location:** The Round  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 21 May 1997  
**Opening night:** 27 May 1997  
**Final performance:** 5 July 1997

### Notes

○ *They’re Playing Our Song* marked the debut of the actress Tamzin Outhwaite with the Scarborough company. She would go on to appear in *Just A Song At Lunchtime*, *Baby On Board* and *Absent Friends* before finding fame on television in the BBC soap opera *EastEnders* and numerous other high profile television and stage roles.
