## The Caretaker (1976)

### Production Details

**Author:** Harold Pinter  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round  
**Location:** Main house (Round)  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 23 November 1976  
**Opening night:** 24 November 1976  
**Final performance:** 11 December 1976

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn **Character** **Actor**  
**Stage Manager:** David Millard **Mick** Robin Herford  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Christopher Gray **Aston** Bob Eaton  
**Asst. Stage Manager:** Wendy Murray **Davies** Stanley Page

### Notes

○ Following the success of a production of Arthur Miller's *A View From The Bridge* in 1975 at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, *The Caretaker* was put in the schedule as a result of it being on the National Curriculum and being studied by schools in the Scarborough area.  
○ ***[The Caretaker](/career/directing-career/the-caretaker-1962)*** had previously been performed by the company in 1962 at the Library Theatre - again directed by Alan Ayckbourn - and was the first professional production of a Pinter play to be staged in Scarborough.  
○ The cast featured Stanley Page as Davies who had also played the same role in the 1962 production at the Library Theatre.
