### Notes

○ Following the success of a production of Arthur Miller's *A View From The Bridge* in 1975 at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, *The Caretaker* was put in the schedule as a result of it being on the National Curriculum and being studied by schools in the Scarborough area.  
○ ***[The Caretaker](/career/directing-career/the-caretaker-1962)*** had previously been performed by the company in 1962 at the Library Theatre - again directed by Alan Ayckbourn - and was the first professional production of a Pinter play to be staged in Scarborough.  
○ The cast featured Stanley Page as Davies who had also played the same role in the 1962 production at the Library Theatre.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd. Image copyright: Scarborough Theatre Trust.*
