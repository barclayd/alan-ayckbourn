## The Caretaker (1962)

### Production Details

**Author:** Harold Pinter  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** The Library Theatre  
**Location:** Concert Room, Scarborough Library  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 16 August 1962  
**Opening night:** 16 August 1962  
**Final performance:** 22 August 1962

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn **Character** **Actor**  
**Mick** Peter King  
**Stage Manager:** Arnold Beck **Aston** Richard Gill  
**Stage Manager:** David Wehner **Davies** Stanley Page

### Notes

○ *The Caretaker* marked the second production of a Pinter play by the Studio Theatre company. The first, *The Birthday Party*, was actually directed by Harold Pinter himself in 1958 and was only the second professional production of the play following its original disastrous London production. *The Birthday Party* was only performed on tour by the company though, so *The Caretaker* marked the first time a Pinter play had been professionally produced and seen in Scarborough.
