### Notes

○ *The Caretaker* marked the second production of a Pinter play by the Studio Theatre company. The first, *The Birthday Party*, was actually directed by Harold Pinter himself in 1958 and was only the second professional production of the play following its original disastrous London production. *The Birthday Party* was only performed on tour by the company though, so *The Caretaker* marked the first time a Pinter play had been professionally produced and seen in Scarborough.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
