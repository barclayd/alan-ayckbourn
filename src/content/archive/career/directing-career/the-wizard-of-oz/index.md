### Production Details

**Author:** Alfred Bradley  
**New Play:** Yes  
**Venue:** York Theatre Royal  
**Staging:** End-stage

**First performance:** 8 August 1968  
**Opening night:** 8 August 1968  
**Final performance:** To be confirmed, 1968

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Helen Wilkinson  
**Music:** Trevor Holroyd  
**Music:** Barrie Davenport

**Character** **Actor**  
**Dorothy** Jean Rogers  
**Aunt Em** Vicky Ireland  
**Uncle Henry** Nigel Forde

**Scarecrow** Christopher Godwin  
**Wicked Witch Of The West** Vicky Ireland  
**Witch's Cat** Mark Rose  
**Tinman** Stephen Holden  
**Cowardly Lion** Ronald Herdman  
**Guardian Of The Gate** Nigel Forde  
**Wizard Of Oz** Danny Schiller  
**Good Witch Of The South** Jenny Austen

### Notes

○ This was a new adaptation of Frank L Baum's famed novel.  
○ It marks the only time Alan Ayckbourn has directed at the Theatre Royal York.  
○ The company included Vicky Ireland, who would go on to found the pioneering children's theatre company, Polka Theatre.  
○ The production was performed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday afternoons throughout the summer school holidays.
