### Notes

○ This was a new adaptation of Frank L Baum's famed novel.  
○ It marks the only time Alan Ayckbourn has directed at the Theatre Royal York.  
○ The company included Vicky Ireland, who would go on to found the pioneering children's theatre company, Polka Theatre.  
○ The production was performed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday afternoons throughout the summer school holidays.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
