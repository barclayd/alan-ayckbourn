### Notes

○ *The Conservatory* was recorded by the BBC and broadcast in *The Monday Play* slot on 2 November 1981 on BBC Radio 4. The production was directed by Alan Ayckbourn and produced by Alfred Bradley with the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round company performing the roles from the summer 1981 production.
