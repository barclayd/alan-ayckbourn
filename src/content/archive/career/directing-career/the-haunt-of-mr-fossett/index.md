**New Play:** Yes

### Notes

○ *The Haunt Of Mr Fossett* was a lunchtime production performed in the Studio at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round.  
○ The production was originally scheduled to open on 7 June 1988 at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round. However, the opening was pushed back a week after a bereavement for one of the actors on 6 June.  
○ Very few details are held in archive about this play and the majority of details were obtained from the press reviews held in archive.
