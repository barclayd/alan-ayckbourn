## The Safari Party (2003)

### Production Details

**Author:** Tim Firth  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** Hampstead Theatre  
**Staging:** End-stage

**First performance:** 5 March 2003  
**Opening night:** 5 March 2003  
**Final performance:** To be confirmed 2003

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Michael Holt  
**Lighting:** Kath Geraghty  
**Stage Manager:** Unknown

**Character** **Actor**  
**Daniel** Daniel Casey  
**Adam** Daniel Crowder  
**Esther** Christine Moore  
**Lol** John Branwell  
**Bridget** Amanda Abbington  
**Inga** Helen Ryan

### Notes

○ This production opened the newly renovated Hampstead Theatre and although it featured much of the same cast as the original **[Stephen Joseph Theatre production](/career/directing-career/the-safari-party)** from 2002, it is not considered a transfer.  
○ It was the last production by an author other than himself to be directed by Alan Ayckbourn.
