## The Ballroom (1988)

### Production Details

**Author:** Peter King  
**New Play:** Yes  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round  
**Location:** Main house (Round)  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 14 September 1988  
**Opening night:** 14 September 1988  
**Final performance:** 22 October 1988

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn **Character** **Actor**  
**Design:** Laura McLaughlin **Lil Powell** Lesley Meade  
**Choreographer:** Angela Hardcastle **Jack Powell, her husband** Graeme Eton

**Stage Manager:** Vaughn Curtis **Babs, her cousin** Jenny Funnell

### Notes

○ *The Ballroom* necessitated all the cast had to learn how to ballroom dance for the production. The image shows the company during practise at Scarborough Spa's Ocean Room.  
○ An ‘orchestra’ was featured on stage, consisting of Musical Director John Pattison and five papier-mâché puppeteered figures. John would sit in place, unmoving, when audiences entered the auditorium giving the impression he was one of the puppets.
