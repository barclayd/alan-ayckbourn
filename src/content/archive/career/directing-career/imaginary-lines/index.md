### Notes

○ *Imaginary Lines* was written by the actor / playwright Reggie Oliver who had previously appeared in the company’s production of ***[His Monkey Wife](/career/directing-career/his-monkey-wife)***. Director Alan Ayckbourn described the play at the time as “simply one of the best comedies I’ve read for ages.”
