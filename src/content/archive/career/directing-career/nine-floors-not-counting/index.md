### Notes

○ No details are known about this production other than it being reported as taking place in a programme for ***[The Birds & The Wellwishers](/career/actor/the-birds-and-the-wellwishers)*** at the Victoria Theatre in October 1962. However, the play was also presented as part of a double-bill during the summer of 1962 at the Library Theatre, Scarborough. Although it was advertised as being directed by Peter Cheeseman, it was a late addition to the schedule and it seems possible this was a revival of the production Alan probably directed in London earlier in the year. As a result, the actors (\*) are listed as those who performed it in Scarborough - it has not been confirmed whether they also performed it in London.
