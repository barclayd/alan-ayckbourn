### Notes

○ This was the final production to be directed by Alan Ayckbourn during his two year tenure as a Company Director for the National Theatre. Not originally scheduled, Alan was offered the chance to direct a fourth play following the move of his acclaimed production of *A View From The Bridge* into the West End from the NT.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.
