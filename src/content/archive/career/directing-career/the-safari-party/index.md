### Notes

○ *The Safari Party* marked the final time Alan Ayckbourn would direct a play by an author other than himself at the Stephen Joseph Theatre. Since 2002, he has only directed the world premieres and revivals of his own plays at the venue.  
○ *The Safari Party* was revived the following year for the opening of the renovated Hampstead Theatre. Although Alan Ayckbourn directed it and it featured the same company, the **[revival](/career/directing-career/the-safari-party-2003)** was not considered a transfer or a Stephen Joseph Theatre production.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.
