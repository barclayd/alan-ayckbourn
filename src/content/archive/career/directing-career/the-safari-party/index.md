## The Safari Party (2002)

### Production Details

**Author:** Tim Firth  
**New Play:** Yes  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre  
**Location:** The Round  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 25 April 2002  
**Opening night:** 30 April 2002  
**Final performance:** 18 May 2002

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Michael Holt  
**Lighting:** Kath Geraghty  
**Stage Manager:** Unknown

**Character** **Actor**  
**Daniel** Daniel Casey  
**Adam** Daniel Crowder  
**Esther** Christine Moore  
**Lol** John Branwell  
**Bridget** Amanda Abbington  
**Inga** Helen Ryan

### Notes

○ *The Safari Party* marked the final time Alan Ayckbourn would direct a play by an author other than himself at the Stephen Joseph Theatre. Since 2002, he has only directed the world premieres and revivals of his own plays at the venue.  
○ *The Safari Party* was revived the following year for the opening of the renovated Hampstead Theatre. Although Alan Ayckbourn directed it and it featured the same company, the **[revival](/career/directing-career/the-safari-party-2003)** was not considered a transfer or a Stephen Joseph Theatre production.
