### Notes

○ *The Boys And The Girls* was a revue featuring of 14 sketches by David Campton charting life from the very young to the very old.  
○ David Campton was a participant in one of Stephen Joseph's playwriting courses held at the Central School Of Speech And Drama, London, prior to the Library Theatre opening. He would become the Library Theatre's first writer-in-residence.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
