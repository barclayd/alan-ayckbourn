### Notes

○ *Later Life* was part of the 10x10 season presented at the Stephen Joseph Theatre during the summer of 1998. It featured 10 plays receiving either their world or British premieres in repertory performed by a company of 10 actors.  
○ This marked the British premiere of the play which was originally produced at the Westside Theatre, off Broadway, in 1993.  
○ *Later Life* was one of only two plays presented during the 10x10 season to not be a world premiere production alongside *Sweet Phoebe*.  
○ This was a late-night show presented in The McCarthy at the Stephen Joseph Theatre.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
