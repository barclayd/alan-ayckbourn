## Fallen Angels (1977)

### Production Details

**Author:** Noël Coward  
**New Play:** No  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round  
**Location:** Main house (Round)  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 28 March 1977  
**Opening night:** 29 March 1977  
**Final performance:** 10 August 1977
