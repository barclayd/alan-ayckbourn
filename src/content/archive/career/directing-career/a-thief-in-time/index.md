### Production Details

**Author:** Joan Macalpine  
**New Play:** Yes  
**Venue:** The Library Theatre  
**Location:** Concert Room, Scarborough Library  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 12 June 1962  
**Opening night:** 12 June 1962  
**Final performance:** 12 September 1962

### Company Details

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Stage Manager:** Arnold Beck  
**Stage Manager:** David Wehner

**Character** **Actor**  
**Mr Timmins** Stanley Page

**Dr Elyot** Richard Gill  
**Sarah Wilson** Elizabeth Bell  
**Squintle** David Wehner  
**Policeman** Arnold Beck

### Notes

○ *A Thief In Time* was Joan Macalpine’s first original (non-adaptation) play to be professionally produced.  
○ Further illustrating Stephen Joseph’s desire to encourage talent within the Scarborough company, Joan had initially been employed by Studio Theatre Ltd as a stage-manager before she turned her hand to writing.  
○ The image illustrating this page is a publicity shot for *A Thief In Time* taken on the roof of Scarborough Library and featuring Stanley Page and Peter King.
