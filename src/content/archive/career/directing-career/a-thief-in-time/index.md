### Notes

○ *A Thief In Time* was Joan Macalpine’s first original (non-adaptation) play to be professionally produced.  
○ Further illustrating Stephen Joseph’s desire to encourage talent within the Scarborough company, Joan had initially been employed by Studio Theatre Ltd as a stage-manager before she turned her hand to writing.
