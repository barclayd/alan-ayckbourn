## Mother Country (1976)

### Production Details

**Author:** Stephen Mallatratt  
**New Play:** Yes  
**Venue:** The Library Theatre  
**Location:** Concert Room, Scarborough Library  
**Staging:** Round

**First performance:** 25 August 1976  
**Opening night:** 25 August 1976  
**Final performance:** 8 September 1976
