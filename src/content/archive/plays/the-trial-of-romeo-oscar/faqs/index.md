*Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's The Trial of Romeo Oscar. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the* ***[Contact Us](/contact)*** *page.*

**When can I see *The Trial of Romeo Oscar*?**  
*The Trial of Romeo Oscar* will premiere on 4 September 2026 at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, and run until 3 October before embarking on a short tour during October to The Victoria Wood Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere, and the New Vic, Newcastle-under-Lyme.

**When will *The Trial of Romeo Oscar* be published?**  
There is no information regarding this yet, but judging by recent publications of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, it will probably be published during 2028.

**Will *The Trial of Romeo Oscar* be filmed and streamed?**  
No, the Stephen Joseph Theatre made the decision in 2023 to no longer record nor stream Alan Ayckbourn's productions.
