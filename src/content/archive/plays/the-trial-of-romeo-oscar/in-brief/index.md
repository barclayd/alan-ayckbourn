### The Trial of Oscar Romeo

**Play Number:** 92  
**World Premiere:** 8 September 2026  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre

**Premiere Staging:** In-the-round

**Published:** No  
**Other Media:** No

**Cast:** 7 (3m / 4f)  
**Run Time:** To be confirmed

**Synopsis:** In the future, the first trial of an android is taking place. The circumstances surrounding the incident are unclear, the evidence is polarising, and selecting an impartial jury is proving a challenge. It will raise the ultimate question, what makes us human?

</aside>

- *The Trial of Romeo Oscar* is Alan Ayckbourn's 92nd play.
- The world premiere will take place at the Stephen Joseph Theatre on 8 September 2026.
- The play is set in 2194 and marks the first time Alan Ayckbourn has written a court-room drama.
- It continues his exploration of the themes of artificial intelligence and what it is that makes us human.
