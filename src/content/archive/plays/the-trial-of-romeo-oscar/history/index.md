*The Trial of Romeo Oscar* is Alan Ayckbourn's 92nd play and will premiere in September 2026 at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough.

The play is set in 2194 and is a courtroom drama surrounding the first trial against an android. Apart from being the first courtroom-set play written by Alan Ayckbourn, it also continues his exploration of the themes of artificial intelligence that he has previously visited in plays such as ***[Constant Companions](/plays/constant-companions)*** and ***[Henceforward…](/plays/henceforward)***.
