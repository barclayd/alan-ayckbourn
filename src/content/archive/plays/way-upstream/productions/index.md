*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Way Upstream, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions of Way Upstream given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*
