*Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd's answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's My Sister Sadie. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the* ***[Contact Us](/)*** *page.*

**I'm having difficulty obtaining *My Sister Sadie*, is it available for production?**  
Yes, *My Sister Sadie* is available for both professional and amateur performance. Enquiries regarding the license and performance scripts should be directed to **[Concord Theatricals](http://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/)** (amateurs) and **[Casarotto Ramsay](http://www.casarotto.co.uk/client/sir-alan-ayckbourn-11716/)** (professional).
