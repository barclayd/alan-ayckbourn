*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Henceforward..., such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1987)

**First performance:** 30 July 1987  
**World premiere:** 30 July 1987  
**Final performance:** 16 January 1988  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Thomas  
**Music:** Paul Todd  
**Synclavier Programming:** Yasmin Hashmi

**Stage Manager:** Duncan Lewis  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** David Parker  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** David O’Neill  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Braco Dawson  
**Assistant Stage manager:** Kate Haswell

**Character** **Actor**  
**Jerome** Barry McCarthy  
**Lupus** Robin Herford  
**Zoë** Serena Evans  
**Geain, aged 9** Victoria Horsfield  
**Corinna** Penny Bunton  
**Mervyn** Michael Roberts  
**Geain, aged 13** Emma Chambers  
**NAN 300F** Herself

**Note:** *Henceforward…* was initially advertised to close on 31 October 1987. However, the play was so popular, it was kept in repertory through the winter season at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, eventually closing on 16 January 1988. It would then be revived during the summer of 1988 at the venue.
