*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Henceforward...; it only lists major productions and does not include the vast majority of the many professional and amateur productions of the play.*

**March 1987**  
Alan Ayckbourn writes *Henceforward...*. The initial complete draft is 'apparently' destroyed to be replaced a week later with a slightly altered final draft.

**30 July 1987**  
*Henceforward...* world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**8 October 1987**  
*Henceforward...* opens at the Alley Theatre, Houston, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**6 January 1988**  
*Henceforward...* is revived at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, prior to touring, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**20 January 1988**  
*Henceforward...* begins a tour at the Lyceum Theatre, Crewe, directed by Alan Ayckbourn. The tour visits venues in the UK, West Germany, Poland, Egypt and Turkey.

**8 June 1988**  
*Henceforward...* is revived at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**10 October 1988**  
Faber & Faber publishes *Henceforward...*.

**21 November 1988**  
*Henceforward...* opens at the Vaudeville Theatre, London, directed by Alan Ayckbourn and starring Ian McKellen as Jerome and Jane Asher as Corinna.

**February 1989**  
*Henceforward...* recast at the Vaudeville Theatre, London, with Martin Jarvis taking over the role of Jerome and Joanna van Gyseghem as Corinna.

**16 September 1989**  
*Henceforward...* closes at the Vaudeville Theatre, London.

**September 1989**  
Samuel French publishes the acting edition of *Henceforward…*.

**November 1989**  
*Henceforward...* receives the Evening Standard Award for Best Comedy.

**1990**  
*Henceforward…* post West End UK tour is launched, directed by John David and starring Simon Ward as Jerome.

**3 November 1991**  
*Henceforward…* opens at the Mark Taper Forum, Los Angeles, directed by Tom Moore and starring John Glover as Jerome.

**C. Early 1990s**  
Emmy nominated producer Dan Guntzelman writes a treatment for a television adaptation of *Henceforward…* which is considered but declined by Paramount Pictures.

**1992**  
*Henceforward...* receives the Drama-Logue Critic Award (Los Angeles).

**17 July 1995**  
Faber & Faber publishes *Alan Ayckbourn: Plays One*; a collection of Ayckbourn plays including *Henceforward...*.

**1 January 2011**  
*Henceforward...* is broadcast by LA Theatre Works, directed by Martin Jarvis.

**10 January 2012**  
The BBC marks the 25th anniversary of *Henceforward...* with a new production for BBC Radio 3, directed by Martin Jarvis.

**28 February 2015**  
*Henceforward…* is revived with an acclaimed and popular major production at the Schauspielhaus in Hamburg. The success of the production, which runs for more than a year in repertory, leads Alan Ayckbourn to revive it in 2016.

**8 September 2016**  
*Henceforward…* is revived at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, with Alan Ayckbourn directing the play for the first time since its West End premiere in 1988.

**19 January 2017**  
*Henceforward…*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, begins a UK end stage tour at the Yvonne Arnaud Theatre, Guildford.
