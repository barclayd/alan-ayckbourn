This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Henceforward…*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough (1987), held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd' (references: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/17](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005452/).**

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1987) and revival (1988) at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough; brochures for the 1987 and 1988 winter seasons at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough.

**V&A:** Annotated rehearsal manuscript by Ian McKellen for the West End premiere at the Vaudeville Theatre, London, in 1988. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*

*If you have any research enquiries regarding Henceforward…, please contact Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd at* ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***.  [![](./_images/web-175-dpi.jpg)](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)
