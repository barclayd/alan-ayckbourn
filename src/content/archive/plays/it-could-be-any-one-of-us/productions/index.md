*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's It Could Be Any One Of Us, such as the world premiere as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1983)

**First performance:** 5 October 1983  
**World premiere:** 5 October 1983  
**Final performance:** 7 January 1984  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Edward Lipscomb  
**Lighting:** Francis Lynch  
**Music:** Paul Todd
