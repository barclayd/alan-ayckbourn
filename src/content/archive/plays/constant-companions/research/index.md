This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Constant Companions.* The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

### Archivist's Note

Archival material relating to Alan Ayckbourn's plays written post- 2019 have not yet been passed to the Borthwick Institute for Archives to be accessioned into the Ayckbourn Archive.

In the interim, any research enquiries regarding these plays should be directed to Alan Ayckbourn's archivist, Simon Murgatroyd M.A., who can be contacted by emailing ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*

*If you have any research enquiries regarding Constant Companions, please contact Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd at* ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***.  [![](./_images/web-175-dpi.jpg)](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)
