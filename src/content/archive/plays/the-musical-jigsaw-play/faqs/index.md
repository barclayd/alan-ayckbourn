Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd's answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's *The Musical Jigsaw Play*. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the **[Contact Us](http://www.alanayckbourn.net/contact-form/Contacts.php)** page.

**Is *The Musical Jigsaw Play* available to produce?**  
No. During 2020, the playwright confirmed *The Musical Jigsaw Play* is now considered a withdrawn work and not available to produce.

**Where can I read *The Musical Jigsaw Play*?**  
A manuscript of *The Musical Jigsaw Play* is held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. The play can be read in situ there..
