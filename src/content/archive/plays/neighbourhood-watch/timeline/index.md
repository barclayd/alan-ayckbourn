*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Neighbourhood Watch; it only lists major productions and does not include the vast majority of the many professional and amateur productions of the play.*

**Summer 2010**  
Alan Ayckbourn confirms the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, has commissioned him to write his 75th play for 2011.

**October 2010**  
Alan Ayckbourn begins writing *Neighbourhood Watch*.

**Mid October 2010**  
Having completed the first act of *Neighbourhood Watch*, Alan Ayckbourn is unhappy with the result, deletes it and rewrites the entire act.

**Circa 5 November 2010**  
Alan Ayckbourn finishes writing *Neighbourhood Watch*.

**6 - 11 August 2011**  
A series of riots take place in major UK cities marking a huge civil disturbance. Parallels are later drawn between the riots and *Neighbourhood Watch*.

**13 September 2011**  
*Neighbourhood Watch*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, premieres at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough.

**24 November 2011**  
It is announced *Neighbourhood Watch* will have a month long residency at the Tricycle Theatre, London, in April 2012. This marks the first time Alan Ayckbourn has allowed his latest play to be presented in London since *Private Fears In Public Places* at the Orange Tree in 2005.

**30 November 2011**  
*Neighbourhood Watch*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, transfers to the 59E59 Theaters, New York, as part of the *Brits Off Broadway* festival for a limited season.

**1 January 2012**  
*Neighbourhood Watch* closes at the 59E59 Theaters, New York.

**18 January 2012**  
*Neighbourhood Watch*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, begins an end-stage UK tour at the Yvonne Arnaud Theatre, Guildford.

**11 April 2012**  
London premiere of *Neighbourhood Watch*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, at the Tricycle Theatre, London, for a limited season.

**5 May 2012**  
*Neighbourhood Watch* closes at the Tricycle Theatre, London.

**June 2013**  
*Neighbourhood Watch* released for professional and amateur production.

**December 2013**  
*Neighbourhood Watch* is published by Samuel French Ltd.

**2 August 2018**  
*Neighbourhood Watch* is published by Faber as part of *Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 6*.
