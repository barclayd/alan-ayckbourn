This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Season's Greetings*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The National Theatre Archive:** The National Theatre Archive holds a substantive collection of material pertaining to its 2010 production of *Season's Greetings*, directed by Marianne Elliott. This includes the NT Live broadcast recording. The record page can be found **[here](http://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx)**.

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough (1980), held in The Modern Playscripts Collection.

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programmes for world premiere (1980) and revival (1981) at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough; brochures for the 1980 / 1981 winter and 1981 summer seasons at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough.

**Scarborough Museums & Galleries:** Original three-act manuscript for the world premiere production at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough (1980). One of only three in existence and held in the Ayckbourn Collection.

**V&A:** Production file for the London premiere at The Round House (1980).
