*Very occasionally, Alan Ayckbourn's plays are published with errors in the text. This page has corrections for the published editions of Snake In The Grass.*

### Snake In The Grass (Samuel French Ltd, 2003) & Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 3 (Faber & Faber, 2005)

Samuel French edition: Act I, Scene II, page 28  
Faber edition: Act I, Scene II, page 51

**As printed:  
Miriam:** I will

*Annabel sits, a little apart from the summer house, hardly noticing Miriam. She breathes deeply, taking a moment to recover from her spasm. Miriam is meanwhile folding away the table and the garden chair to clear the trapdoor.*

I mean, what was the point of drugging the woman….

**Correct Version (correction in bold italics):  
Miriam:** I will

*Annabel sits, a little apart from the summer house, hardly noticing Miriam. She breathes deeply, taking a moment to recover from her spasm. Miriam is meanwhile folding away the table and the garden chair to clear the trapdoor.*

***Annabel:*** I mean, what was the point of drugging the woman…. *(the rest of the speech over three paragraphs is Annabel talking as opposed to the impression it is Miriam talking given in the original publications).*
