*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Snake In The Grass, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (2002)

**First performance:** 30 May 2002  
**World premiere:** 5 June 2002  
**Final performance:** 7 September 2002  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Hughes  
**Costumes:** Christine Wall  
**Music:** John Pattison
