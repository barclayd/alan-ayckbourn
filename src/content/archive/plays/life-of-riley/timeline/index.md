*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Life Of Riley.*

**Circa 2008 (possibly earlier)**  
Alan writes an initial concept for a play called *Life Of Riley*. The concept includes an 11 year old character called Winnie Barnstairs, who will become the lead character in Alan's 73rd play *My Wonderful Day* premiered in 2009.

**October 2009**  
Alan Ayckbourn begins writing *Life Of Riley*.

**11 December 2009**  
Alan Ayckbourn completes the first draft of *Life Of Riley*.

**26 January 2010**  
*Life Of Riley* is publicly confirmed as the title of Alan Ayckbourn's 74th play in an interview in Elmbridge Lifestyle magazine.

**11 May 2010**  
Alan offers his first insights into the play at the Stephen Joseph Theatre's summer season launch, describing it as akin to *Absent Friends* and that the title character, George Riley, does not appear in the play.

**18 June 2010**  
Casting publicly confirmed for *Life Of Riley*.

**21 September 2010**  
*Life Of Riley* world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**26 January 2010**  
End-stage tour of *Life Of Riley*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, begins at the Yvonne Arnaud Theatre, Guildford.

**30 April 2011**  
American premiere of *Life Of Riley* at The Old Globe, San Diego.

**October 2011**  
*Life Of Riley* published by Faber & Faber as part of the *Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 5* collection.

**16 May 2012**  
A film version of *Life Of Riley*, to be directed by Alain Resnais, is announced at the Cannes Fill Festival titled *Aimer, Boire et Chanter*.

**Early 2013**  
Filming on *Aimer, Boire et Chanter* begins.

**5 February 2014**  
*Life Of Riley* is produced in repertory with *Relatively Speaking* for the first time by Dick & Lottie, a then amateur company dedicated to the plays of Alan Ayckbourn; *Relatively Speaking* is featured in *Life Of Riley* and the plays were cross-cast to reflect this.

**10 February 2014**  
World premiere of *Aimer, Boire et Chanter* at the Berlin Film Festival where it wins the Silver Bear Alfred Bauer prize.

**1 March 2014**  
Alain Resnais, director of *Aimer, Boire et Chanter*, dies.

**26 March 2014**  
*Aimer, Boire et Chanter* released in France.

**18 May 2014**  
Variety reports *Life Of Riley* (*Aimer, Boire et Chanter*) has been picked up for the North American theatrical and home-video distribution.

**6 March 2015**  
*Life Of Riley* (*Aimer, Boire et Chanter*) released in UK cinemas.

**10 March 2015**  
North American home-video and digital platforms release of *Life Of Riley* (*Aimer, Boire et Chanter*).

**25 May 2015**  
*Life Of Riley* (*Aimer, Boire et Chanter*) released on Blu-Ray / DVD in the UK.
