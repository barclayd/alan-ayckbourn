This page contains basic details about the London premieres and revivals of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, click on a play title to find out more about the play. This list includes all significant London productions including the West End and major fringe productions. For a list of what are regarded as purely West End productions of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, click **[here](/life/ayckbourn-faqs/west-end)**.

**Notes:** The dates are the official opening dates (i.e. press nights) and do not include previews. Plays not available for production have been omitted from the list. Plays are listed in the order they were written.
