*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Suburban Strains, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1980)

**First performance:** 17 January 1980  
**World premiere:** 18 January 1980  
**Final performance:** 9 February 1980  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Musical Director:** Paul Todd  
**Choreographer:** Sarah Bennett  
**Designer:** John Hallé

**Stage Manager:** Amanda Saunders  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Miles Andrews

**Note:** *Suburban Strains* was revived at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1981, ostensiably for a transfer to London (see below). However, following its initial 11 day run at the venue (14 - 24 January), it was announced the production would return to Scarborough for another nine performances followings its London residency. The extra performances ran from 18 - 28 March 1981.
