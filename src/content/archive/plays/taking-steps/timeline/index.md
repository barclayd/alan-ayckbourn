*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Taking Steps; it only lists major productions and does not include the vast majority of the many professional and amateur productions of the play.*

**28 September 1979**  
World premiere of *Taking Steps* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**February 1980**  
European tour of *Taking Steps* with the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round company, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**March 1980**  
UK tour of *Taking Steps* with the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round company, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**18 June 1980**  
Revival of *Taking Steps* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**2 September 1980**  
London premiere of *Taking Steps* at the Lyric Theatre, directed by Michael Rudman.

**4 September 1980**  
*Taking Steps* celebrates its 100th performance at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round; the first play staged at this venue to have achieved this.

**6 June 1981**  
*Taking Steps* closes at the Lyric Theatre, London.

**1981**  
The West End production of *Taking Steps* embarks on a UK tour.

**1981**  
Chatto & Windus publishes *Taking Steps* in the hardback edition *Two Plays* alongside *Sisterly Feelings*.

**1981**  
Samuel French publishes the acting edition of *Taking Steps*.

**28 November 1990**  
Revival of *Taking Steps* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough, with Michael Gambon and directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**20 February 1991**  
Broadway premiere of *Taking Steps* at the Circle In The Square, directed by Alan Strachan.

**28 April 1991**  
*Taking Steps* closes at the Circle In The Square, New York.

**24 March 2010**  
London revival of *Taking Steps* at the Orange Tree Theatre, Richmond, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**29 May 2010**  
*Taking Steps* closes at the Orange Tree Theatre, Richmond.

**13 July 2017**  
Revival of *Taking Steps* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, opens directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**5 October 2017**  
*Taking Steps* closes at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, prior to touring.
