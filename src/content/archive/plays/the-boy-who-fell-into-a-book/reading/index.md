### Manuscripts (research & reference)

An original manuscript for the musical adaptation of *The Boy Who Fell Into A Book*is held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. The manuscript is accessible onsite for research purposes.
