*This page details the orchestration for the original production of Jeeves at Her Majesty's Theatre, London, and which was featured on the original cast recording of the musical. Many thanks to Stewart Thompson for providing this information as a result of his own research.*

*For comparison, the orchestration for the original production of By Jeeves at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, is also included.*

### Jeeves Orchestration

○ Flute / Piccolo / Alto Flute  
○ Oboe  
○ Clarinet 1 / Bass Clarinet  
○ Clarinet 2 / Flute

○ Horn 1  
○ Horn 2  
○ Trumpet  
○ Trombone

○ Percussion  
○ Drums  
○ Guitar  
○ Keyboard (Electric Piano, Accordion)  
○ Harp

○ Violin 1  
○ Violin 2  
○ Viola  
○ Cello  
○ Double Bass

○ Piano / MD

### By Jeeves Orchestration

○ Guitar  
○ Drums / Percussion  
○ Clarinet / Flute/ Saxophone  
○ Double Bass  
**Arrangements: Jeeves**  
The arrangements were by Andrew Lloyd Webber, Keith Amos, David Cullen and Don Walker with vocal arrangements by Anthony Bowles.

**Arrangements: By Jeeves**  
The arrangements were by David Cullen and Andrew Lloyd Webber.
