*Dracula* was one of a number of sketches and songs presented as part of the revue *What The Devil!* This page contains the details of the show both in its original touring form and its subsequent expanded version at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough. The original touring production did not include Alan Ayckbourn's *Dracula*, although it did feature his song *'Enry Albert's Ghost*.
