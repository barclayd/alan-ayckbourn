*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Hero’s Welcome.*

### World Premiere (2015)

**First performance:** 4 September 2015  
**World premiere:** 8 September 2015  
**Final performance:** 3 October 2015  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Michael Holt  
**Lighting:** Jason Taylor

**Stage Manager:** Fleur Linden Beeley  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Sue Volans  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Emma Lang

**Character** **Actor**  
**Murray** Richard Stacey  
**Baba** Terenia Edwards  
**Derek** Russell Dixon  
**Alice** Elizabeth Boag  
**Kara / Simone** Emma Manton  
**Brad** Stephen Billington  
**Voice only**  
**Interviewer 1** Harry Gration  
**Interviewer 2** Amy Garcia

**Note:** This production was then re-diredted for the end-stage and toured in a co-production with the Yvonne Arnaud Theatre, Guildford, from 13 January - 5 March 2016. The company was the same with the exception of Evelyn Hoskins replacing Terenia Edwards and the inclusion of two understudies, Philip Flynn and Nik Drake.
