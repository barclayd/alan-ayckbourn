*This page contains the company details for significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's The Westwoods.*

### World Premiere (1984)

**Her Side**  
**World premiere:** 29 May 1984  
**Final performance:** 4 September 1984

**His Side**  
**World premiere:** 31 May 1984  
**Final performance:** 6 September 1984

**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** End-stage

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Musical Director:** Paul Todd  
**Design:** Alan Marshall  
**Lighting:** Francis Lynch

**Character** **Actor**  
**Tidge** Jane Hollowood  
**Patricia** Caroline Webster  
**Trish** Alwyne Taylor  
**Pat** Dorcas Jones  
**Robert** Daniel Flynn  
**Rab** Mark Jax  
**Bobby** Robert Cotton  
**Bob** Alan Thompson

**Stage Manager:** Kathryn-Anne Woodward
