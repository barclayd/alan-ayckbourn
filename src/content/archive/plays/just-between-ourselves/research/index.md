This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Just Between Ourselves*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough (1976) held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd' (references: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/18](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374170/)** and **[Add MS 88915/1/12/22](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005457/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1976) at the Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough; brochure for the 1976 summer season at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough.

**Scarborough Museums and Galleries (The Ayckbourn Collection):** Annotated rehearsal manuscript by Christopher Godwin for the World premiere at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1976.
