*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Comic Potential, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1998)

**First performance:** 29 May 1998  
**World premiere:** 4 June 1998  
**Final performance:** 27 June 1998  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Kath Geraghty  
**Costumes:** Christine Wall  
**Music:** John Pattison
