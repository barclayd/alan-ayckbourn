*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Things We Do For Love, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1997)

**First performance:** 25 April 1997  
**World premiere:** 29 April 1997  
**Final performance:** 13 September 1997  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** End-stage

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Hughes  
**Costumes:** Christine Wall  
**Music:** John Pattison
