**What connects *RolePlay* to the other plays in the *Damsels In Distress* trilogy?**  
Although Alan Ayckbourn considers *GamePlan*, *FlatSpin* and *RolePlay* to be a trilogy, the link is not between the plays but a thematic and physical one. Each play features its own set of characters and plot unrelated to any of the other plays. However, they share a thematic link - damsels in distress - and, most significantly, a staging link. Each play is intended to be performed by the same company - offering each actor the chance to play three very different characters - and they all share the same set; a nondescript apartment in London's Docklands.

**How do I get permissions for amateur productions in North America / Canada?**  
Quite bizarrely, Concord Theatricals does not license any of the *Damsels in Distress* trilogy (*GamePlan*, *FlatSpin* or *RolePlay*) for amateur production in North America / Canada. The reason for this is unclear, Concord has the rights for English language speaking productions in the rest of the world and Alan Ayckbourn has never prohibited amateur performance of the plays in these territories - which is obviously frustrating for any amateur company in North America or Canada. However, the plays are available to perform by amateur companies in these territories and can be arranged by contacting Alan Ayckbourn's agents, **[Casarotto Ramsay](http://www.casarotto.co.uk/client/sir-alan-ayckbourn-11716/)**, and seeking permission there.
