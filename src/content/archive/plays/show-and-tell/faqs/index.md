*Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's Show & Tell. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the* ***[Contact Us](http://www.alanayckbourn.net/contact-form/Contacts.php)*** *page.*

**When will *Show & Tell* be published?**  
There is no information regarding this yet, but judging by recent publications of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, it will probably be published during 2026.

**Was *Show & Tell* filmed or recorded?**  
No, the Stephen Joseph Theatre made the decision in 2023 to no longer record nor stream Alan Ayckbourn's productions.
