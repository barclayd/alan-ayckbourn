*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Man Of The Moment; it only lists major productions and does not include the vast majority of the many professional and amateur productions of the play.*

**1987**  
Alan Ayckbourn's London producer Michael Codron suggests Alan direct a Shakespeare play in London in repertory with a new play which would feature the same company.

**Circa March 1988**  
Alan Ayckbourn returns to the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, following an acclaimed two year tenure as a company director at the National Theatre, London.

**Circa mid-March 1988**  
Alan Ayckbourn begins writing *Man Of The Moment*.

**29 March 1988**  
Alan Ayckbourn finishes writing *Man Of The Moment*.

**June 1988**  
It is reported Alan's new play *Man Of The Moment* is intended to transfer to the Aldwych Theatre in the West End in 1989 to play in repertory with an Ayckbourn-directed production of Shakespeare's *Othello*.

**10 August 1988**  
World premiere of *Man Of The Moment* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**March 1989**  
The Stage newspaper reports that the planned repertory production of *Othello* / *Man Of The Moment* in the West End has been cancelled.

**14 February 1990**  
London premiere of *Man Of The Moment* at the Globe Theatre, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**Mid-August 1990**  
Recast of *Man Of The Moment* at the Globe Theatre, London, directed by Alan Strachan.

**12 January 1991**  
*Man Of The Moment* closes at the Globe Theatre, London.

**1990**  
*Man Of The Moment* published by Faber & Faber.

**1991**  
*Man Of The Moment* acting edition published by Samuel French and released for performance by amateur societies.

**10 May 1992**  
BBC Radio 4 broadcast *Man Of The Moment*, directed by Gordon House.

**1994**  
LA Theaterworks release an audio adaptation of *Man Of The Moment*, directed by Robert Robinson.

**1995**  
*Man Of The Moment* collected in the Faber publication *Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 3*.

**12 April 2009**  
BBC Radio 4 broadcast a second adaptation of *Man Of The Moment*, directed by Martin Jarvis .

**27 July 2009**  
Revival of *Man Of The Moment* at the Royal & Derngate, Northampton, directed by Alan Ayckbourn marking the play’s 21st anniversary.

**24 May 2012**  
LA Theatre Works releases *Man Of The Moment* commercially as a digital download.
