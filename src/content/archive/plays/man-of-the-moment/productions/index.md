*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Man Of The Moment, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

**World premiere:** 10 August 1988  
**Final performance:** 29 October 1988  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Michael Holt  
**Lighting:** Mick Thomas  
**Music:** Paul Todd
