This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Bedroom Farce*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The National Theatre Archive:** The National Theatre Archive holds a substantive collection of material pertaining to its 1977 production of *Bedroom Farce*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn. The record page can be found **[here](http://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx)**.

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for London premiere at the National Theatre (1977) held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd' (references: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/7](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005442/)** and **[Add MS 88915/1/12/18](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374170/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1975) and brochure for the 1975 summer season at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough.

**Scarborough Museums and Galleries (The Ayckbourn Collection):** World premiere programme (1974) at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough; posters for the world premiere at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, and subsequent local area tour (1974). *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*
