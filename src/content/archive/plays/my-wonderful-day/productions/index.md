*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's My Wonderful Day, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (2009)

**First performance:** 8 October 2009  
**World premiere:** 13 October 2009  
**Final performance:** 31 October 2009  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Hughes  
**Costumes:** Jennie Boyer  
**Sound:** Ben Vickers

**Stage Manager:** Andy Hall  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Fleur Linden Beeley  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Barry Thompson

**Character** **Actor**  
**Winnie** Ayesha Antoine  
**Laverne** Petra Letang  
**The Man (Kevin)** Terence Booth  
**The Wife (Paula)** Alexandra Mathie  
**The Secretary (Tiffany)** Ruth Gibson  
**The Friend (Josh)** Paul Kemp
