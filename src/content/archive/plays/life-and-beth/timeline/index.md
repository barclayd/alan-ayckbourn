*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Life & Beth. As Life & Beth is related to his other supernatural plays Haunting Julia and Snake In The Grass as well as the playwright's stroke in 2006, these events have been incorporated into the timeline.*

**20 April 1994**  
*Haunting Julia* world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**10 February 1999**  
*Haunting Julia* revived at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**5 June 2002**  
*Snake In The Grass* world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**January 2006**  
Alan Ayckbourn completes his 70th play, *If I Were You*.

**21 February 2006**  
Alan Ayckbourn suffers a stroke.

**17 October 2006**  
*If I Were You* world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**6 July 2007**  
In an interview in The Guardian, Alan says he hopes to begin writing his 71st play imminently; this will be the first play since his stroke 18 months previously.

**Summer 2007**  
Alan Ayckbourn writes *Life & Beth*.

**24 December 2007**  
In an interview with BBC Radio York, Alan Ayckbourn confirms he has written a new play.

**11 February 2008**  
The Stephen Joseph Theatre announces *Life & Beth* will be premiered at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, in summer 2008 as part of an Ayckbourn supernatural season alongside *Haunting Julia* and *Snake In The Grass*.

**27 May 2008**  
*Haunting Julia* revived at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Richard Derrington.

**10 June 2008**  
*Snake In The Grass* revived at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**22 July 2008**  
*Life & Beth* world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**21 January 2009**  
*Life & Beth* tour begins at the Yvonne Arnaud Theatre, Guildford, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**October 2011**  
Faber & Faber publish the collection *Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 5* which includes *Life & Beth*.

**October 2011**  
Samuel French publish an acting edition of *Life & Beth*.
