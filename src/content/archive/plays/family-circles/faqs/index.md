*Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd's answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's Family Circles. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the* ***[Contact Us](http://www.alanayckbourn.net/contact-form/Contacts.php)*** *page.*

**Is it possible to read any of the earlier versions of *Family Circles* - such as *The Story So Far…* or *Me Times Me Times Me*?**  
A single copy each of *The Story So Far…*, *Me Times Me Times Me* and *Me Times Me* are held in the Alan Ayckbourn in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives at the University of York](/)**. They are available to read and research onsite.

**Is it possible to produce any of the earlier versions of *Family Circles*?**  
No - although manuscripts for these earlier itineration of the play, these have been withdrawn from production by the author. Also Alan Ayckbourn considers *Family Circles* to be the definitive version of the play and has always been unhappy with the previous itineration of the play.
