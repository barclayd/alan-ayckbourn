This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Family Circles*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

**Archival Note:** The original 1970 production was presented under the title ***The Story So Far…***, further to this the tours of 1971 were presented as ***Me Times Me Times Me*** / ***Me Times Me*** and 1972 as ***Me Times Me***. Archival resources specifically for these productions may reference these titles rather than *Family Circles*.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere of *The Story So Far…* at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough (1970) and revised *Family Circles* (1985) held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd (reference: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/18](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374170/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1970) and brochure for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre 1970 summer season; accounts for 1970 summer season for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre.

**Victoria & Albert:** **[The Peter Bridge Productions Archive](http://www.vam.ac.uk/archives/unit/ARC107434/)** is held by the museum and, potentially, there is material relating to the world (1970) and proposed West End (1971) premieres of *Family Circles*. As the collection is currently uncatalogued, this is to be confirmed. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*
