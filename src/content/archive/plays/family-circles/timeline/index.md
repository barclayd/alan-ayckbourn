*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Family Circles; it only lists major productions and does not include the vast majority of the many professional and amateur productions of the play.*

**Note:** *Family Circles* was originally produced as *The Story So Far...*, before being retitled *Me Times Me Times Me*, which was retitled *Me Times Me* before arriving at its final title.

**May 1970**  
Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, begins advertising Alan Ayckbourn's new play *The Story So Far…*.

**Late July 1970**  
Alan Ayckbourn begins writing his 10th play, *The Story So Far...*.

**3 August 1970**  
Alan Ayckbourn finishes writing *The Story So Far…*.

**7 August 1970**  
Rehearsals begin for the world premiere of *The Story So Far…*.

**20 August 1970**  
World premiere of *The Story So Far...* at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**Circa late 1970 / early 1971**  
Alan Ayckbourn revises *The Story So Far...* and retitles it *Me Times Me Times Me* for a 1971 tour intended to go into the West End.

**25 August 1971**  
A tour of *Me Times Me Times Me* begins at the Phoenix Theatre, Leicester, directed by Robin Midgley and produced by Eddie Kulukundis.

**September 1971**  
Alan Ayckbourn continues revising the play, changing it from a three act play to a two act play with the shortened title *Me Times Me*.

**20 September 1971**  
*Me Times Me* tours to Edinburgh from where it is advertised the play will immediately transfer in London.

**27 September 1971**  
*Me Times Me* tours to the New Theatre, Oxford, from where it is advertised the play will immediately transfer to London.

**18 October 1971**  
*Me Times Me* tours to the Theatre Royal, Bath. The tour ends here on 23 October and the play does not transfer to London.

**February 1972**  
Alan Ayckbourn informs the board of Scarborough Theatre Trust that the producer Michael Codron is staging a new short tour of *Me Times Me* with the intention of taking it into London.

**13 March 1972**  
A tour of *Me Times Me* opens at the Alexandra Theatre, Birmingham, directed by Basil Coleman and produced by Michael Codron.

**20 March 1972**  
*Me Times Me* tours to the Theatre Royal, Brighton, for a three week run.

**1 April 1972**  
In an interview with the Daily Telegraph, the producer Michael Codron says *Me Times Me* is due to transfer into London later in April.

**Circa 8 April 1972**  
*Me Times Me* tour ends at the Theatre Royal, Brighton. It does not transfer to London.

**Between April 1972 and July 1974**  
Alan Ayckbourn revises the play again and renames it *Family Circles*.

**24 November 1978**  
London premiere of *Family Circles* at the Orange Tree Theatre, Richmond, directed by Sam Walters; the first professional in-the-round staging of an Ayckbourn play in London.

**4 September 1985**  
Revival of *Family Circles* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**28 November 1996**  
Revival of *Family Circles* at the Orange Tree Theatre, Richmond, directed by Sam Walters.

**1997**  
Samuel French publishes the acting edition of *Family Circles*. The play is released for general production.
