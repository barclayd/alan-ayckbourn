### Manuscripts (Reference & research)

Original manuscripts for *The Story So Far…*, *Me Times Me Times Me* and *Me Times Me* are held in the Ayckbourn Archive at the **[Borthwick Insitute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. The manuscripts are accessible onsite for research purposes.
