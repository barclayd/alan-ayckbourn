Heather Stoney **Note:** *Anno Domino* was conceived as a full-length, full-cast stage play. However, during the Covid-19 theatre lockdown of 2020, Alan Ayckbourn adapted it as an audio stream with himself and his wife, Heather Stoney (both former professional actors) performing all the roles. This became the first of the playwright's works to be premiered as an audio drama.

It is hoped that *Anno Domino* will be produced on-stage as intended as a full-cast production in the future.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
