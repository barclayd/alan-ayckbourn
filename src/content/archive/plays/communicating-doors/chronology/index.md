When originally writing *Communicating Doors*, Alan Ayckbourn kept a note of what was happening when and to whom. These notes are organised below (original timeline) alongside the website's attempt to show how the timeline is altered by the events of the play (altered timeline).

### Original Timeline

**Prior to 1974**  
○ Julian kills his own mother.

**1974**  
○ At this point: Reece is 30, Jessica is 25, Julian is 25, Ruella is 25.  
○ Reece & Jessica are married. They honeymoon at the Regal Hotel.

**1975**  
○ Jessica & Reece's daughter, Rachel, is born on 21 March.

**1981**  
○ Poopay (Phoebe) is born  
○ Jessica is murdered by Julian on 15 August 1981, aged 32.

**1982**  
○ Reece marries Ruella when he is 38 and she is 33.

**1983**  
○ Ruella & Reece's son, Thomas, is born.

**1994**  
○ At this point: Reece is 50, Julian is 45, Ruella is 45, Poopay is 13.  
○ Ruella plans to divorce Reece.  
○ Ruella is murdered by Julian on 5 October, aged 45 at the Regal Hotel in room 647.

**Prior to 2013**  
○ Poopay enters the sex trade, at least eight years previously.

**2014**  
○ At this point: Reece is 70, Julian is 65, Harold is 75, Poopay is 33.  
○ Reece writes a confession about the murders of Jessica and Ruella which he gives to Poopay on 25 July.  
○ Threatened by Julian, Poopay steps through a hotel communicating door.

### Altered Timeline

**Prior to 1974**  
○ Julian kills his own mother.

**1974**  
○ At this point: Reece is 30, Jessica is 25, Julian is 25, Ruella is 25.  
○ Reece & Jessica are married. They honeymoon at the Regal Hotel.  
○ Ruella comes back in time from 1994 and warns Jessica about the future; she gives Jessica a note.

**1975**  
○ Jessica & Reece's daughter, Rachel, is born on 21 March.  
○ Jessica reads Ruella's note on 22 March, which convinces her Ruella was telling the truth about her future.

**1981**  
○ Phoebe (Poopay) is born.  
○ Jessica divorces Reece having avoided getting murdered by Julian.

**1982**  
○ Reece marries Ruella when he is 38 and she is 33.

**1983**  
○ Ruella & Reece's son, Thomas, is born.  
○ Jessica marries Count Rory Rizzini.

**1994**  
○ At this point: Reece is 50, Jessica is 45, Julian is 45, Ruella is 45, Phoebe is 13.  
○ Jessica returns to the Regal Hotel on 4 October and waits in the store room next to room 647 intent on saving Ruella.  
○ Poopay comes back in time from 2014 and warns Ruella on 5 October that Julian will kill her that day.\*  
○ Julian is killed on 5 October. He has apparently fallen from the hotel balcony window.

**Circa 1995**  
○ Ruella and Reece adopt Phoebe.

**Prior to 2013**  
○ Phoebe marries and has at least two children.

**2013**  
○ Ruella dies of natural causes in June, aged 64.

**2014**  
○ At this point: Reece is 70, Jessica is (presumably) 65, Phoebe is 33.
