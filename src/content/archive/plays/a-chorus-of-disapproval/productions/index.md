*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's A Chorus Of Disapproval, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

**Note:** Colin Blakely was ill during a substantial portion of the play's run and, eventually, had to step down from the role of Daydd; he would die two months after the play closed in the West End. After his illness became too severe to continue in 1987, the role of Daydd was taken over by Russell Dixon, who had originated it in Scarborough in 1984.
