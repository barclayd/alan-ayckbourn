*Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's Making Tracks. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the* ***[Contact Us](http://www.alanayckbourn.net/contact-form/Contacts.php)*** *page.*

**Is *Making Tracks* available to perform?**  
Yes. Although it has been in something of a grey area for many years, the playwright confirmed during 2020 that *Making Tracks* had not been withdrawn and was available for production. Further information and applications for licenses should be made **[Casarotto Ramsay](http://www.casarotto.co.uk/client/sir-alan-ayckbourn-11716/)**.

**Will *Making Tracks* be published?**  
This is viewed as extremely unlikely. The play has never been published and this situation is unlikely to change.

**Where can I read the play for research purposes?**  
Original manuscripts are held by both the British Library and within the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York, which are accessible for research purposes and can be read on-site.
