### World Premiere (1981)

**First performance:** 15 December 1981  
**World premiere:** 16 December 1981  
**Final performance:** 9 January 1982  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Michael Holt  
**Lighting:** Francis Lynch  
**Music:** Paul Todd  
**Sound:** Jane Smailes

**Stage Manager:** Isobel Nimmo  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Eric Lumsden

**Assistant Stage Manager:** John Skitt
