This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Sisterly Feelings*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The National Theatre Archive:** The National Theatre Archive holds a substantive collection of material pertaining to its 1980 production of *Sisterly Feelings*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn. The record page can be found **[here](http://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx)**.

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for the West End premiere at the National Theatre, London (1980), held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd' (references: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/19](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374172/)** and **[Add MS 88915/1/12/22](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005457/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1979) at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough; brochure for the 1978 / 1979 winter season at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough.  
*The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*

*If you have any research enquiries regarding Sisterly Feelings, please contact Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd at* ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***.
