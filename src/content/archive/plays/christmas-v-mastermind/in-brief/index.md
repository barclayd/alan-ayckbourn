### Christmas V Mastermind

**Play Number:** 5  
**World Premiere:** 26 December 1962  
**Venue:** Victoria Theatre, Stoke-on-Trent

**Premiere Staging:** In-the-round

**Published:** No  
**Other Media:** No

**Cast:** 6m / 3f

**Synopsis:** A criminal genius attempts to usurp Santa Claus by taking over Christmas by fomenting dissent in the toy factory and, later, by attempting to destroy the factory. All the while, two inept police officers are on the trail whilst Santa attempts to save Christmas.

**Note:** *Christmas V Mastermind* has never been published, nor is it available to produce. Original manuscripts are held in the British Library and the Ayckbourn Archive at the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York.

</aside>

- *Christmas V Mastermind* is Alan Ayckbourn's fifth play.
- The world premiere was held at the **[Victoria Theatre](/career/victoria-theatre)**, Stoke-on-Trent, on 26 December 1962.
- *Christmas V Mastermind* is one of Alan Ayckbourn's withdrawn plays. It has never been published and it is not available for production.
- It was the first of two plays to be premiered at the Victoria Theatre whilst Alan Ayckbourn was employed as an actor, director and playwright at the venue between 1962 and 1964. The other play was ***[Mr Whatnot](/plays/mr-whatnot)***.
- *Christmas V Mastermind* is one of only five plays written by Alan Ayckbourn which have not received their world premiere in Scarborough. The others are *Mr Whatnot* (Stoke-on-Trent); ***[Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** (London), ***[A Small Family Business](/plays/a-small-family-business)*** (National Theatre) and ***[All Lies](/plays/all-lies)*** (Bowness-on-Windermere).
- *Christmas V Mastermind* is the first play written by Alan Ayckbourn which is actually credited to Alan Ayckbourn. Prior to this play, he wrote under the pseudonym of Roland Allen; although just to confuse matters, his fourth play ***[Standing Room Only](/plays/standing-room-only)*** was originally credited to Roland Allen when first produced but is now credited to Alan Ayckbourn.
- *Christmas V Mastermind* is the second of Alan Ayckbourn's early 'family plays'. However, the 'family' plays are officially considered to begin with ***[Mr A's Amazing Maze Plays](/plays/mr-as-amazing-maze-plays)*** (1988) due to the earlier plays being withdrawn.
- *Christmas V Mastermind* marked the final time Alan Ayckbourn would perform professionally on stage in one of his own plays.
- It marked the first time the actress Heather Stoney would appear in an Alan Ayckbourn play. She would become one of the most prolific actors to appear in world premieres of Alan's plays.
- *Christmas V Mastermind* has never been published, nor is it available to produce. Original manuscripts are held in the British Library and the Ayckbourn Archive at the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York.
