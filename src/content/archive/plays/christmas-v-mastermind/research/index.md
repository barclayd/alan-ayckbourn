This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Christmas V Mastermind*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at the Victoria Theatre, Stoke-on-Trent (1962) held in The Lord Chamberlain's Play collection.

**The University of Staffordshire:** The Victoria Theatre Collection is held in the university's special collections and, potentially, there is material relating to its significant 1962 world premiere at the Victoria Theatre, Stoke-on-Trent. As the collection is currently uncatalogued, this is to be confirmed. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*

*If you have any research enquiries regarding Christmas V Mastermind, please contact Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd at* ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***.  [![](./_images/web-175-dpi.jpg)](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)
