This page contains details of publications & articles relating to *Christmas V Mastermind*. The information is for research & the website cannot provide copies of the articles listed.

### Published Editions

*Christmas V Mastermind* has not been published.

### Manuscripts (research & reference)

An original manuscript for *Christmas V Mastermind* is held in the Lord Chamberlain's Collection at the British Library with a photocopy of the manuscript held in the Ayckbourn Archive at the **[Borthwick Insitute for Archives](/)** at the University of York.
