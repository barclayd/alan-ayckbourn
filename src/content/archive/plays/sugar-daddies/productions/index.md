*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Sugar Daddies, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently his plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (2003)

**First performance:** 17 July 2003  
**World premiere:** 22 July 2003  
**Final performance:** 13 September 2003  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Hughes  
**Costumes:** Christine Wall  
**Music:** David Newton

**Stage Manager:** Corrie Jamie Cooper  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Andy Hall  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Andrew Wilcox

**Character** **Actor**  
**Sasha** Alison Pargeter  
**Val** Rex Garner  
**Chloe** Anna Brecon  
**Ashley** Terence Booth  
**Charmaine** Eliza Hunt
