This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Standing Room Only*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough (1961) held in The Lord Chamberlain's Play collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd (reference: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/20](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374173/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Brochure for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre 1961 summer season.

**The University of Staffordshire:** The Victoria Theatre Collection is held in the university's special collections and, potentially, there is material relating to its significant 1962 revival by Alan Ayckbourn at the Victoria Theatre, Stoke-onTrent. As the collection is currently uncatalogued, this is to be confirmed. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*

*If you have any research enquiries regarding Standing Room Only, please contact Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd at* ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***.  [![](./_images/web-175-dpi.jpg)](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)
