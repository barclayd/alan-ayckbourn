Naomi Petersen**Note:** During 2020, all theatres in the UK were closed as a result of the Covid-19 pandemic. This resulted in Alan Ayckbourn taking the decision to premiere a new play, *Anno Domino*, as an audio stream with him and his wife, Heather Stoney, taking on all the roles.  
The success of *Anno Domino* led Alan to decide to record *Haunting Julia* as an audio play to be streamed during the Christmas 2020 period - when most theatres were still either closed or opening on a very limited basis.  
The production was initially streamed for an exclusive period on the Stephen Joseph Theatre's website. Initially intended to run from 1 December 2020 to 5 January 2021, the run was extended by the SJT until 31 January 2021.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
