*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's FlatSpin, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (2001)

**World premiere:** 3 July 2001  
**Final performance:** 8 September 2001  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Hughes  
**Costumes:** Christine Wall
