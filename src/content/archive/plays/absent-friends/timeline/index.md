*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Absent Friends; it only lists major productions and does not include the vast majority of the many professional and amateur productions of the play.*

**17 June 1974**  
World premiere of *Absent Friends* at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**28 September 1974**  
*Absent Friends* closes at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, having had its run extended by a week due to its popularity.

**30 June 1975**  
*Absent Friends*, directed by Eric Thompson, opens at the Brighton Theatre Royal for two weeks prior to a week at the Theatre Royal, Norwich, before transferring to the West End.

**23 July 1975**  
London premiere of *Absent Friends* at the Garrick Theatre, London, directed by Eric Thompson.

**1975**  
Samuel French publishes the acting edition of *Absent Friends*.

**2 April 1976**  
North American premiere of Absent Friends at the Long Wharf Theater, New Haven, directed by Eric Thompson.

**24 April 1976**  
*Absent Friends* closes at the Garrick Theatre, London.

**May 1977**  
First UK provincial tour of *Absent Friends* is launched.

**1977**  
Radio adaptation of *Absent Friends* premiered on BBC World Service, directed by Dickon Reed. Produced without the permission of Alan Ayckbourn and running less than an hour, this is later withdrawn from future broadcast.

**1977**  
Chatto & Windus publish *Absent Friends* in the mass market hardback edition *Three Plays*.

**11 July 1977**  
North American revival of *Absent Friends*, directed by Eric Thompson, at the Eisenhower Theater, Kennedy Center, Washington. It closes on 13 August 1977 and an intended transfer to New York never takes place.

**16 March 1982**  
Premiere of *Absent Friends* at the Alley Theatre, Houston, directed by Alan Ayckbourn and featuring the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round company; *Absent Friends* was specifically produced for the venue during a two month residency alongside the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round's production of *Way Upstream*.

**29 September 1985**  
Television adaptation of *Absent Friends* premiered in the UK on BBC2, directed by Michael Simpson.

**29 January 1991**  
New York premiere of *Absent Friends* by the Manhattan Theatre Club at the City Centre Stage, directed by Lynne Meadow.

**15 March 1991**  
*Absent Friends* Closes at the City Centre Stage, New York.

**1991**  
Longman publishes a student edition of *Absent Friends*.

**9 October 1997**  
Revival of *Absent Friends* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**1999**  
Pearson Education Limited publishes *York Notes: Absent Friends* for students studying the play.

**9 February 2012**  
West End revival of *Absent Friends* at the Harold Pinter Theatre, London, directed by Jemermy Herrin.

**14 April 2012**  
West End revival of *Absent Friends* closes at the Harold Pinter Theatre, London.

**21 September 2012**  
The BBC television adaptation of *Absent Friends* is screened in a cinema for the first time as part of the Ayckbourn Film Festival at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough.
