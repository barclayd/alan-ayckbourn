This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *The Square Cat*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough (1959) held in The Lord Chamberlain's Play collection.

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere production (1959) and brochure for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre 1959 summer season, correspondence related to Stephen Joseph and the summer 1959 season at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre.

**Scarborough Museums & Galleries (The Ayckbourn Collection):** Actor's rehearsal manuscript for world premiere production at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough (1959) and two production photographs.

**The John Rylands Research Institute and Library, Manchester University:** Typescript of production manuscript for world premiere at Theatre in the Round at there Library Theatre, Scarborough (1959) held in The Stephen Joseph Papers.
