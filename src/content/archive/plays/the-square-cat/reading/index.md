This page contains details of publications & articles relating to *The Square Cat*. The information is for research and the website cannot provide copies of the articles listed.

### Published Editions

*The Square Cat* has not been published.

### Manuscripts (research & reference)

An original manuscript for *The Square Cat* is held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research)** at the University of York as well as at the British Library, Scarborough Museums and Galleries and the John Rylands Library at the University of Manchester. The manuscripts are accessible onsite for research purposes.
