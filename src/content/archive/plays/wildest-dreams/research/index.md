This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Wildest Dreams.* The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The Royal Shakespeare Company:** The Shakespeare Birthplace Trust holds the RSC archive which includes a substantive collection of material pertaining to its 1993 production of *Wildest Dreams*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn. This includes performance recordings at both The Swan theatre in Stratford-upon-Avon and The Pit at The Barbican in London. The record page can be found **[here](http://collections.shakespeare.org.uk/search/archive/search/everywhere:wildest-dreams/page/2/)**.

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough (1991).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1991) at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough; brochure for the 1991 summer season at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough.
