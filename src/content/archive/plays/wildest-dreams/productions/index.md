*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Wildest Dreams, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1991)

**First performance:** 1 May 1991  
**World premiere:** 6 May 1991  
**Final performance:** 21 September 1991  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Hughes  
**Music:** John Pattison

**Stage Manager:** Jane Eliot-Webb  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Sallith Goodman  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Chris Savage

**Character** **Actor**  
**Stanley Inchbridge** Barry McCarthy  
**Hazel Inchbridge** Anna Keaveney  
**Warren Wrigley** Gary Whitaker  
**Rick Toller** Isabel Lloyd  
**Marcie Banks** Rebecca Lacey\*  
**Austen Skate** Peter Laird  
**Thelma Wrigley** Elizabeth Rider  
**Larry Banks** Glyn Grain

*\*During the original run of the production, Rebecca Lacey left the production for two weeks following the death of her father. During her absence, the role of Marcie was played by Lesley Meade.*
