This page contains details of publications and articles relating to *The Divide*. The information is intended as a research tool and the website cannot able to provide copies of the articles listed.

**The Divide** (Softback)  
*(Alan Ayckbourn, 2019, ISBN 9781786364487)*

**Note:** *The Divide* was adapted for theatrical performance by Annabel Bolton during 2016 for The Old Vic. This version has never been published and is available to read only in archive.

### Manuscripts (research & reference)

A manuscript for the play adaptation of *The Divide* is held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. The manuscript is accessible onsite for research purposes.
