This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Relatively Speaking*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

**Archival Note:** The original 1965 production was presented under the title ***Meet My Father*** and archival resources specifically for this production may reference this title rather than *Relatively Speaking*.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough (1965) held in The Lord Chamberlain's Play collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd (reference: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/14](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005449/), [Add MS 88915/1/12/15](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374168/), [Add MS 88915/1/12/16](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374169/)** and **[Add MS 88915/1/12/22](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005457/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programmes for world premiere (1965) and Ayckbourn-directed revival (1977); brochure for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre 1965 summer season; accounts for 1965 summer season for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre.

**Scarborough Museums and Galleries (The Ayckbourn Collection):** Alan Ayckbourn's personal copy of the first edition of the published Evans edition of the play with handwritten notes and corrections (1968).

**Victoria & Albert:** **[The Peter Bridge Productions Archive](http://www.vam.ac.uk/archives/unit/ARC107434/)** is held by the museum and, potentially, there is material relating to the world (1965) and West End (1967) premieres of *Relatively Speaking*. As the collection is currently uncatalogued, this is to be confirmed. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*
