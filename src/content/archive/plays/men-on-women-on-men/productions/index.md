*This page contains the company details for significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Men On Women On Men.*

### World Premiere (1978)

**World premiere:** 17 June 1978  
**Final performance:** 8 September 1978  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough  
**Staging:** End-stage

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Musical Director:** Paul Todd  
**Design:** David Millard  
**Choreographer:** Fiona Mathieson  
**Costumes:** Frances Upton  
**Music:** Paul Todd

**Stage Manager:** Amanda Saunders**The Company**
