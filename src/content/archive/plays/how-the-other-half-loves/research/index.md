This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *How The Other Half Loves*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough (1969) held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd (reference: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/10](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005445/), [Add MS 88915/1/12/11](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374154/),** and **[Add MS 88915/1/12/22](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005457/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1969) and brochure for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre 1969 summer season; accounts for 1969 summer season for Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre.

**Victoria & Albert:** **[The Peter Bridge Productions Archive](http://www.vam.ac.uk/archives/unit/ARC107434/)** is held by the museum and, potentially, there is material relating to the world (1969) and West End (1970) premieres of *How The Other Half Loves*. As the collection is currently uncatalogued, this is to be confirmed. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*
