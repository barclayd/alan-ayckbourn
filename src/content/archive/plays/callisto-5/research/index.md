This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Callisto 5 / Callisto#7* The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscripts for world premieres at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough (1990 & 1999); correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd' (references: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/17](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005452/)**).

**Scarborough Library (The Library Theatre / Stephen Joseph Collection):** Programme for world premiere (1990) at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough; brochure for the 1990 winter season at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round, Scarborough. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*

*If you have any research enquiries regarding Callisto 5 / Callisto#7, please contact Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd at* ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***.  [![](./_images/web-175-dpi.jpg)](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)
