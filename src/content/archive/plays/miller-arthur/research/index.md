This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's production at the National Theatre of Arthur Miller's *A View From The Bridge.* The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York and the National Theatre Archive. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The National Theatre Archive:** The National Theatre Archive holds a substantive collection of material pertaining to its 1987 production of Arthur Miller's *A View From The Bridge*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn. The record pages can be found **[here](http://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx)**. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*

*If you have any research enquiries regarding A View From The Bridge, please contact Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd at* ***[research@alanayckbourn.net](mailto:research@alanayckbourn.net)***.  [![](./_images/web-175-dpi.jpg)](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)
