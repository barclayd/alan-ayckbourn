This page contains details of publications & articles relating to *Jeeves* & *By Jeeves*. The information is for research & the website cannot provide copies of the articles listed.

### Published Editions

**Jeeves**  
*Jeeves* has not been published.

**By Jeeves**  
*By Jeeves* has not been published. It is available through **[Andrew Lloyd Webber Show Licensing](http://alwshowlicensing.com/show/byjeeves/)** for companies wishing to produce professional / amateur productions of it.

### Manuscripts (research & reference)

Various original draft manuscripts for *Jeeves* & *By Jeeves* are held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. The manuscripts are accessible onsite for research purposes.
