This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *Jeeves / By Jeeves*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere of *Jeeves* at Her Majesty's Theatre, London (1975) and the world premiere of *By Jeeves* (1996) at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd' (reference: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/17](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000005452/)**); programmes and press cuttings for Jeeves (1975) and By Jeeves (1996) in the P.G. Wodehouse Collection (reference: **[LOAN MS 129/7/22](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-003374921/)** and **[LOAN MS 129/7/23](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-003374931/)**).

**V&A:** 12" vinyl original cast recording of *Jeeves* (1975); original designs for *Jeeves* (1975) in the Voytek Archive. *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*
