This page contains details of publications & articles relating to Alan Ayckbourn's adaptation of R.B. Sheridan’s *A Trip To Scarborough*. The information is for research & the website cannot provide copies of the articles listed on this page.

### Published Editions

*A Trip To Scarborough* has not been published.

### Manuscripts (research & reference)

Manuscripts for *A Trip To Scarborough* are held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. The manuscripts are accessible onsite for research purposes.
