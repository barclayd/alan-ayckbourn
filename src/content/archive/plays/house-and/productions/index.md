*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's House & Garden, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

**Final performance:** 10 July 1999
