*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's This Is Where We Came In, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1990)

**World premiere:** 4 August 1980 (pt 1)

**Final performance:** 8 September 1980

**Staging:** Round
