*Very occasionally, Alan Ayckbourn's plays are published with errors in the text. This page has corrections for the text of Private Fears In Public Places published in the Faber collection Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 3. Please note, the* ***[Samuel French acting edition](http://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/s/7618/private-fears-in-public-places/)*** *text is correct.*

### Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 3 (Faber & Faber, 2005)

Page 348, scene 22

**As printed:  
Dan:** No? She walked out again, did she?  
**Ambrose:** No. She died.

**Correct Version (correction in bold italics):  
Dan:** No? She walked out again, did she?  
**Ambrose:** No. ***They*** died.

For an explanation as to why this alteration is significant, please visit the **[Other Perspectives](/plays/private-fears-in-public-places/coeurs/other-perspectives)** page.
