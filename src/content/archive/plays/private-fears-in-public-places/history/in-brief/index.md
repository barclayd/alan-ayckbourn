<aside>

### Private Fears in Public Places

**Play Number:** 67  
**World Premiere:** 17 August 2004  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough

**Premiere Staging:** In-the-round

**Published:** **[Samuel French](http://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/s/7618/private-fears-in-public-places/)**  
**Other Media:** Film

**Cast:** 3m / 3f  
**Run Time:** 1hr 45m

**Short Synopsis:** A play which traces the lives and relationships of six loosely linked people in London.

</aside>

- *Private Fears In Public Places* is Alan Ayckbourn's 67th play.
- The world premiere - directed by Alan Ayckbourn - was held at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)**, Scarborough, on 17 August 2004.
- The London premiere - directed by Alan Ayckbourn - was held at the Orange Tree Theatre, Richmond, on 25 May 2005.
- It is one of only three full-length plays written by Alan Ayckbourn without an interval (and runs at approximately 105 minutes). The others are ***[Haunting Julia](/plays/haunting-julia)*** (1994) and ***[My Wonderful Day](/plays/my-wonderful-day)*** (2009).
- The title was originally intended for a totally different play announced by Alan Ayckbourn in 1994; when he instead wrote ***[Communicating Doors](/plays/communicating-doors)***, he saved the title *Private Fears In Public Places* for a later play.
- *Private Fears In Public Places* was adapted into a French language film, ***[Coeurs](/plays/private-fears-in-public-places/coeurs)***, by the award-winning director Alain Resnais in 2006.
- With 54 scenes, *Private Fears In Public Places* has more scenes than any other Ayckbourn play; the nearest equivalents are ***[The Revengers' Comedies](/plays/the-revengers-comedies)*** pt.1 and pt.2 which has a combined total of 42 scenes and ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges)***, which has a combined total of 31 scenes from its eight permutations.
- The play features a composite set (several locations on one stage at all times). Other Ayckbourn plays to feature composite sets include ***[How The Other Half Loves](/plays/how-the-other-half-loves)***, ***[Bedroom Farce](/plays/bedroom-farce)***, ***[Taking Steps](/plays/taking-steps)***, ***[If I Were You](/plays/if-i-were-you)*** and ***[Life Of Riley](/plays/life-of-riley)***.
- It is one of the few Ayckbourn plays to be specifically set in London. Other London-set plays include ***[Virtual Reality](/plays/virtual-reality)***, ***[GamePlan](/plays/gameplan)***, ***[FlatSpin](/plays/flatspin)***, ***[RolePlay](/plays/roleplay)*** and ***[Sugar Daddies](/plays/sugar-daddies)***.
- *Private Fears In Public Places* is one of a number of full-length Ayckbourn plays to have been directed in New York by the playwright himself. The previous plays are ***[Arrivals & Departures](/plays/arrivals-and-departures)***, ***[Bedroom Farce](/plays/bedroom-farce)*** (co-directed with Peter Hall); ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)***, ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges)***, ***[My Wonderful Day](/plays/my-wonderful-day)***, ***[Neighbourhood Watch](/plays/neighbourhood-watch)***, ***[Time Of My Life](/plays/time-of-my-life)***, ***[Confusions](/plays/confusions)***, ***[Hero's Welcome](/plays/heros-welcome)*** and ***[A Brief History of Women](/plays/a-brief-history-of-women)***.
- Although published as a play text by **[Samuel French](http://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/s/7618/private-fears-in-public-places/)**, *Private Fears in Public Places* was also published in the collection *Alan Ayckbourn: Plays 3* (Faber).
