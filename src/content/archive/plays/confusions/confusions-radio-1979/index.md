*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's Confusions, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently it has been performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1974)

**First performance:** 30 September 1974  
**World premiere:** 30 September 1974  
**Final performance:** 9 October 1974  
**Venue:** Lecture Room, Library Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Three-sided

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Costume Design:** Christine Welch  
**Design:** Helga Wood  
**Music:** Paul Todd

**Stage Manager:** David Millard  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Kevin Wood

**Character** **Actor**  
**Harry / Martin / Vicar / Charles** Christopher Godwin  
**Lucy / Bernice / Mrs Pearce / Beryl** Janet Dale  
**Rosemary / Paula / Polly /** Eileen O’Brien  
**Milly / Doreen**  
**Mr Pearce / Gosforth / Arthur** Stanley Page  
**Terry / Waiter / Stewart / Ernest** Stephen Mallatratt

**Note:** This production immediately went on a local tour with performances at Scarborough, Filey and Whitby presented in weekly repertory with other works. The schedule consisted of Filey Sun Lounge (12 & 26 November), Whitby Pavilion (13, 14, 27 & 28 November) and The Library Theatre,. Scarborough (15, 16, 29 & 30 November).

**Notes**  
◦ The West End premiere was preceded by a short tour to Theatre Royal Bath from 19 April and the Theatre Royal Brighton from 26 April. Erroneous  
◦ It should be noted the West End production deliberately and erroneously changed the author's intention by having John Alderton take all the lead male roles in what is intended to be an ensemble work. The distribution of roles should always follow the pattern of Alan Ayckbourn's Scarborough productions rather than the West End production.
