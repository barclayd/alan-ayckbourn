<aside>

### If I Were You

**Play Number:** 70  
**World Premiere:** 17 October 2006  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough

**Premiere Staging:** In-the-round

**Published:** **[Samuel French](http://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/s/1953/if-i-were-you/)**  
**Other Media:** No

**Cast:** 3m / 2f  
**Run Time:** 2hr 5m

**Synopsis:** Mal and Jill Rodale's marriage is on rocky ground, but when an unexpected event puts them in each other's shoes, they begin to better appreciate each other.

</aside>

- *If I Were You* is Alan Ayckbourn's 70th play.
- The world premiere - directed by Alan Ayckbourn - was held at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)**, Scarborough, on 17 October 2006.
- It was the final play Alan Ayckbourn completed before his stroke on 21 February 2006. The play had been finished only several weeks earlier.
- *If I Were You* was the first play Alan Ayckbourn directed after recovering from his stroke. He has noted that by scheduling the play into the Stephen Joseph Theatre's winter season, it gave him a goal to aim for during his recuperation.
- The play is one of several body-swapping plays written by the playwright. These date back to 1987 and include ***[Henceforward…](/plays/henceforward)*** (a female robot being altered to resemble another woman); ***[Body Language](/plays/body-language)*** (the heads of two women are transposed); ***[The Jollies](/plays/the-jollies)*** (a child finds themselves in an adult's body and vice versa).
- The earliest draft of the play was called *I To I*. It then became *If I Were You*, only to briefly return to *I To I* before reverting back to *If I Were You*.
- The play features a composite set (several locations on one stage at all times). Other Ayckbourn plays to feature composite sets include ***[How The Other Half Loves](/plays/how-the-other-half-loves)***, ***[Bedroom Farce](/plays/bedroom-farce)***, ***[Taking Steps](/plays/taking-steps)***, ***[Private Fears In Public Places](/plays/private-fears-in-public-places)*** and ***[Life Of Riley](/plays/life-of-riley)***.
- The set is unusual in that the exact same set stands in for two totally different locations; the Rodale's house and a furniture showroom. The only play where Alan Ayckbourn had previously done this was ***[A Small Family Business](/plays/a-small-family-business)***. The difference with a play such as ***[How The Other Half Loves](/plays/how-the-other-half-loves)*** is whilst two different places are shown shorting the same place, *How The Other Half Loves* features design elements unique to both houses rather than a generic set being used to represent two completely different locations
- *If I Were You* was the first of several Ayckbourn plays to feature the noted actress Liza Goddard. She had previously worked with Alan Ayckbourn on an end-stage tour of ***[Season's Greetings](/plays/seasons-greetings)*** in 2004. Following *If I Were You*, Liza would appear in the world premiere productions of ***[Life & Beth](/plays/life-and-beth)*** and ***[Life Of Riley](/plays/life-of-riley)*** as well as several revivals directed by the playwright.
- The 2010 tour, produced by Bill Kenwright, marked the first time an Ayckbourn play which had not been produced in the West End had a major UK tour by a producer other than the Stephen Joseph Theatre.
