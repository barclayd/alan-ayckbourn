"It's all going wrong \[for the family\] in a sort of way, then something happens and they all take stock. It's a small play with enormous acting opportunities. It is more about human relations and how people relate to one another. It's a study of a family, mainly focusing on the difference between men and women. It's very interesting the way they approach different things and handle problems."  
*(Scarborough Evening News, 3 August 2006)*
