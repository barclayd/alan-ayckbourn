*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's If I Were You, such as the world premiere as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (2006)

**First performance:** 12 October 2006  
**World premiere:** 17 October 2006  
**Final performance:** 11 November 2006  
**Venue:** Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Roger Glossop  
**Lighting:** Mick Hughes  
**Costumes:** Jennie Boyer

**Stage Manager:** Fleur Linden Beeley  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Sue Volans  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Emma Hanson

**Jill Rodale** Liza Goddard
