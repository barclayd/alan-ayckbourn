*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's If I Were You, such as the world premiere as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*
