*This page contains details of significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's The Revengers' Comedies, such as the world and London premieres as well as Ayckbourn-directed revivals. It is not a complete list of productions given how frequently Alan Ayckbourn's plays are performed by professionals and amateur companies around the world.*

### World Premiere (1989)

**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn
