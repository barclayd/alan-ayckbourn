**Note:** Of the eight major variants of *Intimate Exchanges*, the BBC recorded four: *Events on a Hotel Terrace*; *A Pageant*; *A Game of Golf*; *Love in the Mist*.  
The additional dialogue provided by Alan Ayckbourn was a short addition to the first scene of each play. On stage, Celia’s key-decision as to whether to smoke or not is made in silence. For the radio version, Alan wrote a short piece of dialogue to make it clear what Celia was doing. The dialogue can be found in the **[Articles](/plays/intimate-exchanges/articles/radio-silence)** section.
