*Very occasionally, Alan Ayckbourn's plays are published with errors in the text. This page has corrections for the published edition of Absurd Person Singular.*

### Absurd Person Singular (Samuel French acting edition)

Page 44, Act II.

**As printed:**  
**Sidney:** (moving a stove up beside Ronald) Might I suggest that Marion sits down…

**Correct version (correction in bold italics):**  
**Sidney:** (moving a ***stool*** up beside Ronald) Might I suggest that Marion sits down…
