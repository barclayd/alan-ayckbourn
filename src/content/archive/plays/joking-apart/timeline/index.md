*The Timeline offers a chronological view of significant events in the history of the play Joking Apart; it only lists major productions and does not include the vast majority of the many professional and amateur productions of the play.*

**December 1977**  
The title of Alan Ayckbourn's latest play, *Joking Apart*, is announced. Alan Ayckbourn writes the play in mid-December over five days.

**12 January 1978**  
World premiere of *Joking Apart* at Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**February 1978**  
The Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round production of *Joking Apart* goes on a short UK Tour sponsored by Midland Bank (now HSBC). This is the first tour from the venue to receive sponsorship.

**23 May 1978**  
Revival of *Joking Apart* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**30 September 1978**  
*Joking Apart* closes at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round,

**7 March 1979**  
London premiere of *Joking Apart* at the Globe Theatre, London, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**7 July 1979**  
*Joking Apart* closes at the Globe Theatre, London.

**1979**  
Chatto & Windus publish *Joking Apart* in the mass market hardback edition *Joking Apart And Other Plays*.

**1979**  
Samuel French publishes the acting edition of *Joking Apart*.

**1980**  
*Joking Apart* released for professional repertory production; first production is probably at Bolton's Octagon Theatre.

**12 July 1981**  
*Joking Apart* broadcast on BBC Radio 4, directed by Caroline Smith.

**9 February 1990**  
A second adaptation of *Joking Apart* is broadcast on BBC Radio 3, directed by Michael Fox.

**11 July 2002**  
Revival of *Joking Apart* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**26 July 2018**  
Revival of *Joking Apart* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.
