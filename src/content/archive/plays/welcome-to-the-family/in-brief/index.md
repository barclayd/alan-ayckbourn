### Welcome to the Family

**Play Number:** 88  
**World Premiere:** 16 May 2023  
**Venue:** The Old Laundry Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere

**Premiere Staging:** In-the-round

**Published:** No  
**Other Media:** No

**Cast:** 3 male / 2 female  
**Run Time:** 1hr 40m

**Synopsis:** Josh is keen to introduce his fiancée Sara to his parents – Lord and Lady Meizner; but as they are deceased, this should have posed a problem…. Enter Josh’s wicked Uncle Lance who has gifted him a state-of-the-art “Capture” visit to the past enabling him to revisit his family home whenever he likes. Celebratory champagne flows but family secrets are also about to be spilled.

</aside>

- *Welcome to the Family* is Alan Ayckbourn's 88th play.The world premiere - directed by Alan Ayckbourn - was held at The Old Laundry Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere, on 16 May 2023.
- It was his second play to premiere at the Lake District venue following ***[All Lies](/plays/all-lies)*** in 2022.
- *Welcome to the Family* was written during lockdown in 2021 and was initially intended for production by the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough. However, unhappy with the original draft, Alan wrote another play for Scarborough, ***[Family Album](/plays/family-album)***, before extensively revising *Welcome to the Family* for 2023 at The Old Laundry Theatre.
- Welcome To The Family is set in a near future where memories can be recorded and interacted with as holographic projections. It is considered one of his speculative fiction works which began in 1987 with ***[Henceforward…](/plays/henceforward)***.
- The play was one of two Ayckbourn world premieres in 2023 alongside ***[Constant Companions](/plays/constant-companions)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough.
