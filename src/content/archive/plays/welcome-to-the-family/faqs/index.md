*Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's Welcome to the Family. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the* ***[Contact Us](http://www.alanayckbourn.net/contact-form/Contacts.php)*** *page.*

**When will *Welcome to the Family* be published?**  
There is no information regarding this yet, but judging by recent publications of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, it will probably be published during 2025.

**What was the music used between scenes for the world premiere production?**  
Alan Ayckbourn chose William Bolcom's *The Poltergeist* from his *Three Ghost Rags* for the world premiere production.

**Was *Welcome to the Family* filmed or streamed?**  
No, the world premiere production of *Welcome to the Family* was neither filmed nor streamed.

**Can I read *Welcome to the Family*?**  
An original manuscript for *Welcome to the Family* is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at the **[Borthwick Insitute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. This manuscript is accessible onsite for research purposes.
