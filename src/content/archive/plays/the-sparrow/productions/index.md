*This page contains the company details for significant productions of Alan Ayckbourn's The Sparrow.*

### World Premiere (1967)

**First performance:** 13 July 1967  
**World premiere:** 13 July 1967  
**Final performance:** 6 September 1967  
**Venue:** Theatre in the Round at Library Theatre, Scarborough  
**Staging:** Round

**Director:** Alan Ayckbourn  
**Design:** Catriona Trevelyan  
**Music:** Trevor Holroyd

**Stage Manager:** Ian Watson  
**Deputy Stage Manager:** Catriona Trevelyan  
**Assistant Stage Manager:** Stephen Armstrong

**Character** **Actor**  
**Evie** Pamela Craig  
**Ed** John Nettles  
**Tony** Robert Powell  
**Julia** Heather Stoney

### Rehearsed Reading (2014)

**Reading:** 20 September 2014  
**Venue:** Lawrence Batley Theatre, Huddersfield

**Director:** John Cotgrave  
**Design:** Catriona Trevelyan  
**Music:** Phil Butterfield

**Character** **Actor**  
**Evie** Laura Roberts  
**Ed** John Cotgrave  
**Tony** Craig Sequence  
**Julia** Hannah Head

**Note:** Aside from its world premiere production in Scarborough in 1967, this rehearsed reading marks the only other time *The Sparrow* has been publicly performed in its entirety. Although withdrawn from production, special permission was given to the theatre company Dick & Lottie to perform a rehearsed reading of the play as part of an Ayckbourn readathon celebrating the Ayckbourn-dedicated company’s 10th anniversary.*All research for this page by Simon Murgatroyd.*
