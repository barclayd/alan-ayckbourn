This page contains details of publications & articles relating to *The Sparrow*. The information is for research & the website cannot provide copies of the articles listed.

### Published Editions

*The Sparrow* has not been published.

### Manuscripts (research & reference)

An original manuscript for *The Sparrow* is held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/)** at the University of York. The manuscript is accessible onsite for research purposes.
