*Alan Ayckbourn's Archivist Simon Murgatroyd answers some of the most frequently asked questions about Alan Ayckbourn's Improbable Fiction. If you have a question about this or any other of Alan Ayckbourn's plays, you can contact the website via the* ***[Contact Us](/contact)*** *page.*

**Is it still possible to hire the walnut / egg for the final scene from the Stephen Joseph Theatre?**  
No, the oversized walnut / egg prop is no longer held by the Stephen Joseph Theatre after falling into disrepair.

**Is it possible to obtain the original music for the finale song *Light At The End of the Tunnel*?**  
Yes, the music for the song by the composer Denis King is available on hire from Samuel French Ltd. Enquiries can be made via the **[Concord Theatricals](http://www.concordtheatricals.co.uk/p/1436/improbable-fiction/)** website.
