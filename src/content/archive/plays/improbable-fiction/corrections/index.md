*Very occasionally, Alan Ayckbourn's plays are published with errors in the text. This page has corrections for the published edition of Improbable Fiction.*

### Improbable Fiction (Samuel French acting edition)

Page 30, Act I.

**As printed:**  
**Jess:** Doesn't stop him failing in love with practically everyone else.

**Correct version (correction in bold italics):**  
**Jess:** Doesn't stop him **falling** in love with practically everyone else.
