This page lists the significant archive holdings in relation to Alan Ayckbourn's *A Small Family Business*. The bulk of the material is held in the Ayckbourn Archive at **[The Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)** at The University of York. The Borthwick's relevant holdings can be found in the catalogue below with details of other archives listed beneath this.

If the catalogue content does not appear in the frame, the actual online catalogue can be visited **[here](http://borthcat.york.ac.uk/informationobject/browse/)**.

### Other Archives

**The National Theatre Archive:** The National Theatre Archive holds a substantive collection of material pertaining to its 1987 production of *A Small Family Business*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn and its 2014 revival, directed by Adam Penford. The record pages can be found **[here](http://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx)** (1987) and **[here](http://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx)** (2014).

**The British Library:** Top copy clean manuscript for world premiere at the National Theatre, London (1987), held in The Modern Playscripts Collection; correspondence in the 'Archive of Margaret Ramsay Ltd' (references: **[Add MS 88915/1/12/19](http://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-000374172/)**). *The Research page is presented in association with the* ***[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research/the-ayckbourn-archive)*** *at the University of York, where the Ayckbourn Archive is held. Further details about other collections and researching Alan Ayckbourn can be found in the* ***[Research](/research)*** *section of the website.*
