In addition to his own extensive body of work, Alan Ayckbourn has also contributed to other works - from providing short plays to song lyrics. This page contains links to the significant works which Alan Ayckbourn has contributed to in some form.

Click on the title of a work below to find out more details about Alan Ayckbourn's contribution and the piece itself. The majority of these works are held in the Ayckbourn Archive in the **[Borthwick Institute for Archives](/research)** at the University of York and thus available to read in situ. Further details about these plays can also be found in the website's book ***[Unseen Ayckbourn](/publications)***.

### Contributions

○ **[Cinderella's Star Night](/plays/childrens-plays-index/contributions/cinderellas-star-night) (1982):** Contributed a poem to this fund-raising pantomime.  
○ **[Millennium](/plays/childrens-plays-index/contributions/millennium) (2002):** Contributed several short pieces to the new defunct Scarborough tourist attraction.  
○ **[Mixed Blessings](/plays/childrens-plays-index/contributions/mixed-blessings) (1973):** Wrote the short play *Mother Figure* for the revue *Mixed Blessings*.  
○ **[Mixed Doubles](/plays/countdown) (1962):** Wrote the short play *Countdown* for the revue *Mixed Doubles*.  
○ **[Pen To Paper](/plays/childrens-plays-index/contributions/pen-to-paper) (1981):** Contributed two songs to the BBC Radio 4 programme.  
○ **[What The Devil!](/plays/dracula-sketch-from) (1975):** Contributed the short play *Dracula* and the song *The Ghost Of 'Enry Albert* to the revue *What The Devil!*  
○ **[Where Is Peter Rabbit?](/plays/childrens-plays-index/contributions/where-is-peter-rabbit) (2016):** Wrote the lyrics for the show *Where Is Peter Rabbit?*  
○ **[Where's My Seat?](/plays/childrens-plays-index/contributions/wheres-my-seat) (2011):** Contributed stage directions for the Bush Theatre experimental show.
