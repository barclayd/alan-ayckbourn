This page contains details of publications & articles relating to *Dad's Tale*. The information is for research & the website cannot provide copies of the articles listed.

### Published Editions

*Dad's Tale* has not been published.

### Manuscripts (research & reference)

Original manuscripts of *Dad's Tale* are held in the Ayckbourn Archive at the **[Borthwick Insitute for Archives](/)** at the University of York, The Ayckbourn Collection at Scarborough Museums and Galleries and in the Lord Chamberlain's Collection at the British Library. These manuscripts are accessible onsite for research purposes.
