Since Alan Ayckbourn began his playwriting career there have been more than 1,000 published interviews with him, many of which have been indexed here. While these interviews are not held on this website, many are held in the Ayckbourn Archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives at the University of York. Alternatively, many can be accessed through the archive sites for the relevant publications.
