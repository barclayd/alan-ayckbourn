This section of the website contains details of the actors who have worked with Alan Ayckbourn and appeared in significant productions of the playwright's work.

The index lists the name of the actor alongside the productions they have appeared in. The productions included are: world premieres; West End premieres and revivals; National Theatre productions; other productions directed by Alan Ayckbourn; Stephen Joseph Theatre revivals not directed by Alan Ayckbourn.
