**1 August:** West End premiere of ***[Table Manners](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)***) at The Globe in 1974; West End premiere of ***[A Chorus Of Disapproval](/plays/a-chorus-of-disapproval)*** at the National Theatre in 1985.

**3 August:** West End premiere of ***[Time Of My Life](/plays/time-of-my-life)*** at the Vaudeville Theatre in 1993.

**4 August:** World premiere of ***[This Is Where We Came In](/plays/this-is-where-we-came-in)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1990; revival of ***[Confusions](/plays/confusions)*** at the **[Library Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/theatre-in-the-round-at-the-library-theatre)**, Scarborough, in 1975.

**5 August:** West End premiere of ***[How The Other Half Loves](/plays/how-the-other-half-loves)*** at the Lyric Theatre in 1970; West End premiere of ***[Living Together](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)***) in 1974; world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[Second Helping](/plays/second-helping)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1980.

**6 August:** West End premiere of ***[Mr Whatnot](/plays/mr-whatnot)*** at the **[Victoria Theatre](/career/victoria-theatre)**, Stoke-on-Trent, in 1964 - the first Ayckbourn play to transfer to the West End; world premiere of ***[Arrivals & Departures](/plays/arrivals-and-departures)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2013.

**7 August:** West End premiere of ***[Communicating Doors](/plays/communicating-doors)*** at the Gielgud Theatre in 1995.

**8 August:** West End premiere of ***[Round & Round The Garden](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)***) at the Globe Theatre in 1974; world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Denis King's musical ***[Orvin - Champion Of Champions](/plays/orvin-champion-of-champions)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)** in 2003.

**9 August:** West End premiere of ***[House & Garden](/plays/house-and)*** at the National Theatre in 2000.

**10 August:** World premiere of ***[Man Of The Moment](/plays/man-of-the-moment)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1988; world premiere of ***[My Very Own Story](/plays/my-very-own-story)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1991.

**11 August:** World premiere of the stage adaptation of the novel ***[The Divide](/plays/the-divide)*** at the King's Theatre, Edinburgh, as part of the Edinburgh International Festival in 2017; revival of *How The Other Half Loves* at The Old Vic, London, in 2026.

**12 August:** First *Ayckbourn & The Round* event launched at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2001; world premiere of ***[Consuming Passions](/plays/consuming-passions)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2016.

**13 August:** World premiere of ***[Untitled Farce](/plays/untitled-farce)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2005.

**14 August:** West End premiere of ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges)*** at the Ambassador's Theatre in 1984.

**16 August:** West End premiere of ***[Time & Time Again](/plays/time-and-time-again)*** at the Comedy Theatre in 1972; **[directed](/career/directing-career)** first play by Harold Pinter - ***[The Caretaker](/career)*** - in 1962.

**17 August:** World premiere of ***[Private Fears In Public Places](/plays/private-fears-in-public-places)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2004.

**18 August:** World premiere of Michael Winner's film adaptation of ***[A Chorus Of Disapproval](/plays/a-chorus-of-disapproval/winner-michael)*** at the Paris Theatre, New York, in 1989.

**19 August:** Professional acting debut in Fritz Hochwaelder's ***[The Strong Are Lonely](/career)*** at the Edinburgh Festival in 1956 as part of Donald Wolfit's company; London revival of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[Me, Myself & I](/plays/me-myself-and-i)*** at the Orange Tree, Richmond, in 1983.

**20 August:** World premiere of ***[Family Circles](/plays/family-circles)*** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1970; New York premiere of ***[Communicating Doors](/plays/communicating-doors)*** at the Variety Arts Theater in 1998.

**21 August:** **[Directs](/career/directing-career)** last play at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round - Harold Pinter's ***[Betrayal](/career)*** - in 1996 before the company's move to its new home, the Stephen Joseph Theatre.

**23 August:** Broadcast premiere of ***[Package Deal](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/package-deal)*** by Derek Bickerton, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, on BBC Light in 1965.

**25 August:** **[Directs](/career/directing-career)** last play at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough - Stephen Mallatratt's ***[Mother Country](/career)*** - in 1976 before the company's move to its new home, the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round.

**26 August:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and John Pattison's musical ***[Dreams From A Summer House](/plays/dreams-from-a-summer-house)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1992; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of Alan Ayckbourn and Denis King's musical ***[Whenever](/plays/whenever)*** in 2006.

**28 August:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Confusions](/plays/confusions/confusions-adaptations-in-other-media)*** in 1988.

**30 August:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Table Manners](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)***) in 1990.

**31 August:** Broadcast premiere of ***[Mug's Game](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/mugs-game)*** by Robert Storey, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, on BBC Home Service in 1966.
