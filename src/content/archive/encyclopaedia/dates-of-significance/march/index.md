**2 March:** Television premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking/relatively-speaking-radio)*** in 1969; television premiere of the BBC's adaption of ***[The 7 Deadly Virtues](/plays/the-7-deadly-virtues)*** - retitled *Deadly Virtues* - in 1984.

**3 March:** West End premiere of ***[Things We Do For Love](/plays/things-we-do-for-love)*** at the Gielgud Theatre in 1998.

**4 March:** West End premiere of ***[Mr A's Amazing Maze Plays](/plays/mr-as-amazing-maze-plays)*** at the National Theatre in 1993.

**5 March:** London premiere of Tim Firth's ***[The Safari Party](/career)*** at Hampstead Theatre in 2003 - the final not self-penned play directed by Alan Ayckbourn; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular/absurd-person-singular-radio)*** in 1977.

**6 March:** *Absurd Person Singular* closes on Broadway in 1976 following a record-breaking run.

**7 March:** West End premiere of ***[Joking Apart](/plays/joking-apart)*** at the Globe Theatre in 1978; presented with the **[Lawrence Olivier Awards Special Award](/life/awards)** in 2009; Alan Ayckbourn's production of Herb Gardner's ***[Conversations With My Father](/career)*** transfers to the Old Vic, London, in 1995.

**10 March:** Christopher Marlowe's ***[The Jew of Malta](/career/actor/the-jew-of-malta)*** opens at the **[Victoria Theatre](/career/victoria-theatre)**, Stoke-on-Trent, in 1964 marking Alan Ayckbourn's final acting role with the company.

**13 March:** West End premiere of ***[Invisible Friends](/plays/invisible-friends)*** at the National Theatre in 1991.

**14 March:** London premiere of ***[Making Tracks](/plays/making-tracks)*** at Greenwich Theatre in 1983; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)*** in 2015.

**16 March:** West End premiere of ***[Bedroom Farce](/plays/bedroom-farce)*** at the National Theatre in 1977 - this marked Alan Ayckbourn's directing debut in London; ***[Wildest Dreams](/plays/wildest-dreams)*** transfers to the RSC's Swan Theatre in 1994.

**18 March:** First **[acting](/career)** role at the **[Leatherhead Theatre Club](/career/actor/connaught-theatre)**, playing Roger in Terence Rattigan's ***[She Stoops To Conquer](/career/actor/she-stoops-to-conquer)*** in 1957.

**22 March:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** at the Bristol Hippodrome in 1975.

**23 March:** Following HM Government advice, the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)** closes and cancels its entire summer schedule - including the world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn's ***[Truth Will Out](/plays/childrens-plays-index/unproduced-plays)*** - in response to the Covid-19 pandemic during 2020.

**24 March:** London revival of ***[Taking Steps](/plays/taking-steps)*** at the Orange Tree in 2010; West End revival of ***[Bedroom Farce](/plays/bedroom-farce)*** at the Duke Of York's Theatre in 2010.

**28 March:** The first grand marathon of ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges)*** is launched at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1983 - during two weeks, all 16 variations of the play are performed; broadcast premiere of *HARDtalk* featuring Alan Ayckbourn on BBC News in 2012.

**29 March:** West End premiere of ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking)*** at the Duke Of York's Theatre in 1967; West End premiere of ***[Season's Greetings](/plays/seasons-greetings)*** at the Apollo Theatre in 1982; Broadway premiere of *Bedroom Farce* at the Brooks Atkinson Theatre in 1979.

**31 March:** Steps down as **[Artistic Director](/career)** of the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2009 after 37 years in the role; UK premiere of Alain Resnais's film ***[Private Fears In Public Places](/plays/private-fears-in-public-places/coeurs)*** (*Coeurs*) in 2007; West End revival of ***[How The Other Half Loves](/plays/how-the-other-half-loves)*** in 2016.
