**1 October:** London revival of ***[Bedroom Farce](/plays/bedroom-farce)*** at the Rose Theatre in 2009.

**2 October:** World premiere of ***[Way Upstream](/plays/way-upstream)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1981.

**3 October:** ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** transfers to the Lyric Theatre, London, from the Duke of York's Theatre in 1996.

**4 October:** West End premiere of ***[Way Upstream](/plays/way-upstream)*** at the National Theatre in 1982 - postponed from 18 August due to the set flooding the theatre; world premiere of ***[Love Undertaken](/plays/love-undertaken)*** at St Mary's Parish House, Scarborough in 1961; world premiere of ***[An Evening With PALOS](/plays/an-evening-with-palos)*** at the Lyric Theatre, London, in 1983.

**5 October:** Alan Ayckbourn's most influential mentor, **[Stephen Joseph](/life)**, dies in Scarborough in 1967; world premiere of ***[It Could Be Any One Of Us](/plays/it-could-be-any-one-of-us)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1983; television premiere of ITV's adaptation of ***[Table Manners](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)***) in 1977.

**6 October:** West End revival of ***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** at the Old Vic in 2008.

**8 October:** Broadway premiere of ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular)*** at the Music Box Theatre in 1974; first Ayckbourn Weekend launched in Scarborough in 2010.

**9 October:** The **[Victoria Theatre](/career/victoria-theatre)** opens in Stoke-on-Trent in 1962 - this is the country's first purpose-built theatre-in-the-round venue and Alan Ayckbourn worked there between 1962 and 1964 as writer, director and actor.

**12 October:** Television premiere of ITV's adaptation of ***[Living Together](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)***) in 1977.

**13 October:** World premiere of ***[My Wonderful Day](/plays/my-wonderful-day)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)** in 2009; West End premiere of ***[Comic Potential](/plays/comic-potential)*** at the Lyric Theatre in 1999; London premiere of ***[Season's Greetings](/plays/seasons-greetings)*** at The Round House in 1980.

**16 October:** West End premiere of ***[The Revengers' Comedies](/plays/the-revengers-comedies)*** at the Strand Theatre in 1990.

**17 October:** World premiere of ***[If I Were You](/plays/if-i-were-you)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2006; North American premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** at the Norma Terris Theater, Chester, Connecticut in 1996; London revival of ***[Me, Myself & I](/plays/me-myself-and-i)*** at the Orange Tree, Richmond, in 2003.

**18 October:** Broadway revival of ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular)*** at the Biltmore Theatre in 2005.

**19 October:** Television premiere of ITV's adaptation of ***[Round & Round The Garden](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests/the-norman-conquests-audio-drama)***) in 1977; UK premiere of Michael Winner's film adaptation of ***[A Chorus Of Disapproval](/plays/a-chorus-of-disapproval/winner-michael)*** in 1989.

**21 October:** Broadcast premiere of *HARDtalk* featuring Alan Ayckbourn on BBC News in 2000.

**22 October:** Premiere of the film of Alan Ayckbourn's world premiere production of ***[Family Album](/plays/family-album)*** in 2022; Broadcast premiere of ***[The Luck Of Arthur Clough](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/the-luck-of-arthur-clough)*** by Peter Russell with Alan Rothwell, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, on BBC Home Service in 1966.

**24 October:** The Guardian reports that Alan Ayckbourn will no longer allow his plays to be produced in the West End following issues with the London production of the ***[Damsels In Distress](/plays/damsels-in-distress)*** trilogy in 2003.

**25 October:** Alan Ayckbourn's Official Website announces the discovery of the long-thought lost original abandoned draft of *Absurd Person Singular* by the playwright's Archivist, Simon Murgatroyd, within the Ayckbourn archive at the Borthwick Institute for Archives at the University of York.

**26 October:** The Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round opens in 1976 with the Scarborough premiere of ***[Mr Whatnot](/plays/mr-whatnot)***.

**28 October:** Broadway premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** at the Helen Hayes Theatre in 2001.

**29 October:** Alan Ayckbourn is **[awarded](/life/awards)** Yorkshire Man of the Year in 1994 - despite not being born in Yorkshire!

**30 October:** Broadcast premiere of *The South Bank Show - The Plain Guide To Playwriting* on ITV in 1994.

**31 October:** West End premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[Me, Myself & I](/plays/me-myself-and-i)*** at the National Theatre in 1985.
