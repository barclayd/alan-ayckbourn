**1 February:** Alan Ayckbourn receives his **[CBE](/life/awards)** in 1987; London revival of ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** at the Landor Theatre in 2011; the stage adaptation of the novel ***[The Divide](/plays/the-divide)*** opens at the Old Vic, London, in 2018.

**2 February:** World premiere of ***[Communicating Doors](/plays/communicating-doors)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1994; television premiere of the BBC's adaptation of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[Men On Women On Men](/plays/men-on-women-on-men)*** in 1979.

**3 February:** World premiere of ***[Virtual Reality](/plays/virtual-reality)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)** in 2000; the final performance is held at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1996 - the play is ***[Just Between Ourselves](/plays/just-between-ourselves)***.

**4 February:** The first major television documentary on Alan Ayckbourn is broadcast on BBC's *Arena* in 1974.

**5 February:** London premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's musical ***[Suburban Strains](/plays/suburban-strains)*** at The Round House in 1981.

**6 February:** West End revival of ***[Woman In Mind](/plays/woman-in-mind)*** at the Vaudeville Theatre in 2009; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of *Just Between Ourselves* in 1984.

**7 February:** West End premiere of the stage adaptation of the novel *The Divide* at the Old Vic, London, in 2017.

**9 February:** London premiere of ***[Snake In The Grass](/plays/snake-in-the-grass)*** at the Print Room in 2011; West End revival of ***[Absent Friends](/plays/absent-friends)*** at the Harold Pinter Theatre in 2012; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Joking Apart](/plays/joking-apart/joking-apart-radio-1981)*** in 1990.

**10 February:** Premiere of Alain Resnais's film adaptation of ***[Life Of Riley](/plays/life-of-riley)*** - *Aimer, Boire et Chanter* - at the Berlin Film Festival in 2014.

**11 February:** Knighted for 'services to the theatre in 1997.

**12 February:** Alan Ayckbourn & Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** is officially announced for the West End in 1975 with a £100,000 budget; premiere of Alan Ayckbourn's production of Arthur Miller's ***[A View From The Bridge](/plays/miller-arthur)*** at the **[National Theatre](/career/national-theatre)** in 1987; New York premiere of *Absent Friends* at the Manhattan Theatre Club in 1991.

**13 February:** The BBC culture show *Omnibus - Sex, Politics & Alan Ayckbourn* is dedicated to Alan Ayckbourn in 1990.

**14 February:** West End premiere of ***[Man Of The Moment](/plays/man-of-the-moment)*** in 1990 at the Globe Theatre; death of P.G. Wodehouse - just two days after the musical *Jeeves* was announced - in 1975.

**17 February:** New York premiere of ***[Woman In Mind](/plays/woman-in-mind)*** at the Manhattan Theatre Club in 1988.

**20 February:** Broadway premiere of ***[Taking Steps](/plays/taking-steps)*** at the Circle In The Square Theater in 1991.

**21 February:** Alan Ayckbourn suffers a stroke in 2006 in Scarborough - it is not reported until a week afterwards.

**22 February:** Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd contribute two songs to the day's episode of BBC Radio 4's ***[Pen To Paper](/plays/childrens-plays-index/contributions/pen-to-paper)*** in 1981.

**24 February:** London premiere of ***[Drowning On Dry Land](/plays/drowning-on-dry-land)*** at the Jermyn Street Theatre in 2011.

**26 February:** Alan Ayckbourn's mother Irene Maud Worley (better known as 'Lolly') dies in 1999; Alain Resnais's film adaptation of ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges)*** - ***[Smoking / No Smoking](/plays/intimate-exchanges/intimate-exchanges-film)*** - wins several Cesar Awards in 1994.

**28 February:** First public report that Alan Ayckbourn has suffered a stroke in 2006 (seven days after the actual stroke).
