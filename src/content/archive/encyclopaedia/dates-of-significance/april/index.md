**2 April:** Broadcast premiere of *With Great Pleasure* featuring Alan Ayckbourn on BBC Radio 4 in 2009.

**5 April:** West End premiere of ***[Ten Times Table](/plays/ten-times-table)*** at the Globe Theatre in 1978; broadcast premiere of *Profile: Alan Ayckbourn* on BBC Radio 4 in 2014.

**6 April:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Confusions](/plays/confusions/confusions-adaptations-in-other-media)*** in 1987; Allan Ramsay's portrait of Alan Ayckbourn is unveiled at the National Portrait Gallery in 1989; radio premiere of Don Haworth's ***[There's No Point In Arguing The Toss](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/theres-no-point-in-arguing-the-toss)*** in 1967, directed by Alan Ayckbourn and nominated for the Prix Italia award.

**7 April:** The Old Vic's acclaimed revival of ***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** transfers to the Circle In The Square, New York, in 2009.

**8 April:** West End revival of ***[A Small Family Business](/plays/a-small-family-business)*** at the National Theatre in 2014; West End revival of ***[Bedroom Farce](/plays/bedroom-farce)*** at the Aldwych Theatre in 2002.

**9 April:** Television premiere of the comedy series ***[Hark At Barker](/career/recordings/other-media/hark-at-barker)*** in 1969 on ITV - Alan Ayckbourn is one of the script-writers but due to being employed by the BBC, writes under the pseudonym of Peter Caulfield.

**11 April:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Man Of The Moment](/plays/man-of-the-moment/man-of-the-moment-radio-1992)*** in 2009 on BBC Radio 4; London premiere of ***[Neighbourhood Watch](/plays/neighbourhood-watch)*** at the Tricycle Theatre, London, in 2012; *A Chorus Of Approval* event held at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)** to celebrate Alan Ayckbourn's 60th birthday in 1999.

**12 April:** **[Alan Ayckbourn](/life)** was born in 1939 in Hampstead; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[A Small Family Business](/plays/a-small-family-business/a-small-family-business-radio)*** in 2009; Broadway premiere of *A Small Family Business* at the Music Box Theater in 1992.

**13 April:** Broadcast premiere of *Nightwaves - Landmarks: The Norman Conquests* on BBC Radio 3 in 2011.

**14 April:** Experiences theatre-in-the-round for the first time when he sees a performance of Sartre's *Huis Clois* at the Mahatma Gandhi Hall, in London, by Stephen Joseph's Studio Theatre company in 1957; directs ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking)*** for the first time with an amateur production at Leeds Civic Theatre in 1970.

**15 April:** Alan Ayckbourn directs one of his own plays for the first time with a production of ***[Standing Room Only](/plays/standing-room-only)*** at the Victoria Theatre, Stoke-on-Trent, in 1963.

**17 April:** The first mass-market edition of an Ayckbourn play is published in 1975 with a hardback edition of *The Norman Conquests*.

**18 April:** Broadcast premiere of ***[The Events At Black Tor](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/the-events-at-black-tor)*** by Roy Clarke, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, on BBC Radio 2 in 1968.

**20 April:** World premiere of ***[Haunting Julia](/plays/haunting-julia)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1994; world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and John Pattison's musical ***[A Word From Our Sponsor](/plays/a-word-from-our-sponsor)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1995; West End premiere of ***[Just Between Ourselves](/plays/just-between-ourselves)*** at the Queen's Theatre in 1977; appointed **[Director Emeritus](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/director-emeritus-sjt)** of the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2018.

**21 April:** World premiere of ***[Time Of My Life](/plays/time-of-my-life)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1992; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Confusions](/plays/confusions/confusions-adaptations-in-other-media)*** in 1985.

**22 April:** West End premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** in 1975 at Her Majesty's Theatre.

**23 April:** The second ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges)*** Grand Marathon begins in 2007 in which all 16 variations of the play are performed in two weeks for only the second time since the play's world premiere in 1982.

**24 April:** The Stephen Joseph Theatre opens in 1996 - this is a £5.2m conversion of Scarborough's former Odeon cinema and marks the first permanent home for the company founded by **[Stephen Joseph](/life)** in 1955 and which Alan Ayckbourn was **[Artistic Director](/career/artistic-director)** of at the time.

**27 April:** Revival of *Intimate Exchanges* at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2006.

**28 April:** New York premiere of *A Small Family Business* at the Manhattan Theatre Club in 1992.

**29 April:** World premiere of ***[Things We Do For Love](/plays/things-we-do-for-love)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 1997 - a rare example of an Ayckbourn play specifically written for the end-stage.

**30 April:** Alan Ayckbourn is listed in ***[Who's Who](/life/biographies/whos-who-biography)*** for the first time in 1975.
