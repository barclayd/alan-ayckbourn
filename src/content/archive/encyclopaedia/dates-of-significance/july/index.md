**1 July:** Broadcast premiere of *The Levin Interviews* featuring Alan Ayckbourn on BBC2 in 1984.

**2 July:** World premiere of ***[Round & Round The Garden](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)***) at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre in 1973; West End premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** at the Duke of York's Theatre in 1996.

**3 July:** World premiere of ***[FlatSpin](/plays/flatspin)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)** in 2001.

**4 July:** Rehearsed reading of ***[Standing Room Only](/plays/standing-room-only)*** by Dick & Lottie at The Square Chapel, Halifax in 2019 - its first public performance since 1966.

**5 July:** World premiere of ***[The Ten Magic Bridges](/plays/the-ten-magic-bridges)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2003.

**6 July:** New York premiere of ***[Season's Greetings](/plays/seasons-greetings)*** at the Joyce Theatre in 1985; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Joking Apart](/plays/joking-apart/joking-apart-radio-1981)*** in 1981.

**8 July:** World premiere of ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking)*** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1965; world premiere of ***[Time & Time Again](/plays/time-and-time-again)*** at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1971; world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn & Paul Todd's revue ***[First Course](/plays/first-course)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1980.

**9 July:** Received an Honorary Doctor of Letters (Hon DLitt) in recognition of his outstanding contribution to theatre in Scarborough and his services to creative industries across the world at a ceremony from the University Of Coventry Scarborough Campus in 2018.

**10 July:** Awarded an Honorary Doctorate by the University of York in 1992.

**11 July:** First **[acting](/career)** role at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, playing Eric Birling in J.B. Priestley's ***[An Inspector Calls](/career)*** in 1957.

**12 July:** World premiere of ***[Dear Uncle](/plays/dear-uncle-chekhov)*** - an adaptation of Chekhov's *Uncle Vanya* - at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2011; world premiere of ***[The Karaoke Theatre Company](/plays/the-karaoke-theatre-company)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2016; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges/intimate-exchanges-film)*** in 1987; radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Joking Apart](/plays/joking-apart/joking-apart-radio-1981)*** in 1981.

**13 July:** World premiere of ***[Standing Room Only](/plays/standing-room-only)*** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1961; world premiere of ***[The Sparrow](/plays/the-sparrow)*** at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1967.

**14 July: [Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/theatre-in-the-round-at-the-library-theatre)**, Scarborough, is opened by Stephen Joseph in 1955 - it is home to the UK's first professional theatre-in-the-round company; London premiere of ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular)*** at the Criterion Theatre in 1973.

**17 July:** World premiere of ***[Surprises](/plays/surprises)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2012.

**18 July:** London premiere of ***[The Boy Who Fell Into A Book](/plays/the-boy-who-fell-into-a-book)*** at Soho Theatre in 2012; BBC Radio 3 broadcasts *Essential Classics* featuring Alan Ayckbourn over five days in 2016.

**19 July:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[Backnumbers](/plays/backnumbers-with-paul-todd)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1983.

**21 July:** Live television broadcast of the first act of ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking/relatively-speaking-radio)*** from the West End on BBC1 in 1967.

**22 July:** World premiere of ***[Life & Beth](/plays/life-and-beth)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2008; West End premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** at the Duke Of York's Theatre in 1996; world premiere of ***[The Inside Outside Slide Show](/plays/the-inside-outside-slide-show)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1989; radio premiere of Don Haworth's ***[We All Come To It In The End](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions)*** in 1968, directed by Alan Ayckbourn and nominated for the Prix Italia award.

**23 July:** World premiere of ***[Sugar Daddies](/plays/sugar-daddies)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2003; television premiere of Yorkshire Television's adaptation of ***[Just Between Ourselves](/plays/just-between-ourselves/just-between-ourselves-audio-drama)*** in 1978; West End premiere of ***[Absent Friends](/plays/absent-friends)*** at the Garrick Theatre in 1975; world premiere of ***[The Girl Who Lost Her Voice](/plays/the-girl-who-lost-her-voice)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2005; world premiere of the musical adaptation ***[The Boy Who Fell Into A Book](/plays/the-boy-who-fell-into-a-book)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2014.

**25 July:** Alan Ayckbourn's 26th play is announced in the media as the thriller *Sight Unseen* - this will never be written and is replaced by ***[Season's Greetings](/plays/seasons-greetings)*** - in 1980.

**27 July:** Alan Ayckbourn directs a revival of ***[Man Of The Moment](/plays/man-of-the-moment)*** at the Royal & Derngate, Northampton, in 2009.

**28 July:** Broadcast premiere of *The South Bank Show - The Plain Guide To Playwriting pt 2* on ITV in 1996.

**29 July:** Broadcast premiere of ***[The Long And The Short And The Tall](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/the-long-and-the-short-and)*** by Willis Hall, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, on BBC Radio 4 in 1968.

**30 July:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn's first professionally produced play ***[The Square Cat](/plays/the-square-cat)*** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1959; world premiere of ***[Henceforward…](/plays/henceforward)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1987.

**31 July:** World premiere of ***[How The Other Half Loves](/plays/how-the-other-half-loves)*** at Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1969; revival of ***[Joking Apart](/plays/joking-apart)***, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2018; revival of *Season's Greetings*, directed by Alan Ayckbourn at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2019.
