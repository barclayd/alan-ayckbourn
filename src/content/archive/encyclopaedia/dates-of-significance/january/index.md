**1 January:** Alan Ayckbourn was made a **[CBE](/life/awards)** in 1987 and **[knighted](/life/awards)** for 'services to theatre' in 1997; television premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular/absurd-person-singular-radio)*** in 1985; television premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Way Upstream](/plays/way-upstream/way-upstream-radio)*** in 1988.

**6 January:** Faber & Faber publishes its first Ayckbourn play with ***[A Chorus Of Disapproval](/plays/a-chorus-of-disapproval)*** in 1986; revival of Woman in Mind at The Duke of York's Theatre, London, in 2026.

**9 January:** A touring production of **[Harold Pinter](/life/influences/harold-pinter)**'s ***[The Birthday Party](/career)*** opens in 1959 at Birmingham Theatre Centre, directed by Harold Pinter with Alan Ayckbourn playing Stanley.

**10 January:** World premiere of ***[Sisterly Feelings](/plays/sisterly-feelings)*** at the**[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1979.

**11 January:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Woman In Mind](/plays/woman-in-mind/woman-in-mind-radio-2000)*** in 2014.

**12 January:** World premiere of ***[Joking Apart](/plays/joking-apart)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1978; world premiere of the revues ***[Incidental Music](/plays/incidental-music)*** (1983) and ***[The 7 Deadly Virtues](/plays/the-7-deadly-virtues)*** (1984) at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round.

**15 January:** The Evening Standard announces Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber are collaborating on a musical based on P.G. Wodehouse's ***[Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** novels in 1974.

**18 January:** World premiere of ***[Ten Times Table](/plays/ten-times-table)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1977; world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's musical ***[Suburban Strains](/plays/suburban-strains)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1980.

**21 January:** Television premiere of ***[A Cut In The Rates](/plays/a-cut-in-the-rates)*** on BBC2 written for the *English Files* series in 1984.

**22 January:** Alan Ayckbourn receives his first **[award](/life/awards)** with ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular)*** winning the Evening Standard Best Comedy Award in 1974; the softback edition of Alan Ayckbourn's *The Crafty Art Of Playmaking* is published by Faber & Faber in 2004.

**25 January:** Premiere of Alan Ayckbourn's production of John Ford's ***['Tis Pity She's A Whore](/career)*** at the **[National Theatre](/career/national-theatre)** in 1988.

**26 January:** Inducted into the Hall Of Fame For Achievements in American Theatre in 2009.

**27 January:** Premiere of what is believed to be Alan Ayckbourn's first production as a **[BBC Radio Drama Producer](/career/bbc-career)** with John Pennington's *[I](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/i-want-to-be-an-engine-driver)****[Want To Be An Engine Driver](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/i-want-to-be-an-engine-driver)*** on the BBC World Service in 1965.

**28 January:** World premiere of ***[Just Between Ourselves](/plays/just-between-ourselves)*** at the Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, Scarborough, in 1976; London revival of ***[Season's Greetings](/plays/seasons-greetings)*** at the Greenwich Theatre in 1982; world premiere of Alan Ayckbourn's adaptation of Ostrovsky's ***[The Forest](/plays/the-forest-ostrovsky)*** at the National Theatre in 1999; 45th Street in New York city is renamed Ayckbourn Alley for the day in 1976 to mark four Ayckbourn plays running simultaneously on Broadway.

**29 January:** West End revival of ***[Woman In Mind](/plays/woman-in-mind)*** at the Vaudeville Theatre in 2009; receives Lloyd's Private Banking Playwright Of The Year **[Award](/life/awards)** in 1997.

**30 January:** Broadcast premiere of ***[All Honour Mr Todd](/career/bbc-career/bbc-radio-productions/all-honour-mr-todd)*** by Peter Person, directed by Alan Ayckbourn, on BBC Radio 3 in 1970.

**31 January:** Takes the Chair of Cameron Macintosh Visiting Professor of Contemporary Theatre at Oxford University in 1992.
