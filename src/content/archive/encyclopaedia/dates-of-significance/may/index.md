**1 May:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre)** in 1996; New York premiere of ***[A Brief History Of Women](/plays/a-brief-history-of-women)*** at the 59E59 Theaters.

**2 May:** World premiere of ***[A Chorus Of Disapproval](/plays/a-chorus-of-disapproval)*** at the **[Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round](/career/ayckbourn-and-the-sjt/sjt-venues/stephen-joseph-theatre-in-the-round)** in 1984; Alan Ayckbourn receives a telegram from Noël Coward congratulating him on the success of ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking)*** in 1967; Alan Ayckbourn directs his first play by the farceur Ben Travers - ***[Rookery Nook](/career)*** - at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1978.

**3 May:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular/absurd-person-singular-radio)*** in 1977.

**4 May:** World premiere of ***[Drowning On Dry Land](/plays/drowning-on-dry-land)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2004.

**5 May:** London premiere of ***[Private Fears In Private Places](/plays/private-fears-in-public-places)*** at the Orange Tree, Richmond, in 2005.

**6 May:** World premiere of ***[Wildest Dreams](/plays/wildest-dreams)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1991; world premiere of ***[All Lies](/plays/all-lies)*** at The Old Laundry Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere, in 2022.

**8 May:** ***[By Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** live-streamed on Youtube as part of *The Shows Must Go On!* initiative during the Covid-19 theatre lockdown in 2020.

**9 May:** London premiere of ***[Table Manners](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)***) at the Greenwich Theatre in 1974; marries his first wife, Christine Roland, in 1959.

**10 May:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Man Of The Moment](/plays/man-of-the-moment/man-of-the-moment-radio-1992)*** in 1992.

**14 May:** Radio premiere of the BBC's adaptation of ***[Woman In Mind](/plays/woman-in-mind/woman-in-mind-radio-2000)*** in 2000.

**15 May:** West End revival of ***[Absurd Person Singular](/plays/absurd-person-singular)*** at the Whitehall Theatre in 1990.

**16 May:** World premiere of ***[Welcome to the Family](/plays/welcome-to-the-family)*** at The Old Laundry Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere; West End revival of ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking)*** at Wyndham's Theatre in 2013.

**18 May:** Television premiere of ITV's adaptation of ***[Time & Time Again](/plays/time-and-time-again/time-and-time-again-television)*** in 1976.

**19 May:** West End premiere of ***[Confusions](/plays/confusions)*** at the Apollo Theatre in 1976.

**20 May:** Television premiere of the BBC's ***[Service Not Included](/plays/service-not-included)*** - the first produced screenplay written by Alan Ayckbourn in 1974; Alan Ayckbourn quits the Arts Council's Drama Advisory Panel in protest at Government reforms in 1998; London premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[The Westwoods](/plays/the-westwoods-his-side)*** at the Etcetera Theatre, Camden, in 1987.

**21 May:** World premiere of ***[A Small Family Business](/plays/a-small-family-business)*** at the **[National Theatre](/career/national-theatre)** in 1987; world premiere of ***[Body Language](/plays/body-language)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1990; London premiere of ***[Living Together](/plays/the-norman-conquests)*** (***[The Norman Conquests](/plays/the-norman-conquests)***) at the Greenwich Theatre in 1974; New York premiere of ***[House & Garden](/plays/house-and)*** at the Manhattan Theatre Club in 2002; revival of *Things We Do For Love* at The Old Laundry Theatre, Bowness-on-Windermere, directed by Alan Ayckbourn.

**22 May:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[Mere Soup Songs](/plays/mere-soup-songs)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1986.

**23 May:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue ***[Boy Meets Girl](/plays/boy-meets-girl)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1985; Queen Elizabeth II sees her first Ayckbourn play - ***[Relatively Speaking](/plays/relatively-speaking)*** - in 1967; Alan Ayckbourn announces his retirement as **[Artistic Director](/career)** to the Stephen Joseph Theatre company in 2007.

**24 May:** World premiere of ***[GamePlan](/plays/gameplan)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2001; Alan Ayckbourn and Andrew Lloyd Webber's musical ***[Jeeves](/plays/jeeves)*** closes in London after less than a month in 1975.

**25 May:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's ***[Girl Meets Boy](/plays/boy-meets-girl)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1985; New York premiere of ***[Communicating Doors](/plays/communicating-doors)*** at the Variety Arts Theatre in 1998; world premiere of ***[Anno Domino](/plays/anno-domino)*** - the first Ayckbourn play to be premiered as a digital stream (due to the Covid-19 pandemic) in 2020.

**27 May:** London premiere of ***[Haunting Julia](/plays/haunting-julia)*** at the Riverside Studios in 2011.

**28 May:** New York premiere of ***[Joking Apart](/plays/joking-apart)*** at the T. Schrieber Studio in 2010.

**29 May:** World premiere of Alan Ayckbourn and Paul Todd's revue of ***[The Westwoods](/plays/the-westwoods-his-side)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in the Round in 1984; New York premiere of ***[Arrivals & Departures](/plays/arrivals-and-departures)*** at the 59E59 Theaters in 2014.

**30 May:** World premiere of ***[Woman In Mind](/plays/woman-in-mind)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre In The Round in 1985; in a possibly unique achievement for a playwright, Alan Ayckbourn has a production at the National Theatre (***[A Chorus Of Disapproval](/plays/a-chorus-of-disapproval)***), in the West End (*Woman In Mind*) and on the London fringe (***[The Westwoods](/plays/the-westwoods-his-side)***) all running on the same day in 1986.

**31 May:** World premiere of ***[Improbable Fiction](/plays/improbable-fiction)*** at the Stephen Joseph Theatre in 2005; New York premiere of ***[Intimate Exchanges](/plays/intimate-exchanges)*** at the 59E59 Theaters in 2007.
