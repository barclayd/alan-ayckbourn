*Unseen Ayckbourn* is the most recent edition of the book by Alan Ayckbourn's archivist Simon Murgatroyd M.A., looking at the playwright's unpublished, withdrawn and unwritten plays.

*Unseen Ayckbourn* examines Alan Ayckbourn's phenomenal writing career by exploring the plays which have rarely been seen, withdrawn, lost or - occasionally - altered.

It draws from unused or abandoned concepts and ideas as well as looking at variations of existing plays, alternative titles as well as other ephemera from the playwright's six decades of writing. When first published, it was named as one of The Stage newspaper's best theatre books of 2013.

Updated in 2023, the book includes extracts from many of the works included as well as extensive quotes by the playwright himself. It is of particular interest in charting Alan Ayckbourn's work during the COVID pandemic lockdowns, when the playwright wrote eight full-length plays, the majority of which have not been - nor are likely to be - staged.

The book also contains a biography of the playwright as well as a complete list of Alan Ayckbourn's plays and writing as of 2023.
