Alan Ayckbourn's Official Website has a number of official social media channels on **[WordPress](http://archivingayckbourn.home.blog/)** (@archivingayckbourn), **[Instagram](http://www.instagram.com/ayckbourn_playwright/)** (@Ayckbourn), on **[YouTube](http://www.youtube.com/channel/UCw_wK39Pa4Hnfziu9On5nNg/)** (@AlanAyckbournPlaywright) and **[Bluesky](http://bsky.app/profile/alan-ayckbourn.bsky.social)**.

The playwright himself does not participate in any social media and all his social media accounts are run by Simon Murgatroyd for this website. Any social media account pertaining to be Sir Alan's personal account written by himself is fake and should be ignored and, ideally, reported.

There is no official Alan Ayckbourn presence on Facebook after it suspended the account for no reason nor X / Twitter as a result of the level of offensive and extreme speech found relating even to Alan Ayckbourn. Any active account pertaining to be an Alan Ayckbourn account on X / Twitter / X is fake and should be ignored.
