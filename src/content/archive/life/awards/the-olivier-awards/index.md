This page breaks down all the awards received for both Alan Ayckbourn and his productions at The Olivier Awards.

### The Olivier Awards: Awards & Nominations

**1976 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Confusions*); Director (Alan Strachan for *Confusions*); Designer (Alan Tagg for *Confusions*)

**1977 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Bedroom Farce*); Best Actor (Colin Blakely in *Just Between Ourselves*); Actress (Rosemary Leach in *Just Between Ourselves*); Comedy Performance (Joan Hickson in *Bedroom Farce*); Supporting Actress (Constance Chapman in *Just Between Ourselves*)

**1978 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Ten Times Table*)

**1980 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Sisterly Feelings*)

**1982 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Season's Greetings*)

**1984 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Intimate Exchanges*); Comedy Performance (Lavinia Bertram for I*ntimate Exchanges*)

**1985 Winners:** Best Comedy (*A Chorus of Disapproval*); Comedy Performance (Michael Gambon for *A Chorus of Disapproval*); Supporting Role (Imelda Staunton for *A Chorus of Disapproval*)

**1986 Nominations:** Actress (Julia McKenzie for *Woman in Mind*)

**1987 Winners:** Actor (Michael Gambon in *A View From The Bridge* directed by Alan Ayckbourn); Most Promising Newcomer (Suzan Sylvester in *A View From The Bridge* directed by Alan Ayckbourn)

**1987 Nominations:** Director (Alan Ayckbourn for *A View From The Bridge*); Supporting Role (Diane Bull in *Tons of Money* directed by Alan Ayckbourn)

**1988 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Henceforward…*); Supporting Role (Serena Evans for *Henceforward…*)

**1989 Winners:** Comedy Performance (Michael Gambon in *Man of the Moment*)

**1989 / 1990 Nominations:** Best Play (*Man of the Moment*)

**1994 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Time Of My Life*)

**1996 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Communicating Doors*)

**1997 Nominations:** Outstanding Musical Production (*By Jeeves*); Actor in a Musical (Steven Pacey for *By Jeeves*); Costume Design (Louise Belson for *By Jeeves*)

**1999 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Things We Do For Love*)

**2000 Winners:** Actress (Janie Dee for *Comic Potential*)

**2000 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*Comic Potential*)

**2001 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*House & Garden*)

**2003 Nominations:** Best Comedy (*RolePlay*)

**2009 Winners:** Special Society Award (*Alan Ayckbourn*)

**2009 Nominations:** Best Revival (*The Norman Conquests* at The Old Vic); Supporting Role (Paul Ritter in *The Norman Conquests* at The Old Vic); Company (*The Norman Conquests* at The Old Vic); Costume Design (Rob Howell for *The Norman Conquests* at The Old Vic)
