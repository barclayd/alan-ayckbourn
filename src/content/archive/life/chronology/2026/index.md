### Notable Events

During 2026, Alan Ayckbourn…

○ will premiere his 92nd play, ***[The Trial of Romeo Oscar](/plays/the-trial-of-romeo-oscar)***, at the Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough.

○ saw a major revival of ***[How The Other Half Loves](/plays/how-the-other-half-loves)*** at The Old Vic, London, directed by Philip Breen as part of Artistic Director Matthew Warchus' farewell season.

○ had a major West End revival with ***[Woman in Mind](/plays/woman-in-mind)*** at the Duke of York's Theatre, starring Sheridan Smith, before touring to Liverpool and Glasgow.

○ saw the republication of his play *Woman in Mind* in Faber's new re-designed editions of significant plays.
