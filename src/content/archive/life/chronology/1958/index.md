### Notable Events

During 1958, Alan Ayckbourn…

○ rejoined **[Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre](/career/ayckbourn-and-sjt/sjt-venues/theatre-in-the-round-at-the-library-theatre)**, Scarborough, for the summer and winter seasons after leaving **[Oxford Playhouse](/career/actor/connaught-theatre)**.

○ complained to **[Stephen Joseph](/life)**, Artistic Director of Theatre in the Round at the Library Theatre, about the quality of his role in David Campton's ***[Ring Of Roses](/career)***; Stephen challenged Alan to write his own play with a better role for himself.

○ wrote a number of unproduced plays (between 1957 and 1959) which included: ***[The Season](/plays)***; ***[Relative Values](/plays)***; ***[Mind Over Murder](/plays)***; ***[The Party Game](/plays)***. Most of these plays are now lost and none have been performed.

○ became engaged to the actress Christine Roland - another member of the Studio Theatre Ltd company - whilst on tour in September / October to Leicester (immediately prior to this he had been engaged to one of the company's stage management team, Ann Summers, for several months).
